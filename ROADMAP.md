## Ideas we want to test

*Not designed yet. Each goes through a written design, a synthetic test and, where it reads data, a null test before anything it shows is claimed.*

- **The information sphere: the geometry of healthy order.** Place every site by how it behaves, not by where it sits on the DNA. Under the square-root map each methylation state lies on a sphere and the Fisher-Rao angle is its information distance; sites held in order together cluster, and disorder shows as that shape blurring. Read it with the microwave-sky tools after a written design, a synthetic test and a null test on held-out healthy cells. This is an application question - whether our meter predicts which hatchery fish come back - and is separate from the temperature test of the law, where hibernation is the stronger design (DEV-LEVERS-01 test 2b). Built on the PATTERN, not on DNA addresses: the strand is hardware, the map is of the information (CANON/foundation.json, what_is_read; DEV_SPHERE_01). Needs the laptop; design record written 2026-10-13 (DEV-SPHERE-01): our own framing, the Fisher-Rao mathematics, the nucleus version, and the three steps before anything is claimed.
- **The whole-genome sky map.** Draw every site of the genome on the sphere and read it with the microwave-sky tools. The honest catch: the genome's own shape is a line, so patch sizes on a sphere partly measure our own wrapping. Two versions earn their keep. First, the information sphere above. Second, the nucleus itself: DNA is folded inside a ball, the stretches pressed against the nuclear wall are mostly switched off and are exactly where cancers lose large blocks of methylation, and biologists already measure which stretches touch the wall. A sphere built from where DNA actually sits would have real curvature, and then the sky tools would be measuring biology rather than our filing order. Built on the PATTERN, not on DNA addresses: the strand is hardware, the map is of the information (CANON/foundation.json, what_is_read; DEV_SPHERE_01). Needs the laptop; design record written 2026-10-13 (DEV-SPHERE-01): our own framing, the Fisher-Rao mathematics, the nucleus version, and the three steps before anything is claimed.
- **Where on the scale does cancer begin? With enough sealed readings across pre-cancer, early and late disease, ask whether there is a line cells cross rather than a gradual slide..**  Needs the laptop; idea; needs the pre-cancer results first.
- **What reads below the healthy range? If a sample reads better than healthy, either the instrument or the sample is wrong, and finding out which is a real test of both..**  Needs the laptop; idea, not designed.
- **Memory of damage.** If the writing enzyme is switched off and then restored, does the pattern come back, and how exactly? That is the cleanest test of whether a cell's order is recoverable. Needs the laptop; idea; a public experiment is being looked for.
- **Temperature.** The physics floor depends on body temperature, so a hibernating animal should read differently, by an amount we can predict in advance. Needs the laptop; designed, not run.
