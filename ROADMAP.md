## Ideas we want to test

*Not designed yet. Each goes through a written design, a synthetic test and, where it reads data, a null test before anything it shows is claimed.*

- **The information sphere: the geometry of healthy order.** Place every site by how it behaves, not by where it sits on the DNA. Under the square-root map each methylation state lies on a sphere and the Fisher-Rao angle is its information distance; sites held in order together cluster, and disorder shows as that shape blurring. Read it with the microwave-sky tools after a written design, a synthetic test and a null test on held-out healthy cells. Needs the laptop; idea, not designed.
