\chapter{Law and Order: one accounting at five places}\label{ch:synthesis}

\section*{The two halves of the title}
The law is the first half. Every irreversible transition from quantum superposition to a classical record pays at least $\kB T\ln2$ per
bit at the nearest surface on which the record is written, at that surface's temperature (Chapter~\ref{ch:iams_law}). Decoherence makes the
record~\cite{Zurek2003}; Landauer and Bennett price it~\cite{Landauer1961,Bennett1982}; B\'erut and colleagues measured the price~\cite{Berut2012};
Bekenstein, Hawking and Jacobson give the surface its capacity, its temperature and its role in gravity~\cite{Bekenstein1973,Hawking1975,Jacobson1995}.
The single identification the book adds is that the records belong in the entropy of the surface on which they are written. \conjecture

Order is the second half. A record, once written, does not stay written for free. Thermal noise at the surface's temperature randomises it,
and a system that is to remain what it is must spend energy to hold its records against that noise. A qubit holds its state against the
quasiparticles and two-level defects of its own junction; a transistor holds a logic level against $\kB T$ at the gate; a cell holds its
methylation pattern against copying errors at body temperature; a bound system holds its configuration in the balance the virial theorem
states. Each of these is a fidelity held against thermal noise, and each can be read against its floor. \derived{} (for the floor at each
surface) The author's further reading, that the written surface is what the next moment is built from, so that without it nothing
would follow, is an interpretation: it is how the book's results are understood, not one of them. \interp

Three things are common to every row and are established. The cost per bit is $\kB T\ln2$ at the surface's own temperature. Each surface has a
capacity, and the far end of its gauge is fixed exactly by that capacity: the Bekenstein bound for a horizon, one bit per site for a cell. And

\section{The one half that recurs}
The virial theorem gives $2K+U=0$ for any system bound by a $1/r$ potential: half the binding energy is kinetic, half is released. It holds
for every atom and molecule, for stars and galaxies and for clusters of galaxies (Table~\ref{tab:virial_domains}). \observed{} At a horizon the
same half appears as the Smarr relation: the bits on a Schwarzschild horizon, priced at its own temperature, total $\tfrac12Mc^2$. \derived{} In
cosmology it fixes the size of the informational term, $\beta_m=\Omega_m/2$, and with it $\mu_0=-0.136$ with no free parameter
(Chapter~\ref{ch:virial}). \derived{} In a transistor the energy drawn from the supply to charge a gate splits in half between the capacitor and the
resistance, whatever the resistance. \derived{} That the CMOS half and the virial half are one quantity is a conjecture. \conjecture

\section{What has been measured, place by place}
\paragraph{Cosmology.} With the coupling fixed by the law, the Planck 2018 fit is as good as $\Lambda$CDM's ($\Delta\chi^2=+0.54$) and only
$\sigma_8$ moves ($0.8087\to0.7998$). The predicted growth deficit, 4.25\,\% in $f\sigma_8$ today and 0.41\,\% at $z=1$, with lensing unchanged, is
below what present surveys resolve; Euclid's first complete release in mid-2027 tests $\mu_0$ at about $1.7\sigma$ and its final survey at about
$3.4\sigma$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:predictions}). \measured, \prediction

\paragraph{Black holes.} The bits of a horizon at its own temperature total $\tfrac12Mc^2$ for every mass, and a black hole saturates the
Bekenstein bound. Both are standard results; the book reads them as the law at its limit. \derived

\paragraph{Quantum processors and chips.} Gate errors and switching energies sit above their floors by measured factors: Quantinuum Helios at
about $8\times$ its calibrated floor (a reading of 0.13 for the floor on its gauge), the Ryzen 9 9950X at 576 times $\kB T_j\ln2$. The floors and
temperature exponents of processors are calibrated, so the dated device predictions test those calibrations
(Chapters~\ref{ch:ascoreqc},~\ref{ch:thermaln},~\ref{ch:cmos}). \calibrated, \calc

\paragraph{Cells.} The instrument reads neutrophils on EPIC arrays against their own healthy reference, measured on six purified arrays; held out,
those arrays read with an SD of 0.020 around 1 (Chapter~\ref{ch:meta}). \calibrated{} On constructed whole-blood mixtures with at least 50\,\%
neutrophils, the healthy mixtures read 0.982--1.016 and a 2\,\% loss of the neutrophil pattern read 1.049--1.079. \measured{} Three development
measurements read a known change in the right direction:
\begin{itemize}
\item blocking the copying enzyme DNMT1 in three leukaemia cell lines moved Met-A from 0.97--1.05 (vehicle) to 1.16--1.85, on the methylated
      channel only, with no change under an inactive look-alike (\texttt{PROC\_DNMT\_01\_PARTA\_OUTCOME.md});
\item on single molecules from the same kind of experiment, IAM-A read 1.65--1.97 in all eight treated samples, with conversion failure unchanged
      (\texttt{PROC\_DNMT\_01\_PARTB\_OUTCOME.md});
\item in six early-onset colorectal tumours, the copy error was above that of the same patient's adjacent normal tissue in all six, median ratio 1.148
      (\texttt{PROC\_TUMOUR\_01\_OUTCOME.md}).
\end{itemize}
\measured{} These show that the readings respond to a known change in maintenance fidelity. They are not commissioning results and not disease
readings; where breach and the cancer region lie on this gauge is still to be measured (Chapter~\ref{ch:gauge}).

\section{What the book has not shown}
The floors are not yet derived. The cell's floor height comes from a measured holding energy, $E_{\rm hold}=3.41\,\kB T$; the processors'
floors and exponents are calibrated; the cosmic coupling is derived from the partition, but the entropy identification it rests on is a
conjecture tested by its consequences. The electron-mass formula carries a factor found numerically; the $3/16$ of the baryon relation and the
$2/\pi$ of the cosmological-constant estimate are not derived. A derivation of one floor from the law alone, before the data are consulted, would
turn the ladder of places from a shared accounting into a numerical unification. That is the first item of the next chapter.
