\chapter{Law and Order: one accounting at five places}\label{ch:synthesis}

\section*{The two halves of the title}
The law is the first half. Every irreversible transition from quantum superposition to a classical record pays at least $\kB T\ln2$ per
bit at the nearest surface on which the record is written, at that surface's temperature (Chapter~\ref{ch:iams_law}). Decoherence makes the
record~\cite{Zurek2003}; Landauer and Bennett price it~\cite{Landauer1961,Bennett1982}; B\'erut and colleagues measured the price~\cite{Berut2012};
Bekenstein, Hawking and Jacobson give the surface its capacity, its temperature and its role in gravity~\cite{Bekenstein1973,Hawking1975,Jacobson1995}.
The single identification the book adds is that the records belong in the entropy of the surface on which they are written. \conjecture

Order is the second half. A record, once written, does not stay written for free. Thermal noise at the surface's temperature randomises it,
and a system that is to remain what it is must spend energy to hold its records against that noise. A qubit holds its state against the
quasiparticles and two-level defects of its own junction; a transistor holds a logic level against $\kB T$ at the gate; a cell holds its
methylation pattern against copying errors at body temperature; a bound system holds its configuration in the balance the virial theorem
states. Each of these is a fidelity held against thermal noise, and each can be read against its floor. \derived{} (that each surface has a
floor at $\kB T\ln2$ per bit; for chips and qubits the floor follows from the temperature the record sees, for the cell its height is
measured, as the last section of this chapter states) Our further reading,
that the written surface is what the next moment is built from, so that without it nothing would follow, is an interpretation: it is how the
book's results are understood, not one of them. \interp

The virial theorem gives $2K+U=0$ for any system bound by a $1/r$ potential: half the binding energy is kinetic, half is released. It holds
for every atom and molecule, for stars and galaxies and for clusters of galaxies (Table~\ref{tab:virial_domains}). \observed{} At a horizon the
same half appears as the Smarr relation: the bits on a Schwarzschild horizon, priced at its own temperature, total $\tfrac12Mc^2$. \derived{} In
cosmology it fixes the size of the informational term, $\beta_m=\Omega_m/2$, and with it $\mu_0=-0.136$ with no free parameter
(Chapter~\ref{ch:virial}). \derived{} In a transistor the energy drawn from the supply to charge a gate splits in half between the capacitor and the
resistance, whatever the resistance. \derived{} That the CMOS half and the virial half are one quantity is a conjecture. \conjecture

\paragraph{Cosmology.} With the coupling fixed by the law and no free parameter, IAM reproduces Planck 2018 as well as $\Lambda$CDM ($\Delta\chi^2=+0.54$) and only
$\sigma_8$ moves, as the late-time term predicts ($0.8087\to0.7998$). The predicted growth deficit, 4.25\,\% in $f\sigma_8$ today and 0.41\,\% at $z=1$, with lensing unchanged, is
below what present surveys resolve. Euclid's published forecasts bracket the test of the IAM $\mu(z)$ from about $0.3\sigma$ (conservative scale cuts) to about $7\sigma$ (smallest
scales modelled), and a forecast made with the IAM $\mu(z)$ itself is the next step (Chapter~\ref{ch:latetime}). \fitted, \calc, \prediction

\paragraph{Black holes.} The bits of a horizon at its own temperature total $\tfrac12Mc^2$ for every mass, and a black hole saturates the
Bekenstein bound. Both are standard results~\cite{Bekenstein1973,Hawking1975}. \derived{} The book reads them as the law at its limit. \interp

\paragraph{Quantum processors and chips.} Gate errors and switching energies sit above floors that temperature alone sets. A 5~GHz transmon
at its own 35~mK, with $T_1=68~\mu$s and a 40~ns gate, has a per-gate thermal floor of $6.2\times10^{-7}$, three orders of magnitude below a
two-qubit error near $10^{-3}$; the Ryzen 9 9950X switches at 576--593 times $\kB T_j\ln2$
(Chapters~\ref{ch:ascoreqc},~\ref{ch:qplatforms},~\ref{ch:cmos}). \derived, \calc{} That room, read channel by channel on one device, is what the
device gauges offer a laboratory and a fab.

\paragraph{Cells.} The instrument reads neutrophils on EPIC arrays against their own healthy reference, measured on six purified arrays; held out,
those arrays read with an SD of 0.020 around 1 (Chapter~\ref{ch:meta}). \calibrated{} On six DNA mixtures of known composition with 63--75\,\%
neutrophils, the healthy mixtures read 0.982--1.016 and a simulated 2\,\% loss of the neutrophil pattern read 1.049--1.079. \measured{} Three development
measurements read a known change in the right direction:
\begin{itemize}
\item blocking the copying enzyme DNMT1 in three leukaemia cell lines moved Met-A from 0.97--1.05 (vehicle) to 1.16--1.87, on the methylated
      channel only, with no change under an inactive look-alike (GEO GSE135205~\cite{Pappalardi2021});
\item on single molecules from the same kind of experiment (one cell line, EM-seq, GEO GSE329728), IAM-A read 1.65--1.97 in all eight treated
      samples, with conversion failure unchanged;
\item in six early-onset colorectal tumours, the copy error was above that of the same patient's adjacent normal tissue in all six, median ratio 1.148
      (WGBS, ENA PRJNA1198593).
\end{itemize}
\measured{} These show that the readings respond to a known change in maintenance fidelity. They are not commissioning results and not disease
readings; where breach and the cancer region lie on this gauge is the next measurement (Chapter~\ref{ch:gauge}).

\section{The open problems}\label{sec:synth:notshown}
The next step for the cell is to derive its floor from the law alone; today its height comes from a measured holding energy, $E_{\rm hold}=3.41\,\kB T$. The processors' floors
follow from $\kB T$ at the temperature each record sees; how close each loss channel comes to its floor is still to be measured device by
device. The cosmic coupling is derived from the partition, but the entropy identification it rests on is a
conjecture tested by its consequences. The electron-mass formula carries a factor found numerically; the $3/16$ of the baryon relation and the
$2/\pi$ of the cosmological-constant estimate are taken as given here; their derivation is an open problem. A derivation of the cell's floor from the law alone, before the data are consulted, would
turn the ladder of places from a shared accounting into a numerical unification. It is the first item of the open problems
(Chapter~\ref{ch:open}). Chapter~\ref{ch:onegauge} sets the device and cell readings side by side on one gauge, and Chapter~\ref{ch:reach} says what each instrument could give.

A shared accounting across five places is already a strong result; a derivation of one floor from the law alone would make it a numerical unification. That is the first open problem, and the most rewarding one to take up.
