\chapter{Exploratory: what the law permits for gravitational engineering}\label{ch:exploratory}

\section*{The place}
This chapter asks what IAM permits for gravitational engineering and interstellar transit. The content falls into two zones: results that
follow from established physics and the structure of IAM already stated in Part~\ref{part:2}, and a conjectured engineering coupling that would
need a material not known to exist. Each zone carries what a referee would add to it, and every claim carries its status. Nothing in this
chapter is used anywhere else in the book.

\section{The premise}
IAM prices each irreversible record at $\kB T_H\ln2$ at the horizon temperature $T_H=\hbar H/2\pi\kB$, and the cost enters the matter-sector expansion as
$H^2_{\rm eff,m}=H^2_{\Lambda{\rm CDM}}+\beta_m E(a)H_0^2$ with $\beta_m=\Omega_m/2$ and $E(a)=\exp(1-1/a)$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:theory}). In the
weak field the local acceleration is the gradient of a potential, $\mathbf g=-\nabla\Phi$. The question is what follows if that gradient
could be shaped. Nothing below says it can.

\paragraph{Free fall carries no felt acceleration.} A body moving only under $\mathbf g=-\nabla\Phi$ follows a geodesic, and an observer
aboard feels no proper acceleration whatever the size or direction of $\nabla\Phi$, provided the gradient is uniform over the body; the residual
is the tidal term $f_{\rm tidal}\sim mL\,\partial^2\Phi$. \derived{} (equivalence principle). A craft that changed speed or direction by moving
a gradient would therefore carry its occupants through the change without felt force. This removes the inertial objection to abrupt
manoeuvres; it does not say how a gradient could be moved.

\paragraph{Hover and departure.} A hover is a gradient that cancels the ambient one; a departure is a steep gradient along the departure
direction, entered in free fall. Both are Newtonian gravity plus the equivalence principle. \derived{} (conditional on a shaped gradient)

\paragraph{No tilt signature.} A gradient-following craft does not tilt toward its acceleration like a plumb bob, and a tilt is not a
discriminator. That would contradict the free-fall result above: a plumb bob hangs because it is supported; a body in free
fall in a uniform gradient feels no torque and has no preferred orientation. Any tilt would come from the tidal term (a non-uniform gradient
across the hull) or from attitude control, and neither is fixed by the acceleration. \derived

\paragraph{Recession faster than light.} Comoving objects separate at $v_{\rm rec}=H_0D$, which exceeds $c$ beyond the Hubble radius
$D_H=c/H_0=1.40\times10^{10}$~ly at $H_0=70$ (1.45$\times10^{10}$~ly at the Planck value). \calc{} No object moves through space faster than $c$;
the expansion carries them. In IAM that expansion is the global term of the partition. Whether a space-carrying drive has a ``speed limit''
set by how steep an actualization imbalance can be sustained is open: $E(a)$ is a function of the scale factor, bounded by $E\to e$, and nothing
in it sets a rate of proper-separation change for a local region. \openprob

\paragraph{Proper time inside a locally flat displaced region.} If a region of flat spacetime is carried relative to distant stars by reshaping
the geometry around it, the occupants are inertial, their proper time equals the region's coordinate time, and the elapsed times aboard and at the
origin differ only by $\Delta\Phi/c^2\ll1$. \derived{} This is Alcubierre's result~\cite{Alcubierre1994}, conditional on such a region
existing. Three published results stand against it: every such metric requires negative energy density
in the wall of the region, violating the weak energy condition~\cite{Alcubierre1994}; quantum inequalities confine any negative energy to walls of
near-Planck thickness and push the total requirement to enormous magnitudes~\cite{PfenningFord1997}; and for a superluminal region the front wall is
outside the causal reach of anyone inside it, so it can neither be created on demand nor controlled from aboard~\cite{EverettRoman1997}.
\derived{} (published results) These are the obstacles, and none of them is addressed by IAM as formulated.

\paragraph{The Pleiades.} At $D=136$~pc~\cite{Melis2014} $=444$~ly, a one-way transit in seven days needs an effective rate of proper-separation change
$\xi c$ with $\xi=444/0.0192=2.3\times10^4$. \calc{} The distance is $3\times10^{-8}$ of the Hubble radius. Relativity does not forbid the number,
because it is not a local velocity; the energy conditions above are what stand in the way.

\section{Zone II: the conjectured engineering coupling}
\paragraph{The coupling.} The conjecture is a response kernel $G$ by which a controlled input $J(\mathbf x,t)$ drives the actualization
potential, $\Phi=\Phi_0+\int G\,J$. IAM states that $\Phi$ is sourced by the rate of irreversible actualization; the conjecture adds that this rate
can be driven rather than only read. $G$ is not derived. \conjecture

\paragraph{The material.} The further conjecture is that driving the rate needs matter poised at the potential/actual boundary, placed in the
predicted island of stability of superheavy nuclei ($Z\sim114$--126), and holding macroscopic quantum coherence (superconductivity) in its
operating state. Nuclear metastability and macroscopic electronic coherence are not known to coexist, and the proposal that the shell closures
that give the island its lifetime also support coherence is unproven. \conjecture{} The superheavy nuclei made so far live from milliseconds to
seconds and are made a few atoms at a time~\cite{Oganessian2015}; no bulk sample exists to test. \observed

\paragraph{The one checkable claim, and where it already stands.} If part of such a material's mass-energy did not source gravity, the sample
would weigh less than its measured mass-energy. That is a violation of the equivalence between gravitational and inertial mass. For ordinary
materials the equivalence holds to $\eta\lesssim10^{-15}$~\cite{Touboul2022}, and lunar laser ranging confirms that gravitational binding energy
itself gravitates as mass~\cite{Williams2004}. \observed{} For superconductors specifically, the one claim of anomalous weight (Podkletnov, 1992~\cite{Podkletnov1992}) was not reproduced: two replication attempts found no change in the
weight of a test mass~\cite{Woods2001,Hathaway2003}, although neither reproduced the original apparatus, so the claim
is neither confirmed nor excluded. A material with the proposed property would therefore have to differ from all tested matter, and a weighing at
$10^{-15}$ or better would be the test. \prediction{} (conditional on the material existing)

\paragraph{Three sources.} Conditional on $G$ and the material, three non-collinear phase-locked sources are the minimum to steer a single focal
node through a volume: two give only axial placement and one gives none. \derived{} as geometry of superposition, conditional on the unknown $G$.

In summary, the kinematics permit the destination; the vehicle is not derivable. The kinematics already require something general relativity
has never been shown to supply, negative energy in bulk, and IAM as formulated does not supply it either.
