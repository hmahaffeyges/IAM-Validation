\chapter{Falsifiable predictions}\label{ch:predictions}

\section*{How this list was made}
We collected every quantitative statement the framework makes, 655 in all, in one register (\path{CANON/predictions_register.csv} in the
repository, Appendix~\ref{app:repro}). The 136 cell entries wait for the commissioning of each cell type and are not listed here. Each of the other
519 was recomputed and given one verdict: kept, with its value recomputed where needed (87), already tested (63), a duplicate of another entry
(162), or dropped (207). An entry was dropped when it is not falsifiable (a definition, a restatement, an interpretation), when the recomputation
does not support it, when it is a vendor roadmap or commercial statement with no physics in it, or when no test is named. The verdict for every
entry, with its reason, is in \path{CANON/predictions_triage_2026-10-02.json}.
Appendix~\ref{app:register} lists every entry that stands, with its test, its falsifier and its verdict.

What remains is about forty distinct predictions. Each is given below with its number, the measurement that tests it, what would falsify it,
and the register entries it merges (in brackets). Every value is recomputed. Dates are those of the named surveys; none is claimed for an
experiment no one has announced. A prediction built on a calibrated input says so: it tests the input as much as the law.

\section{What the list shows}
The cosmology carries the decisive tests, and they are close. One parameter-free line fixes $\mu_0$, the $f\sigma_8$ ramp, $\Sigma=1$, $E_G$,
$S_8$ and the two Hubble rates together; how strongly Euclid decides it depends on the smallest scales it can model, $0.3\sigma$ to about $7\sigma$ for the IAM $\mu(z)$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:latetime}), and redshift-space distortions at the per-cent level test the shape of the ramp. The quantum-device predictions are dated and
cheap to check, but each rests on a calibrated floor or exponent. The decoherence predictions are the most distinctive, a coherence time that
grows with temperature, and the furthest from current experiments. Several claims have already failed or been contradicted, and they are listed
above with the rest.
