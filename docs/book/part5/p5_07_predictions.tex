\chapter{Falsifiable predictions}\label{ch:predictions}

\section*{How this list was made}
The source papers state 655 predictions and results; the register in \texttt{CANON/\allowbreak{}predictions\_register.csv} collects them with their
sources. The 136 cell entries wait for the commissioning of each cell type and are not listed here. Each of the other 519 was checked against the
corrections record (Appendix~\ref{app:errata}) and against the numbers recomputed for this book, and given one verdict: kept as stated (14),
corrected (73), already tested (63), a duplicate of another entry (162), or dropped (207). An entry was dropped when it is not falsifiable (a
definition, a restatement, an interpretation), when a correction withdrew it, when it is a vendor roadmap or commercial statement with no physics
in it, or when no test is named. The full verdict list, with the reason for every entry, is in
\texttt{CANON/\allowbreak{}predictions\_triage\_2026-10-02.json}.

What remains is about forty distinct predictions. Each is given below with its number, the measurement that tests it, what would falsify it,
and the register entries it merges (in brackets). Every value is the corrected one. Dates are those of the named surveys; none is claimed for an
experiment no one has announced. A prediction built on a calibrated input says so: it tests the input as much as the law.

\section{What the list shows}
The cosmology carries the decisive tests, and they are close. One parameter-free line fixes $\mu_0$, the $f\sigma_8$ ramp, $\Sigma=1$, $E_G$,
$S_8$ and the two Hubble rates together; Euclid's complete first data release in mid-2027 tests $\mu_0$ at about $1.7\sigma$ and its final survey at
about $3.4\sigma$, and redshift-space distortions at the per-cent level test the shape of the ramp. The quantum-device predictions are dated and
cheap to check, but each rests on a calibrated floor or exponent. The decoherence predictions are the most distinctive, a coherence time that
grows with temperature, and the furthest from current experiments. Several claims have already failed or been contradicted, and they are listed
above with the rest.
