\chapter{Duration, the arrow of time, and the boundary between potential and actual}\label{ch:time}

\section{Two faces}\label{sec:time_faces}
Time enters physics in more than one role, and it is two-faced. The first face was identified by Barbour and Bertotti in 1982 within the Machian
relational framework: coordinate time is redundant, a label on a dimension, not a river carrying anything forward~\cite{BarbourBertotti1982}. That result
has shaped how foundational questions about time are posed. The second face concerns irreversibility. Is every temporal quantity equally redundant? One
is not: the accumulated count of irreversible records on a worldline has a physical carrier and a direction, and the relational framework accommodates
it naturally. \interp

\section{Three kinds of time}\label{sec:time_three}
Three quantities are often treated as one. The differences between them are not merely terminological.

\paragraph{Coordinate time: the map.} Coordinate time, $t$ or the scale factor $a$, is a label on the spacetime manifold. It assigns a number to each
event. It does not require anything to happen: not worldlines, not mass, not irreversible processes. The manifold exists with its coordinate structure
whether or not any event within it is irreversible. Barbour's insight~\cite{BarbourBertotti1982,Barbour1994} is that this coordinate has no privileged
status: it is redundant as a fundamental concept, and the relational framework builds dynamics from configurations alone, without an external temporal
parameter. Coordinate time is the map, not the territory. \derived{} (relational mechanics)

\paragraph{Proper time: path length on the map.} Proper time is the geometric length of a worldline,
\begin{equation}
\tau=\frac1c\int\sqrt{-g_{\mu\nu}\,dx^\mu dx^\nu}.\label{eq:time_tau}
\end{equation}
It is still geometric, a property of the manifold measured along a particular path. A clock measures it: it tells how far one has travelled on the map.
Proper time is reversible in principle. Under time-reversal-symmetric laws a worldline run forward and backward in coordinate time accumulates $\tau$ in
both directions; proper time distinguishes clocks from one another but does not, by itself, distinguish past from future. For null geodesics,
$d\tau=0$ identically. Photons appear on the manifold but accumulate no proper time. This is not an approximation; it is the definition of a null
geodesic. \derived

\paragraph{Accumulated record: the territory.} The third quantity is neither coordinate time nor proper time. It is not geometric; it is
thermodynamic. It counts the irreversible quantum-to-classical transitions on a worldline, each a record written at the nearest encoding surface at a
Landauer cost of at least $\kB T\ln2$ per bit~\cite{Landauer1961}; for the records of collapsing matter the largest such surface is the cosmic horizon
(Chapter~\ref{ch:quantumrecords}). It requires
\begin{enumerate}
\item massive particles on timelike worldlines ($d\tau>0$), and
\item irreversible quantum-to-classical transitions (gravitational decoherence).
\end{enumerate}
Massive particles exist from electroweak symmetry breaking ($T\approx160$\,GeV, $t\approx9\times10^{-12}$\,s; Chapter~\ref{ch:electroweak}), when the $W$, the
$Z$ and the charged fermions acquire mass. The irreversibility itself does not depend on CP violation: decoherence and entropy production are
irreversible for any interaction. The accumulated record is negligible for most of cosmic history, $E(z{=}10)=4.5\times10^{-5}$, and grows as structure
forms. \calc{} Figure~\ref{fig:time_two_faces} sets the three side by side: the same worldline admits a geometric and a thermodynamic description.

Photons therefore accumulate neither proper time nor record. Massive particles accumulate both, but only the record is irreversible. The arrow of time is
then not a statistical statement about low-entropy initial conditions~\cite{Penrose1989,Carroll2010}. It is the direction in which records are written,
and every entry costs at least $\kB T\ln2$ per bit at the surface where it is written. \conjecture

\section{The second face: the accumulated record}\label{sec:time_record}
Within IAM the accumulated record of the matter sector is measured by the activation function (Eq.~\ref{part2:eq:Ea}),
\begin{equation}
E(a)=\exp\!\left(1-\frac1a\right),\label{eq:time_Ea}
\end{equation}
with $a$ the scale factor normalised to unity today. Its properties follow from its physical reading:
\begin{itemize}
\item $E(a)\to0$ as $a\to0$: negligible in the early universe;
\item $E(1)=1$: normalised to today;
\item $E(a)\to e$ as $a\to\infty$: a finite asymptote, approached but never reached;
\item $dE/da=a^{-2}\exp(1-1/a)>0$ for all $a$: monotonic, one direction only.
\end{itemize}
\derived{} (sympy, C1). The monotonic increase is not imposed. It follows from the irreversibility of each record: a bit written cannot be unwritten,
and the record only accumulates. This is the arrow of time --- not statistical, not dependent on initial conditions, but thermodynamically required.
\interp{} The universe has been paying the thermodynamic price of its own classicality, one record at a time; the arrow of time is the accumulated
receipt. In the language of quantum Darwinism (Chapter~\ref{ch:quantumrecords}) each selection of a pointer state of collapsed matter is one such
entry, and the record has a beginning, the appearance of massive particles, and an asymptotic end. \interp

\paragraph{Light records nothing in flight.} Photons travel null geodesics, $d\tau=0$ by construction. No proper time accumulates, and no gravitational
record is written; such a record needs nonzero proper time on a timelike worldline. Therefore
\begin{equation}
E(a)\big|_{\rm photon}\equiv0 .\label{eq:time_Ephoton}
\end{equation}
\derived{} This is the physical basis of the sector split. Photons interact with the geometry --- they are bent by gravity and redshifted by
expansion --- but they add nothing to $E(a)$. The change that $E(a)$ makes to gravitational dynamics applies to the matter sector alone.

\paragraph{The Machian framework already allows it.} The relational framework insists that physics be built from observable relations between things.
Coordinate time fails this test: it is not directly observable. Proper time passes: clocks measure it. The accumulated record passes as well, and it is
the temporal quantity that distinguishes past from future through a physical mechanism rather than a statistical assumption. Mach's criterion --- that
time is an abstraction reached through the changes of things --- selects physical change over coordinate time. Not all changes are equal: reversible
changes leave $E(a)$ unchanged; only irreversible records, each paying its Landauer cost, advance it. \interp

\section{What the sector split implies}\label{sec:time_split}
The distinction between the geometric and the thermodynamic quantity has an observable consequence that bears on a persistent tension. The CMB
measurement of $H_0$ uses photons emitted at last scattering and travelling null geodesics for 13.8\,Gyr. The whole measurement is geometric: no proper
time accumulated and no record written along the way. It reads the geometry: $H_0^{(\rm photon)}=67.16$\,km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ is what the manifold
says about its own expansion rate~\cite{Planck2018VI}. \measured{} The local distance ladder uses Cepheids, Type~Ia supernovae and the tip of the red
giant branch --- massive objects on timelike worldlines that have accumulated records for billions of years. It reads the matter sector:
$H_0^{(\rm matter)}=72.26$\,km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ is what the matter sector says about its own expansion rate~\cite{Riess2022}
(Chapter~\ref{ch:sectortension}). \calc{} (Level~2 chain value; $67.16\sqrt{1+\beta_m}=72.26$)

On this reading the two are not necessarily two measurements of one quantity. They may be measurements of two quantities, one geometric and one
thermodynamic, that a purely geometric treatment does not distinguish. The Hubble tension is consistent with the sector split, and both values are
predicted with the coupling held fixed by the virial partition,
\begin{equation}
\beta_m=\frac{\Omega_m}{2}=0.15765\label{eq:time_betam}
\end{equation}
(Chapter~\ref{ch:virial}). \derived{} (given IAM's Law and the virial partition) The split itself is a prediction tested by the chains, not a
closed-form derivation of either number. \prediction

\section{The relational framework and shape dynamics}\label{sec:time_shape}
Shape dynamics~\cite{Barbour2012,BarbourKoslowskiMercati2014} replaces the refoliation invariance of four-dimensional spacetime with three-dimensional
conformal invariance. The universe is described by the evolution of its shape, the relational configuration of matter, without reference to absolute
scale or absolute time. IAM is compatible with this framework at every level. It does not modify the background geometry and does not alter the
Friedmann equations. It operates at the level of perturbations, through one term in the matter perturbation equation,
\begin{equation}
\ddot\delta_m+2H\dot\delta_m-\frac32\,\Omega_mH_0^2a^{-3}\,\mu(a)\,\delta_m=0,\qquad
\mu(a)=\frac{H^2_{\Lambda\rm CDM}(a)}{H^2_{\Lambda\rm CDM}(a)+\beta_mE(a)H_0^2}<1,\label{eq:time_growth}
\end{equation}
in which $\mu$ multiplies the source term (the form of the Level~1 chains, Eq.~\ref{part2:eq:mu}). \derived{} The Level~2 chains put the same term into
the expansion rate felt by matter perturbations, $H_m^2=H^2+\beta_mE(a)H_0^2$, so that it enters as additional Hubble friction with the Poisson equation
unmodified (Chapter~\ref{ch:level2}); each growth number in the book states which form it uses. In both forms the geometric arena --- shape space, the
relational configuration --- is untouched. The accumulated record is what happens on the worldlines within that arena; it does not modify the arena.
Shape dynamics asks what the universe's configuration is; IAM asks how much irreversible information the matter sector has produced in reaching it. The
two questions are complementary. \interp

\section{Before and after the electroweak transition}\label{sec:time_ew}
Before electroweak symmetry breaking ($t\approx9\times10^{-12}$~s; Chapter~\ref{ch:electroweak}) every Standard Model particle is
massless. There are no timelike worldlines carrying rest mass, no gravitational decoherence
of massive bodies, and $E(a)\equiv0$. After the transition the matter sector exists and the
photon remains massless under exact $U(1)_{\mathrm{em}}$, so the two sectors of
Chapter~\ref{ch:dual} separate permanently.

\begin{remark}
The activation function of Eq.~\eqref{part2:eq:Ea} is normalized to $E(1)=1$ and is exponentially
small, not zero, at $a_{\mathrm{EW}}\sim10^{-15}$: $E=\exp(1-10^{15})$. ``$E\equiv0$ before
the transition'' is therefore a physical statement about the sector content, not a property
of the formula, which has no feature at $a_{\mathrm{EW}}$.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{An open question.} Electroweak symmetry breaking selects one point of the degenerate Higgs vacuum manifold. Whether that selection was itself
the first gravitational-scale record, with its own Landauer cost at the transition temperature, is not settled (Chapter~\ref{ch:higgsrecord}). \openprob
