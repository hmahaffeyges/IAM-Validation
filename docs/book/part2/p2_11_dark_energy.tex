\chapter{The equation of state of the record}\label{ch:darkenergy}

\section*{The place}
The cosmic horizon, at $T_{GH}=\hbar H/2\pi k_B$; the record is $S_{\rm info}$, written by matter as it collapses into structure
(Chapter~\ref{ch:surfaces}). This chapter asks how the energy of that record behaves as the universe expands, where it stands today, and
where it ends. Chapter~\ref{ch:wzfuture} follows it through time, compares it with the dark-energy fits, and sets out the tests.

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:de_intro}
The accelerating expansion of the universe is one of the most precisely established facts in modern cosmology~\cite{Riess1998,Perlmutter1999,Planck2018VI}.
\observed\ Its cause remains unexplained within standard physics. The simplest model, a cosmological constant $\Lambda$ with equation of state
$w=-1$ exactly, fits current data well but carries significant theoretical difficulties: the fine-tuning problem (why is $\Lambda$ so small?), the
coincidence problem (why is it comparable to the matter density now?), and a profound implication that is rarely stated explicitly --- if $w=-1$
is eternal and exact, the universe will expand forever, all structure will dilute, and thermodynamic equilibrium will be approached
asymptotically from above. Heat death. No saturation. No completion.

DESI measurements of the baryon acoustic oscillation scale now provide observational evidence that dark energy is not a pure cosmological
constant. DESI DR2 in combination with CMB and supernova data finds a preference for dynamical dark energy at $2.8$--$4.2\sigma$ depending on the
supernova dataset~\cite{DESI2025}. The best-fit $w_0$--$w_a$ parametrization favors $w_0>-1$ and $w_a<0$, though the physical origin of this
evolution is unknown within the $w_0$--$w_a$ framework. \observed\ That preference comes from distances alone (BAO, supernovae and the CMB
acoustic scale), which are measured with light.

IAM derives a specific dark energy equation of state from first principles, without free parameters. The derivation proceeds via the
Jacobson--Cai-Kim thermodynamic procedure applied to the cosmological apparent horizon, with an additional informational entropy term
$S_{\rm info}$ sourced by gravitational decoherence during structure formation~\cite{Jacobson1995,CaiKim2005}. The result is a perturbation-level
modification of the growth equations (not the background Friedmann equation) producing the dual-sector signature $\mu<1$, $\Sigma=1$, consistent
with all current observational constraints (Chapters~\ref{ch:latetime} and~\ref{ch:level2}). IAM does not replace $\Lambda$ or general
relativity: it adds the Landauer cost of every irreversible record, here the gravitational record written to the cosmic horizon as structure
forms (Part~\ref{part:1}).

This chapter and Chapter~\ref{ch:wzfuture} derive the full future evolution of an IAM universe. Section~\ref{sec:de_eos} presents the IAM
equation of state and its asymptotic properties. Section~\ref{sec:de_saturation} analyzes the saturation of the activation function and the
asymptotic Hubble parameter. Section~\ref{sec:de_maturity} develops the informational maturity framework and milestone timeline.
Chapter~\ref{ch:wzfuture} advances three arguments against the heat death picture, compares the IAM prediction to DESI DR2 in the $w_0$--$w_a$
plane, identifies specific falsifiable tests, and summarizes the predictions and their observational consequences.

\section{The IAM equation of state}\label{sec:de_eos}
\subsection{Derivation from the informational sector}\label{sec:de_sinfo}
IAM's derivation follows the Jacobson--Cai-Kim procedure applied to the cosmological apparent horizon. The standard procedure equates the first
law of thermodynamics on the apparent horizon to the Friedmann equations~\cite{CaiKim2005}. IAM adds an informational entropy term
\begin{equation}
S_{\rm info}(a)=S_0\,E(a)=S_0\exp\!\left(1-\frac1a\right),\label{eq:de_sinfo}
\end{equation}
where $E(a)$ is the activation function derived from horizon thermodynamics (Chapter~\ref{ch:theory}). This term reflects the cumulative
irreversible gravitational information produced by structure formation and encoded on the cosmological apparent horizon. The coupling constant
$S_0$ is fixed by the virial theorem: $\beta_m=\Omega_m/2$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:virial_law}), $0.15765$ for $\Omega_m=0.3153$, the same value in
every chain. \prediction

In the expansion rate the term appears in the matter sector only:
\begin{equation}H_m^2(a)=H^2(a)+\beta_mE(a)H_0^2,\qquad E(a)=e^{1-1/a},\qquad\beta_m=\Omega_m/2,\label{part2:eq:Hm}\end{equation}
while the photon sector keeps $H(a)$, the $\Lambda$CDM rate. The informational term therefore behaves as an energy density
\begin{equation}\rho_{\rm info}(a)=\beta_m\,E(a)\,\rho_{\rm crit,0},\qquad\rho_{\rm crit,0}=\frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G}.\label{eq:de_rhoinfo}\end{equation}

The informational contribution to the effective dark energy equation of state follows from the first-law identity relating the pressure to the
rate of change of the informational energy density. No scalar field is introduced. The two assumptions are (i) the informational density is
Eq.~\eqref{eq:de_rhoinfo}, $\rho_{\rm info}\propto E(a)$, and (ii) it is separately conserved, with pressure $p_{\rm info}=w_{\rm info}\rho_{\rm info}c^2$.
Energy conservation in an expanding background then gives
\begin{equation}\dot\rho_{\rm info}+3H\,(1+w_{\rm info})\,\rho_{\rm info}=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad w_{\rm info}=-1-\frac13\,\frac{d\ln\rho_{\rm info}}{d\ln a}.\label{eq:de_cont}\end{equation}
With assumption (i), $d\ln\rho_{\rm info}/d\ln a=d\ln E/d\ln a$, and since $\ln E=1-1/a$,
\begin{equation}\frac{d\ln E}{d\ln a}=a\,\frac{d}{da}\left(1-\frac1a\right)=a\cdot\frac{1}{a^2}=\frac1a .\label{eq:de_dlnE}\end{equation}
Substituting into Eq.~\eqref{eq:de_cont} gives the informational sector equation of state:
\begin{equation}\boxed{w_{\rm info}(a)=-1-\frac{1}{3a}.}\label{eq:de_winfo}\end{equation}
\derived\ This result contains no free parameters. The $-1$ arises from the vacuum-like pressure of the encoded information; the $-1/(3a)$ arises
from the derivative of the activation function with respect to the scale factor. Substituting Eq.~\eqref{eq:de_winfo} back into
Eq.~\eqref{eq:de_cont} with $\rho_{\rm info}=\rho_0E(a)$ leaves a residual of zero (verification script, section A2).

Eq.~\eqref{eq:de_winfo} is the equation of state of the matter-sector term in Eq.~\eqref{part2:eq:Hm}. Distances measured with light follow the
photon-sector rate $H(a)$, on which the term predicts $w=-1$ (Section~\ref{sec:de_rulers}).

\subsection{Properties of the equation of state}\label{sec:de_props}
Equation~\eqref{eq:de_winfo} has several immediate consequences.

\paragraph{Always phantom.} $w_{\rm info}(a)<-1$ for all finite $a>0$. The phantom character is not divergent --- it is strictly bounded. \derived

\paragraph{Asymptotically approaches $-1$.} As $a\to\infty$:
\begin{equation}\lim_{a\to\infty}w_{\rm info}(a)=-1 .\label{eq:de_wlim}\end{equation}
The informational equation of state becomes indistinguishable from a cosmological constant at late times. The phantom behavior is a transient
feature of the structure-formation epoch, not a permanent property of the dark energy. \derived

\paragraph{Current value.} At $a=1$ (today):
\begin{equation}w_{\rm info}(1)=-1-\tfrac13=-\tfrac43 .\label{eq:de_w1}\end{equation}
\derived

\paragraph{No Big Rip.} A Big Rip requires $w<-1$ that is constant or grows more phantom over time. In IAM, $w_{\rm info}(a)$ is phantom but
\emph{decreasing} in phantom character ($dw/da=1/(3a^2)>0$ for all $a$), approaching $-1$ from below; and because $E(a)\le e$, the
informational density never exceeds $e$ times its present value. \derived\ The informational pressure term is self-limiting. As expansion
accelerates, structure formation slows, reducing the rate of information production, which reduces the informational pressure contribution.
This feedback mechanism prevents runaway acceleration. \interp\ The proof is given in Chapter~\ref{ch:wzfuture}, Section~\ref{sec:wz_norip}.

\paragraph{Weight in the past.} At early times $w_{\rm info}$ is strongly negative, but $\rho_{\rm info}\propto E\to0$: at $z=3$ the

Section~\ref{sec:wz_history}). \calc

\subsection{CPL mapping for observational comparison}\label{sec:de_cpl}
The Chevallier--Polarski--Linder (CPL) parametrization $w(a)=w_0+w_a(1-a)$ is the standard tool for observational dark energy
analysis~\cite{ChevallierPolarski2001,Linder2003}. To compare IAM's prediction to DESI DR2, $w_{\rm info}(a)$ is mapped to effective CPL parameters
by matching the value and first derivative at $a=1$:
\begin{equation}w_0=w_{\rm info}(a)\big|_{a=1}=-\tfrac43\approx-1.333,\qquad w_a=-\left.\frac{dw_{\rm info}(a)}{da}\right|_{a=1}=-\tfrac13\approx-0.333 .\label{eq:wz_cpl}\end{equation}
\derived\ The IAM point $(w_0,w_a)=(-4/3,-1/3)$ lies in the phantom regime ($w_0<-1$) with a small negative $w_a$, meaning the effective equation
of state becomes less phantom over time in the CPL approximation: in the CPL form $dw/da=-w_a>0$. How this point compares with what DESI DR2
reports, why that comparison is not a test, and the limitations of the CPL comparison are discussed in Chapter~\ref{ch:wzfuture},
Sections~\ref{sec:wz_cplsign} and~\ref{sec:wz_cpllimits}.

\section{$E(a)$ saturation and the asymptotic universe}\label{sec:de_saturation}
\subsection{The activation function and its ceiling}
The activation function $E(a)=\exp(1-1/a)$ is a monotonically increasing function of the scale factor. Its properties:
\begin{equation}E(0^+)=0,\qquad E(1)=1\ \ (\text{today}),\qquad\lim_{a\to\infty}E(a)=e\approx2.71828\ldots\label{eq:de_Elimits}\end{equation}
\derived\ The function saturates at $e$. This is not an imposed ceiling --- it emerges from the thermodynamic structure of the activation function
derived from horizon thermodynamics. As $a$ grows large, the argument $(1-1/a)\to1$ and the exponential approaches $e$ from below. No finite value
of $a$ achieves saturation; the approach is asymptotic. $E(1)=1$ because $1-1/a=0$ at $a=1$: it is today's normalisation. $E$ has its inflection
in $a$ at $a=1/2$ ($z=1$), where $d^2E/da^2=-E\,(2a-1)/a^4$ changes sign, and its inflection in $\ln a$ at $a=1$ (verification script, sections
A6--A7). \derived

\subsection{Asymptotic Hubble parameter}\label{sec:de_Hinf}
The IAM background expansion is standard $\Lambda$CDM. The matter perturbation equations are modified by the informational sector, but the
Friedmann equation governing the background is unchanged (Chapter~\ref{ch:level2}):
\begin{equation}H^2(a)=H_0^2\left[\Omega_ma^{-3}+\Omega_\Lambda\right].\label{eq:de_friedmann}\end{equation}
As $a\to\infty$, the matter term $\Omega_ma^{-3}\to0$ and the background expansion approaches de~Sitter behavior driven by the cosmological
constant:
\begin{equation}H_\infty=H_0\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}\approx H_0\times0.8275\approx55.6~\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}},\label{eq:de_Hinf}\end{equation}
\calc\ for the photon-sector $H_0=67.16$ and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.6847$ of the Level~2 chains ($55.57$; $55.78$ for the Planck 2018 base values
$H_0=67.4$, $\Omega_\Lambda=0.685$).

The expansion does not halt; it approaches a constant rate determined by $\Lambda$. However, the \emph{informational contribution} to the
expansion driver (the $E(a)$ term that modifies the perturbation equations) saturates. The universe reaches a final state where the
informational entropy has been fully encoded on the cosmic horizon, structure formation has ended, and the background expansion coasts at
$H_\infty$. \calc

This is not heat death. It is informational completion --- a distinct and physically meaningful final state that $\Lambda$CDM cannot describe
(Chapter~\ref{ch:wzfuture}, Section~\ref{sec:wz_heatdeath}). \interp\ In the photon sector the end state is the de~Sitter state of $\Lambda$CDM;
what ends is the writing.

\subsection{Final effective equation of state}\label{sec:de_weff}
As $a\to\infty$, the informational contribution to the departure of the total equation of state from $-1$ vanishes ($w_{\rm info}\to-1$), and the
universe behaves as a pure cosmological constant. The \emph{effective} total equation of state, blending the $\Lambda$ sector with the saturated
informational sector, approaches:
\begin{equation}\lim_{a\to\infty}w_{\rm eff}(a)=-1 .\label{eq:de_weff}\end{equation}
\derived\ The universe arrives at $w=-1$ not because it started there, but because the informational sector exhausts itself. The phantom phase is a
transient --- the epoch of structure formation, from $z\sim2$ to the far future, during which information is being actively produced and encoded.
Once structure formation is complete and the whole record has been written, the informational driver shuts off and the cosmological constant
takes full control. \interp

decoherence. Since $E(a)$ ranges from $0$ (no information encoded) to $e$ (saturation), the ratio $E(a)/e$ ranges from $0$ to $1$.

At $a=1$ (today):
\begin{equation}\frac{E(1)}{e}=\frac{e^0}{e}=\frac1e\approx0.36788=36.8\,\%.\label{eq:de_maturity_today}\end{equation}
\derived\ The universe is currently at exactly $1/e$ maturity. This is not a coincidence of units --- it is the mathematical inflection point of
the activation function in $\ln a$. The rate of informational maturation per unit scale factor,
\begin{equation}\frac{d(E/e)}{da}=\frac{E(a)}{e\,a^2}=\frac{e^{-1/a}}{a^2},\label{eq:de_rate_a}\end{equation}
equals $1/e\approx0.368$ per unit scale factor at $a=1$ and achieves its maximum, $4/e^2=0.541$, at $a=1/2$ ($z=1$). Per e-fold of expansion the
rate,
\begin{equation}\frac{d(E/e)}{d\ln a}=\frac{e^{-1/a}}{a},\label{eq:de_rate_lna}\end{equation}
achieves its maximum, $1/e$, at $a=1$. \derived\ Today, per e-fold of expansion, the universe is maturing faster than at any other epoch in its past
or future. Per unit cosmic time the peak lies in the past (Section~\ref{sec:de_rate_t}).
