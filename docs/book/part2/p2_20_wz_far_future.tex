\section{Against heat death: three arguments}\label{sec:wz_heatdeath}
\interp\ The standard heat death picture follows from $w=-1$ eternal: dark energy drives accelerating expansion indefinitely, all matter dilutes,
all structure dissolves, temperature approaches absolute zero everywhere, and thermodynamic gradients that enable work and information processing
vanish. IAM contradicts this picture from three independent directions. Each is an interpretation of the results of Chapter~\ref{ch:darkenergy};
the calculation underneath them is stated at the end of the section.

\subsection{Argument 1: bounded informational pressure}
In $\Lambda$CDM, the dark energy density is constant ($\rho_\Lambda=\mathrm{const}$) and the expansion it drives has no natural endpoint. In IAM,
the informational contribution to the dark energy driver is $\propto E(a)$, which is bounded above by $e$. As $a\to\infty$, the informational
pressure saturates and the informational term in the modified expansion equations approaches a constant.

The universe does not accelerate forever without bound. It approaches a final expansion rate $H_\infty$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:de_Hinf}) at which point
the informational contribution has been completely encoded and the remaining expansion is driven solely by the cosmological constant. The
distinction is subtle but physical: the informational epoch ends, even if the $\Lambda$-driven expansion continues. \interp

\subsection{Argument 2: thermodynamic drive toward equilibrium}
Classical thermodynamics describes heat death as the maximum entropy state of a closed system. But the universe is not a closed system in the
sense assumed by the heat death argument --- it is bounded by the cosmological horizon, which is itself a thermal surface at the Gibbons--Hawking
temperature $T_{GH}=\hbar H/(2\pi k_B)$~\cite{GibbonsHawking1977}.

A thermal system bounded by surfaces that can exchange energy approaches equilibrium with those surfaces, not infinite dissolution. IAM's
self-limiting expansion ($E(a)$ bounded) means the horizon settles toward a final configuration. The system reaches \emph{equilibrium}, not the
open-ended dissolution of classical heat death. These are thermodynamically distinct endpoints. \interp

\subsection{Argument 3: the record is kept}
The heat death scenario requires that all distinctions be erased --- all structure dissolved into maximum disorder. But in IAM, information is
thermodynamically real and is kept. The Bekenstein--Hawking entropy $S=A/(4\ell_P^2)$ on the cosmological horizon represents a permanent record of
every decoherence event that contributed to it. The horizon does not erase information; it \emph{encodes} it~\cite{Bekenstein1973,Hawking1975}.

If information is kept on encoding surfaces, heat death in the classical sense cannot occur: the horizon retains the record of all structure that
has ever existed. The universe at informational saturation is not maximally disordered --- it is maximally \emph{encoded}. Every bit that could be
written has been written. This is not death. It is completion. \interp

\paragraph{What the calculation fixes.} Whatever reading is preferred, the physics is the same: the far future of the photon sector is the
de~Sitter state of $\Lambda$CDM at $H_\infty$, and what ends is the writing, $E\to e$ (Section~\ref{sec:wz_farfuture}). \calc\ How the end
state relates to the thermodynamic arrow of time is taken up in Part~\ref{part:5}.

\section{The no-Big-Rip proof}\label{sec:wz_norip}
For completeness, the no-Big-Rip result is formalized here. A Big Rip requires $w<-1$ with a density that grows without bound, as for constant
$w<-1$~\cite{Caldwell2002,Caldwell2003}, leading to divergent expansion at finite time. For IAM:
\begin{equation}\frac{dw_{\rm info}}{da}=\frac{1}{3a^2}>0\quad\text{for all }a>0,\qquad\rho_{\rm info}(a)\le e\,\rho_{\rm info}(1).\label{eq:wz_norip}\end{equation}
The equation of state increases monotonically toward $-1$, never decreasing further below $-1$. The phantom character is strictly decreasing over
cosmic time. The proof has three steps.

\paragraph{Step 1: the density from the equation of state.} Integrating the continuity equation \eqref{eq:de_cont},
\begin{equation}\frac{\rho_{\rm info}(a)}{\rho_{\rm info}(1)}=\exp\!\left[-3\int_1^a\bigl(1+w_{\rm info}\bigr)\,\frac{da'}{a'}\right]
=\exp\!\left[\int_1^a\frac{da'}{a'^2}\right]=e^{\,1-1/a}=E(a),\label{eq:wz_rho_from_w}\end{equation}
since $-3(1+w_{\rm info})=1/a$. The equation of state returns the density it came from. \derived

\paragraph{Step 2: the bound.} The integral $\int_1^\infty da'/a'^2=1$ converges, which gives the bound in Eq.~\eqref{eq:wz_norip}. A constant
$w<-1$ makes the same integral diverge, which is why constant phantom dark energy rips and this term does not. The condition $dw/da>0$ gives the
direction of travel, toward $-1$; the boundedness of Eq.~\eqref{eq:wz_rho_from_w} is what excludes the rip. \derived

\paragraph{Step 3: no finite-time singularity.} With the bound, the matter-sector rate satisfies
$H_m^2\le H_0^2(\Omega_ma^{-3}+\Omega_\Lambda+\beta_me)$, so for $a\ge1$, $H_m\le H_0\sqrt{1+\beta_me}=1.195\,H_0$. The scale factor then grows at
most exponentially, $a(t)\le\exp[1.195\,H_0(t-t_0)]$, and reaches no infinite value at finite time. The Big Rip is excluded. \derived\ The bound
follows from $E(a)\le e$ alone; no scalar field is introduced.

\subsection{Falsifiable predictions}\label{sec:wz_tests}
IAM makes specific, testable predictions for the equation of state as a function of redshift, each stated on the ruler it belongs to:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{$w(z)$ trajectory, on the matter ruler.} The informational equation of state follows $w(z)=-1-(1+z)/3$ exactly (substituting
$a=1/(1+z)$). It is a property of the matter-sector rate. The test is the expansion history measured with matter (distance ladder, sirens, peculiar
velocities) against the history measured with light, redshift by redshift: $H_m/H=1.076$ today, rising to $1.275$ as $a\to\infty$

If $w>-1$ is confirmed on the matter ruler at any redshift, IAM is disfavored. \prediction
\item \emph{Light-ruler distances follow $w=-1$.} Distances measured with light requiring $w\neq-1$, if confirmed, are not produced by this term.
\prediction
\item \emph{Asymptotic approach.} At high redshift ($z\gg1$, $a\ll1$) the IAM equation of state becomes strongly phantom,
$w(z)\approx-1-(1+z)/3$, but $\rho_{\rm info}\propto E\to0$: $3.1\,\%$ of $\rho_\Lambda$ at $z=2$ and $1.15\,\%$ at $z=3$. No high-redshift
supernova survey can separate it from $w=-1$. \calc
\item \emph{No Big Rip.} If the equation of state is confirmed to be phantom but trending toward $-1$ over time, consistent with the IAM trajectory,
and no Big Rip signature is detected by future surveys, that is consistent with the no-Big-Rip result (Section~\ref{sec:wz_norip}). \prediction
\item \emph{Consistency with growth suppression.} The equation of state prediction and the growth suppression ($\sigma_8=0.800$ in the Level~2
chains, $\mu<1$, $f\sigma_8$ low by $4.25\,\%$ today, Chapter~\ref{ch:surveys}) derive from the same activation function $E(a)$. A survey that
simultaneously measures the matter-ruler expansion and the growth rate $f\sigma_8(z)$ can test whether both are consistent with the same underlying
$E(a)$. This cross-check is unique to IAM and is not reproducible by generic $w_0$--$w_a$ models with separate growth parameters. \prediction
\item \emph{Falsifier.} Matter-ruler data that require $w>-1$ where the term predicts $w_{\rm info}<-1$. No such comparison has been made yet.
\openprob
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Relation to existing dark energy models}\label{sec:wz_models}
IAM's equation of state $w_{\rm info}=-1-1/(3a)$ shares the phantom character ($w<-1$) with phantom scalar field models~\cite{Caldwell2002} but
differs fundamentally in its mechanism and trajectory. Standard phantom models require a negative kinetic energy term, which violates energy
conditions and, for constant $w<-1$, leads to a Big Rip at finite time~\cite{Caldwell2003}. IAM's phantom behavior emerges from the
thermodynamics of information encoding, not a negative kinetic term, and is bounded by the saturation of $E(a)$ (Section~\ref{sec:wz_norip}).
\derived

The equation of state does not fit cleanly into any existing parametrization class. It is not constant, not CPL, not quintessence (which has
$w\ge-1$). It is derived, with no free parameters, from a specific physical mechanism. This distinguishes it observationally from all fitting
functions: the $w(z)$ trajectory is a prediction, not a model. \prediction

\subsection{The inflection point and the present epoch}\label{sec:wz_inflection}
The mathematical fact that $E(1)/e=1/e$ --- that the universe's informational maturity today equals $1/e$, precisely the inflection point of the
activation function in $\ln a$ --- is a consequence of the normalization $E(1)=1$: the argument $(1-1/a)$ of the activation function vanishes at
$a=1$. \derived

At this inflection point the rate of informational maturation per e-fold, $d(E/e)/d\ln a$, is maximized. Per e-fold of expansion, the current
epoch is the era of fastest growth in the universe's informational complexity: structure formation --- the production of irreversible
gravitational information through galaxy formation, stellar evolution, and black hole growth --- is occurring at its maximum rate. \interp\ Per
unit scale factor the peak was at $z=1$ and per unit cosmic time at $z=1.26$ (Section~\ref{sec:wz_clocks}); the present epoch is singled out by
the e-fold clock only.

\section{Conclusion}
IAM derives a specific dark energy equation of state from horizon thermodynamics with zero free parameters: $w_{\rm info}=-1-1/(3a)$. This
equation of state is always mildly phantom, asymptotes toward $-1$ at late times, and excludes the Big Rip scenario through a built-in
thermodynamic governor (Equation~\eqref{eq:wz_norip}). The activation function $E(a)$ saturates at $e$, bounding the informational contribution to
cosmic expansion and ensuring a final state of informational completion rather than the indefinite dissolution of classical heat death. \interp

The informational maturity framework quantifies where the universe stands in this process: at $a=1$ today, exactly $1/e\approx36.8\,\%$ of the
universe's total informational record has been written, at the inflection point of maximum maturation rate per e-fold. The maturity milestone
table (Table~\ref{tab:de_maturity}) provides a specific, falsifiable timeline derived from first principles. \calc

The IAM prediction maps to $(w_0,w_a)=(-4/3,-1/3)$ in the CPL parametrization, lying outside current DESI DR2 contours and sharing the negative
sign of $w_a$ (trending toward less phantom). Because the DESI fits use light-ruler distances, on which the term predicts $w=-1$, the comparison is
not a test; the correct comparison is the matter-ruler expansion history against the light-ruler history.

Three kinds of measurement will determine whether these predictions are correct: redshift-space growth rates $f\sigma_8(z)$ from DESI, Rubin-LSST
weak lensing growth constraints that probe the $\mu<1$, $\Sigma=1$ signature directly, and the matter-ruler expansion rate from sirens and the
distance ladder (Chapter~\ref{ch:surveys}). If confirmed, one mechanism accounts for the weakening of growth that bears on the $S_8$ comparison
(Chapter~\ref{ch:s8trend}) and for the divergence of the two rulers. \prediction

The comparison this chapter calls for is the matter-ruler expansion history against the light-ruler history, redshift by redshift. A cosmologist with growth and distance data can start it now, and the surveys of this decade will sharpen it.
