\chapter{Reach: the next surfaces}\label{part4:ch:reach}

\section{The order}

After commissioning, the order of work is set:
\begin{enumerate}
\item human blood conditions, blood cancers first;
\item plasma cell-free DNA;
\item tissue specimens;
\item dogs and other mammals;
\item salmonids;
\item ageing, in the same person over time;
\item back to the quantum processors of Part~\ref{part:3}, to check which of its predictions have come due.
\end{enumerate}
Each of items 1--6 needs something chain v3 does not yet have: a floor for another cell type, another platform, a non-blood template, or serial mode.
Public data for these tracks have been collected, with raw array files preferred wherever they exist so that each specimen enters through the chain's own
Stage~1. The list of collected sets is not in the repository and is not quoted here. \openprob

\paragraph{What each new surface needs.} A reading generalises in three directions: the cell type, the platform, and the route by which
the DNA reaches the array. The same cell type may need its own reference on each route: a neutrophil in a whole-blood draw, the same
lineage's DNA in plasma after cell death, and the same cell in a tissue biopsy reach the array through different handling. A reference
measured on one platform does not transfer to another (Chapter~\ref{ch:meta}); whether one transfers between routes on the same
platform is not yet measured. \openprob{} Each combination therefore enters with a reference measured on purified healthy cells through
that route, the entry tests of Chapter~\ref{ch:atlas}, and its own read line. For a combination not yet run, the statement is that it
has not been tested, never that it cannot be read.

\section{Blood cancers}

A leukaemic cell is usually the majority cell in the tube, so the fraction that limits a minor cell does not limit it. The test is per cell: the affected
lineage read against its own healthy floor, the other lineages in the same specimen against theirs, each against 1.00. \prediction{} The affected lineage
reads above Normal; the unaffected lineages do not. Two records set what is needed first. On the neutrophil identity sites, leukaemic blood at diagnosis stayed
in Normal in 6 of 10 patients, because the blasts share those sites. On pre-registered 450K
progenitor floors (four to five donors per type), Met-A did not separate leukaemic CD34$^+$ cells from healthy progenitors reliably: 16 of 29 outside Normal
(bar 80~\%, failed), 13 of those 16 above it. \measured{} Progenitor floors on EPIC with more donors, the C-score on the same arrays, and IAM-A on single-molecule
leukaemia data are the next steps. \openprob

\section{Tumours against the same person's normal tissue}

The single-molecule reading needs no reference group and no composition step when tumour and normal tissue of one person are sequenced the same way. In six
early-onset colorectal cancer patients with a true tumour--normal pair, the tumour's copy error was higher in 6 of 6, by 7--33~\%, median ratio 1.148; one pair's
conversion-failure difference was just over the instrument bar (0.0051 against 0.005), and on the other five the median ratio was 1.183. In oral squamous cell carcinoma
the tumour was higher in 4 of 4 by whole-genome bisulfite sequencing, and in 3 of 4 with 5-hydroxymethylcytosine removed.
\measured{} Not shown: why. Tumour purity is unknown, the tissue mix differs between tumour and normal, and the pairs
come from two laboratories. The reading is the copy-error channel; IAM-A floors for these tissues are not frozen. \openprob

\section{Plasma}

In plasma, cell-free DNA from solid tissues is a much larger share than in whole blood. Two things change. The genome count is of order $10^3$--$10^4$, so the cell-count floor
of Chapter~\ref{ch:sky} becomes part of every interval. And a shed cell has to be found before it is read, which on arrays needs a template for it and on molecules needs
enough of its molecules. On single molecules, a per-molecule threshold found cancer DNA at 1~\% in 0 of 20 constructed mixtures (bar 18 of 20, failed); the total isolated-error count,
with no threshold and not pre-registered, found it in 20 of 20 at 10~\%, 18--20 of 20 at 3~\% and 1--6 of 20 at 1~\%, with 0.5--1.5 million molecules. \measured{} The limit scales as $1/\sqrt N$ in the number of molecules; a deep plasma library would reach lower, which is an
estimate, not a measurement. \calc

\section{Stool}

For colon lining, stool selects the cells that shed into the gut. Against the cells that shed into stool, 29 marker regions (227 CpGs) were found for lower-gut
epithelium, enough to pull its molecules from a deep stool library; whether enough tumour molecules survive in stool is not assessed. \measured{} No stool methylome has been read. \openprob

\section{Tissues}

A tissue profile is a specimen, not a reference: a biopsy is a mixture of cells like a blood draw, and it is separated and read cell by cell.
Chain v3 has no tissue cell types, so it does not read tissue arrays. \openprob{} The test, when it can be run, is that each healthy tissue separates into the cells known
to be in it, and each healthy cell reads Normal against its own floor.

\section{Other mammals}

No purified-cell methylation reference for dogs was found in public data. \observed{} The mammalian methylation array measures conserved CpGs across species~\cite{Arneson2022}, and the first step is
a canine healthy reference from purified canine blood cells on it, with its own identity sites and floor. Then sorted healthy canine cells, held out of the reference, are
read on it. \prediction{} They read 1.00 within tolerance.

\section{Salmonids}

Fish red blood cells are nucleated, so salmonid blood is close to one cell type and needs almost no separation. Four sets have been read on single molecules with the same
statistic (Chapters~\ref{ch:temperature} and~\ref{ch:salmonid}). In each the instrument repeats almost perfectly, and in each the small differences between fish track the library, for example
duplicates in brook charr sperm, conversion failure in Atlantic salmon fin, Methow steelhead and coho smolts. \measured{} A
library-quality term has to be removed before any fish-level difference is read, and the floor at 10~\textdegree C has to be measured on fish, not assumed. \openprob

\section{Ageing in one person}

Individuals drawn repeatedly over two decades are the direct test of whether an elevation in older blood is a slope shared by every lineage or a clone moving one lineage
(Chapter~\ref{ch:leukocyte}). It needs serial mode, a floor on the platform the draws were made on, and intake status for every draw (Chapter~\ref{ch:serial}). \openprob
