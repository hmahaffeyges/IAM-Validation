\chapter{Serial mode: one person, many draws}\label{ch:serial}

\section{The strongest measurement is a difference}

The instrument's value lies in the sequence of tests for one person: a trajectory from draw to draw that shows change or its absence,
and the cleanest sky map that one person's draws can give. The reason is in the measurement. Everything that is constant in a person between two draws, their genotype, their laboratory's pipeline if it is
held fixed, the reference's errors at their composition, cancels in a difference. In CMB language, two draws of one person are two maps of one sky,
and their difference is the split-map null test turned into a signal.

\section{What serial mode will compute}

Serial mode is not yet part of chain v3. \openprob{} Its design is pre-registered (\texttt{doors/\allowbreak{}PROC\_SERIAL\_01\_PREREG.md}), and a module of
pure functions exists (\texttt{chain/\allowbreak{}serial\_mode.py}) that is not wired into the run. Given a draw and an earlier draw of the same person (same
identifier hash, same array type, same pipeline; otherwise the pair is refused), it computes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$\Delta A$} for the cell read in both draws, with its fraction in each;
\item \textbf{the difference sky}, $\Delta\beta_i=\beta_i^{\mathrm{now}}-\beta_i^{\mathrm{prior}}$ at every address. No expectation, no $\sigma$ from any
      population and no composition enter: two measurements of one person and their difference;
\item \textbf{a trajectory} when there are three or more draws: $A$ by date, the sign of the last step, and whether any step exceeded the change floor.
      No fit is made to a trajectory and no population defines its expected shape.
\end{enumerate}
Composition drops out of a difference only in part: if a person's fractions change between draws, the difference sky carries that change. The
out-of-span projection of Chapter~\ref{ch:sky} would remove it exactly, which is why one of its tests is written on serial pairs.

\section{The change floor}

A difference means nothing without the size of difference that noise alone produces. That is set by technical replicates: the same DNA measured twice,
read through the same chain. For neutrophil Met-A it is not yet measured. \openprob{} What the records hold so far:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Same person, repeat arrays, another laboratory.} Isolated neutrophils of two donors, eight times of day, three arrays each, read with no tare:
      within-person SD 0.045 and 0.044 on all 48 arrays (\texttt{doors/\allowbreak{}data/\allowbreak{}t2\_diag.csv}), and the spread followed the arrays' noise index, not the time of day (\texttt{PROC\_NEUT\_TEST\_01\_T2\_OUTCOME.md}).
      \measured{} This is the floor of an untared reading in a noisy laboratory, not of a tared one.
\item \textbf{Technical replicates of whole blood.} A 64-array replicate set (GSE250556) was in the pre-registered battery. The neutrophil fraction there was
      0.30--0.56, and under the 0.50 read line then in force $A$ was withheld on 63 of 64 arrays (\texttt{PROC\_NEUT\_TEST\_01\_OUTCOME.md}, T3). \measured{} At the
      present 0.20 line they are read: in development run 3, with the noise-corrected tare, 63 of the 64 arrays carry a tared reading, 0.967--1.026,
      within-person SD 0.008, all 63 in Normal (Figure~\ref{fig:p4_replicates}; \texttt{DEV\_CHAIN\_V3\_RUN3.md}). \measured{} This is one
      development run in one laboratory; a tared change floor from same-chip replicates through the commissioned chain is still to be measured. \openprob
\item \textbf{Same person, two remission draws.} Ten patients with two blood draws in remission read within 0.05 of themselves on 10 of 10
      (\texttt{PROC\_AML\_SERIAL\_01\_OUTCOME.md}, bar S5). \measured
\end{itemize}

collects them.

\section{Which serial data the chain can read}

The longest public serial set, individuals drawn up to five times over two decades (E-MTAB-7309)~\cite{Wang2018SATSA}, has not been read through
chain v3. Its input quality is the first obstacle: of 1,056 arrays calibrated by Stage~1, 738 fall below the 0.93 intake line, median call rate 0.894,
and the call rate falls with the draw's age decade (\texttt{doors/\allowbreak{}PLAN.md}). \measured{} A trajectory built from such draws must carry the intake status of
every draw, and a quarantined draw is not a point on it. \openprob

\section{Why serial mode also makes the reference better}

A person measured repeatedly is their own reference at every address. With enough draws, the per-address spread of one person between draws is a noise map made
with no population at all, and differences from it are that person's changes. The same draws, pooled over many people, would say which addresses are stable in
every person and which move: information the purified-cell reference does not carry. Neither use requires comparing one person with another.
