\chapter{The chain of custody}\label{ch:chain}

\section{What the chain is}

The Cellular Performance Gauge is a chain of stages that takes a specimen's raw array files and returns a reading on the one gauge of
Chapter~\ref{ch:gauge}. Chain v3 is a development build, not commissioned and not a diagnostic test. Its scope is one cell type, neutrophils,
on EPIC v1 arrays; other cell types are added one at a time after each passes its tests. The entry point is
\texttt{chain/\allowbreak{}MethylPhys\_Interface/\allowbreak{}run\_sample.py --engine v3}; the stages after calibration are in \texttt{chain/\allowbreak{}conductor\_v3.py}. The
procedure is \texttt{sop/\allowbreak{}MethylPhys\_CPG\_SOP\_v3.md} and the operator chapter \texttt{manual/\allowbreak{}OM\_v3\_neutrophil\_chain.md}. Where a document and the code
differ, the code is what runs. This chapter walks through the stages (Figure~\ref{fig:chain}).

\section{Stage 0: intake}

The IDAT pair is opened and the array's own numbers are read: manifest, integrity hash, control probes, per-probe detection, call rate and sex check.
Below a call rate of 0.93 the specimen is quarantined and no reading is written; between 0.93 and 0.98 it proceeds with a flag. A sex mismatch, a failed
integrity check or a deferred detection or call-rate check also quarantines (Chapter~\ref{ch:instrument}). A cleartext identifier is hashed before the
engine sees it. Input that arrives as $\beta$ values without the raw files can be read, but the bundle then carries \texttt{intake\_verified: False}.

\section{Stage 1: calibration}

Noob background and dye-bias correction and probe-type normalisation, per array, produce $\beta$ at every probe. Probes indistinguishable from background are
removed before any later stage sees a $\beta$. Stage~1 returns, beside $\beta$, the array's detection numbers and control intensities.

\section{Platform check}

The chain reads EPIC v1 only. A specimen whose array type, probe names or probe count say 450K or EPIC v2 is refused with one sentence, because no frozen
neutrophil floor exists for that platform.

\section{Stage A: composition (whole blood)}

Eight blood groups are solved by non-negative least squares on 963 markers, none of them a neutrophil identity site (Chapter~\ref{ch:separation}). Fewer than
867 measured markers: no composition and no reading. Isolated neutrophils skip this stage.

\section{Stage M: Met-A}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Isolated neutrophils:} the mean $H(\beta)$ over the 6,000 identity sites divided by the floor, 0.330263 bits (Eq.~\ref{eq:meta}).
\item \textbf{Whole blood:} read when neutrophils are at least 20~\% of the specimen; the mean $H(\beta)$ over the sites divided by the mean $H(e)$ of the specimen's
      own healthy expectation (Eq.~\ref{eq:metawb}). Below 20~\% the fraction is printed and $A$ is withheld.
\end{itemize}
Either way at least 90~\% of the sites must be measured. Stage~M also records the noise index $N$, the shift a 1~\% loss of the neutrophil pattern would cause in
this specimen, and the methylated sites' mean $\beta$ with a flag when it is below one half, past the entropy ceiling.

\section{Stage MC: the Met-A C-score}

The residual map over the identity sites, in genome order, is reduced to the C-score: the variance of 50-site block means over the variance of the sites, divided
by the healthy baseline 1.1104 (Chapter~\ref{ch:cscore}). It is printed with every reading, with no band. The IAM-A C-score is defined and not yet built. \openprob

\section{Stage T: the tare}

The reading is divided by the median of at least three healthy references of the same specimen type run the same way, on the same slide, else in the same batch;
with at least 20 references carrying $A$, $f_{\rm neu}$ and $N$, by the noise-corrected expectation instead (Chapter~\ref{ch:instrument}). The result is
$A_{\rm rel}$, and the gauge state is printed from $A_{\rm rel}$ only: \emph{Normal} (0.95--1.05), \emph{above Normal} or \emph{below Normal}. Every tared reading
carries its detection limit: twice the references' spread over the shift per 1~\% loss, the smallest loss of the neutrophil pattern this specimen could have shown.
Without references the reading is printed as \emph{untared}, with no state. A batch is run in two passes: the first reads every specimen, the second re-reads each
with the others of its batch as references (\texttt{chain\_tests/\allowbreak{}run\_chain\_acceptance.py}).

\section{Stage Q: IAM-A}

Single-molecule input, a read-level file of the one pipeline for which a neutrophil position is frozen (\texttt{loyfer\_pat\_v1}), is read as IAM-A,
Eq.~\eqref{eq:iama}, with at least 100,000 opportunities and a reading on each half of the molecules as a repeat check (\texttt{stage\_q\_iam\_a.py}). A table from
any other pipeline is refused until a position has been measured on healthy cells with that pipeline.

\section{What the chain withholds}

Every bundle lists what the build does not print: tier lines beyond Normal, which are not yet measured on this scale, and every cell type other than neutrophils.
Composition-based detection of cells outside the eight groups, the whole-array sky, serial mode and the out-of-span map are not part of chain v3. \openprob

\section{The record of a run}

Each run writes one HTML page, a JSON bundle with every stage's output and the versions of the floor and reference files, and one row appended to an evidence
ledger (Chapter~\ref{ch:report}). Each run takes its number from the ledger. The v3 bundle does not yet record the chain's commit or a checksum of each
frozen file it read, and the v3 procedure and operator chapter are written by hand, not generated from the code. \openprob
