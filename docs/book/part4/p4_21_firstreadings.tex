\chapter{First readings on chain v3}\label{ch:firstreadings}

\begin{keybox}[title=Status of everything in this chapter]
These readings come from chain v3, a development build, before any cell type is commissioned. They show what the instrument does on real arrays and
single molecules. They are not evidence about any person or condition, and no conclusion in this book rests on them. Every number is copied from the
record named beside it.
\end{keybox}

\section{End to end: IDATs in, report out}

The acceptance run passed 22 IDAT pairs through \texttt{run\_sample.py --engine v3} with no hand step: Stage~0, Stage~1, Stage~A, Stage~M, Stage~MC, then a second
pass with Stage~T against the other specimens of the batch (\texttt{chain\_tests/\allowbreak{}CHAIN\_V3\_ACCEPTANCE\_RUN3.md}, \texttt{chain\_acceptance.csv}).

\measured{} Three cautions go with the table. The six isolated arrays are the reference arrays themselves, so 6 of 6 is not independent; their held-out reading is
0.983--1.045 (Chapter~\ref{ch:identity}). The mixtures were made in the reference laboratory from cells of the same series the composition profiles come from
(Chapter~\ref{ch:atlas}), and they have no donor, so intake was not run on them. And the run used a read line of 0.50 neutrophils: it withheld five remission
bloods at 0.06--0.47. At the present 0.20 line four of them (0.28--0.47) would be read; they have not been re-run. \openprob{} The untared offset is the reason for the
tare: the reference laboratory's mixtures read low and the other laboratory's bloods high, and the same-batch tare brought both into Normal.

\section{A second laboratory, and what the reading was reading}

A pre-registered battery read 692 arrays (\texttt{doors/\allowbreak{}PROC\_NEUT\_TEST\_01\_OUTCOME.md}). On six healthy whole bloods with flow-cytometry counts the composition
matched the counts (T1a, T1b), and untared $A$ fell in the predicted 0.93--0.98 on 2 of 3 readable specimens (T1c, failed). Isolated neutrophils from another laboratory
failed every bar for array noise (T2; Chapter~\ref{ch:instrument}). On 570 whole bloods of one infection study, tared healthy specimens spread with SD 0.052, wider than
Normal, and untared $A$ sat around 1.22 (T4). \measured

The development analysis that followed asked what the whole-blood reading was reading (\texttt{DEV\_NOISE\_02\_OUTCOME.md}). Among 76 healthy adults of that study, untared $A$
was explained by the neutrophil fraction and the noise index with $R^2=0.85$; only 33 of 495 arrays had a noise index inside the reference arrays' range. Divided by the
expectation for its own fraction and noise index, fitted on the other healthy arrays (leave-one-out), the readings were these. \measured

On this gauge, severe infection did not move the neutrophils' identity pattern ($p=0.81$ for above Normal, severe against healthy). That does not say the neutrophils are

\section{A known cause on arrays and on single molecules}

The most direct test of the gauge is a known cause with a known direction. A DNA-methyltransferase inhibitor blocks the enzyme that copies methylation at division.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Arrays} (\texttt{PROC\_DNMT\_01\_PARTA\_OUTCOME.md}; 51 EPIC arrays, three leukaemia cell lines, each read against its own vehicle arrays). Vehicle arrays read
      0.968--1.048; an inactive analogue at 10~$\mu$M 1.002--1.032; the active drug at 3.2--16~nM 1.001--1.028, and at $\ge80$~nM 1.16--1.85. A second active compound read
      1.73--1.81 in all three lines. The change sits on the methylated channel (median $+1.56$ against $+0.017$ on the unmethylated one). \measured
\item \textbf{The ceiling.} Between 16 and 80~nM the methylated sites fall from $\beta\approx0.94$ to 0.57--0.89, and by 400~nM to 0.36--0.60. Past $\beta=\tfrac12$ the methylated
      channel's reading stops rising (observed 2.66--2.85, against a ceiling of about 2.8) and then falls. Met-A is monotone only while those sites stay above one half, which is why
      the chain prints their mean $\beta$ and flags the ceiling. \measured
\item \textbf{Single molecules} (\texttt{PROC\_DNMT\_01\_PARTB\_OUTCOME.md}; EM-seq, one leukaemia line, 100~nM for 7 days, four genotypes in duplicate). Copy error on the vehicle
      libraries 0.0209--0.0217; treated 0.0405--0.0511; IAM-A against the same genotype's vehicle libraries 1.65--1.97, above 1.05 in 8 of 8, with conversion failure unchanged
      (largest difference 0.0007). \measured
\end{itemize}
Both readings see a known loss of copying fidelity, in the right direction, from the right channel, and not when an inactive look-alike is given. These are cancer cell lines in one
laboratory each, and the drug also slows proliferation. They show the gauge reads a known change; they are not a disease reading.

\section{Leukaemic blood}

Ten patients' blood was read at diagnosis and twice in remission on the neutrophil sites (\texttt{PROC\_AML\_SERIAL\_01\_OUTCOME.md}). Diagnosis blood read outside Normal in 4 of 10
(bar 8 of 10, failed); remission blood read Normal in 8 of 10 and 10 of 10; the same person's two remission draws agreed within 0.05 in 10 of 10. \measured{} The diagnosis explains the
failure: the identity sites are held by every mature leukocyte, so blasts share them, and diagnosis blood is blast-dominated; reading it against the neutrophil floor is not the gauge's
definition (Chapter~\ref{ch:identity}). The next steps written in the record are a reading of the dominant cell against its own floor, a healthy C-score line from healthy whole blood, and
the blast fraction printed beside $A$. \openprob

\section{Development readings outside the chain's platform}

Two pre-registered procedures read neutrophils on 450K arrays against a development 450K floor from purified 450K neutrophils; chain v3 does not read 450K.
In 246 held-out controls 67.9~\% read Normal, and the reading split by sex (women 0.961, men 1.007) (\texttt{PROC\_PREDX\_NEUT\_01\_OUTCOME.md}). The purified neutrophils of the
floor differed by sex by only 0.008, and a second set read against controls on the same slide removed the split: 92.9~\% of 170 controls in Normal
(\texttt{PROC\_PREDX\_SLIDE\_01\_OUTCOME.md}). \measured{} The sex gap was a slide effect, and the same-slide tare is what made the reading usable on those arrays. Neither procedure showed a
per-person pre-diagnostic signal.

\section{What these readings are for}

They show four things about the instrument, and nothing yet about people. Chain v3 runs end to end on raw arrays with no population-derived number. Untared readings carry a
laboratory and noise offset that a same-run tare removes. The reading follows a known physical cause, on arrays and on molecules, and stops at the entropy ceiling where the physics says
it must. And the neutrophil's identity sites, chosen to be steady, are blind to a cell that shares them, which is a statement about the sites, not about the cells.
