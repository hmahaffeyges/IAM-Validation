DRAFT - Two readings of one record (for the opening chapter of Part VI; not in the book)

A cell holds its identity as a methylation pattern. The pattern is a record, and the cell copies it at every division. The record can be read at two scales: on single molecules, and across the many cells of a specimen. The instrument reads it both ways.

IAM-A: copy errors on single molecules. Take one molecule from a long read. Where the molecule is methylated along its length, an unmethylated call standing alone between methylated neighbours is a copy error: the copy did not carry the mark across. Count the errors and the places where one could have happened:

    eps = errors / opportunities                                   [measured]

The floor is eps0 = 0.032, set by the energy that holds a mark at 310 K.   [derived]
The reading is

    IAM-A = H(eps) / (P_cell * H(eps0))

where H is the binary entropy and P_cell is the healthy position of that cell type on one read-level pipeline.   [calibrated]
This is the cell's error per gate, read against a thermal floor.

Met-A: the pattern across the ensemble. An array does not see molecules. It sees beta, the fraction of copies methylated at each site, over all the cells in the specimen. Met-A is the Shannon entropy of beta at the cell's identity sites, over the healthy floor of that cell type on the same platform. The floor comes from purified healthy cells.   [calibrated]
This is the cell's state fidelity, read across the ensemble.

One record, two scales. The two readings are of one record. Copy errors are what blur the pattern: an error moves the beta of its site toward one half, where H(beta) is largest.   [derived]
So IAM-A reads the cause on single molecules and Met-A reads its result across the specimen. Each A score carries one C-score beside it, computed from the same sites, which says where along the genome the departures sit.   [calc]

The efficiency side. The Mahaffey number is the drive over the thermal quantum, M = dG_ATP / kT. For one ATP at 310 K it is about 21 (M = 20.94).   [calc]
It is the efficiency side of the same cell, and it sets eps0 through the holding energy.   [derived]

Two kinds of method. Most methods that read methylation for disease are classifiers: machine-learning models trained on large groups of people with and without disease. Ageing clocks are regressions trained on large groups of people of known age. Both ask which group a specimen looks like. The readings here ask a different question: how far is this cell from its own healthy record, measured against a physical floor? One compares a specimen with other people; the other measures one cell. They are different methods, and nothing here says which detects disease better. Detection is not tested.   [openprob]

Both readings are open to anyone with a long-read run or an array. Take a healthy cell, read it both ways, and try to break it.
