\chapter{Glossary}\label{app:glossary}
This glossary lists the technical terms, named quantities, data sets and acronyms used in Parts~\ref{part:1}--\ref{part:5}, in cosmology, quantum
physics, quantum computing, semiconductors, methylation and statistics, with one or two plain sentences each and the chapters where the term is
used or defined. Where the canon file (\texttt{CANON/\allowbreak{}iam\_canon.json}, reproduced in Appendix~\ref{app:canon}) defines a term, the entry
follows the canon. Symbols, units and constants are in Appendix~\ref{app:notation}; every displayed equation is in Appendix~\ref{app:formulas}.
Status labels are defined in the front matter and listed here under their names. Entries are alphabetical by their first word; a symbol is
filed under the name of its letter.

\textbf{1/$f$ noise} & Noise with power inversely proportional to frequency, from an ensemble of fluctuators; measures $\tTLS$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:xqp})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{5mC, 5hmC} & 5-methylcytosine, the methyl mark, and 5-hydroxymethylcytosine, its first oxidation product; bisulfite reads both as methylated, oxidative bisulfite separates them (the methylome's E/B separation). {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:floorbreach}, \ref{ch:iama}, \ref{ch:translation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{6dFGS, SDSS MGS} & Low-redshift galaxy surveys providing $f\sigma_8$ points at $z=0.067$ and 0.15. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:dual}, \ref{ch:latetime}, \ref{ch:level2})}\\[1pt]
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\normalsize\textbf{A}}\\[2pt]
\textbf{$A$ (informational fidelity ratio)} & A reading divided by the same system's healthy reading (the healthy reference), measured on the same instrument. Dimensionless; $A=1$ is the system working as itself, in the middle of the gauge; above 1 is more error than healthy; the floor $\Hmin$ lies below 1 and nothing reads below it. Cells: Met-A and IAM-A; qubits: $\varepsilon$ over the device as built; chips: $E_{\rm sw}$ over the chip as built. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:gauge}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{$A_{\rm rel}$ (tared reading)} & A Met-A reading divided by the median reading of at least three healthy references run the same way (same slide, else same batch). The gauge state is printed on $A_{\rm rel}$ only; without references a reading is reported as untared. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:instrument}, \ref{ch:separation}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Acoustic scale ($\theta_s$)} & The angle subtended by the sound horizon at recombination, read from the CMB peak spacing. A photon-ruler quantity; with $\Sigma=1$ it is that of $\Lambda$CDM, and it bounds any photon coupling to $\beta_\gamma<0.0052$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:theory}, \ref{ch:dual}, \ref{ch:dsvalidation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Across-array SD} & The standard deviation of $\beta$ at a site across the physical reference arrays; the identity-site rule requires at most 0.05. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:identity})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{ACT, WMAP} & CMB experiments (the Atacama Cosmology Telescope and the Wilkinson Microwave Anisotropy Probe); combined with SDSS and supernovae they give $\mu_0=0.02\pm0.19$ independently of Planck. \observed {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:entropicgravity}, \ref{ch:latetime}, \ref{ch:surveys})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Activated process} & A rate proportional to $e^{-E_a/\kB T}$. Its local logarithmic slope $E_a/\kB T$ changes with temperature, so a power law in $T$ describes it only over a window. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:thermaln})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Activation energy $E_a$} & The energy barrier of a thermally activated process; its local logarithmic slope is $E_a/\kB T$. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:thermaln})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Activation function $E(a)$} & $E(a)=\exp(1-1/a)=e^{-z}$, the accumulated record term of the cosmic horizon normalized to $E(1)=1$. It vanishes before structure forms, grows monotonically because no payment is returned, has its inflection at $a=1/2$, and tends to $e$. Derived from the record-density constraint $\dot\rho_{\rm info}=\rho_{\rm info}H/a$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:virial}, \ref{ch:level2}, \ref{ch:darkenergy})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{AdS/CFT} & The correspondence between gravity in anti-de Sitter space and a conformal field theory on its boundary. Cited as the setting in which a fixed-charge sector reduces to an orbit; in de Sitter space the reduction is open. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:bhinformation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Affine parameter} & A parameter $\lambda$ along a null geodesic in which the geodesic equation takes its simplest form; in Jacobson's construction it measures distance along the generators of the local horizon, zero at the surface element. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:bekenstein}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Affleck--Dine mechanism} & A baryogenesis model in which a scalar condensate carrying baryon number decays; like other mechanisms it leaves the size of the asymmetry set by its own parameters. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:baryon})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Aging clock} & A model that places a person on an age curve built from many people; set aside for the instrument, whose measure of aging is one person's own earlier readings. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:reach})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Alcubierre metric} & A solution of general relativity in which a flat region is carried by reshaping the geometry around it. It requires negative energy density in the wall, violating the weak energy condition. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:exploratory})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{$A_{\max}$ (full surface)} & The far end of a cell gauge: the reading when every identity site sits at a coin flip, one bit over the healthy reference. For neutrophils 3.03 on Met-A and 4.45 on IAM-A, both computed from the healthy reference alone. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:floorbreach}, \ref{ch:gauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{AMD Ryzen 9 9950X} & A desktop processor; the worked example of the chip gauge and the baseline of prediction S2. Its power and clock are the maker's specification; no maker-published transistor count exists, so its reading is the range 576--593 times $\kB T_j\ln2$ set by the two die-level counts, $(20.0\text{--}20.6)\times10^9$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:cmos}, \ref{ch:landauer}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{AML (acute myeloid leukemia)} & A cancer of myeloid progenitors. The leukemic cell is usually the majority cell, so the read line does not limit it. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:identity}, \ref{part4:ch:reach})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{AMPS argument, firewall} & The argument of Almheiri, Marolf, Polchinski and Sully that late Hawking radiation carrying information would violate the monogamy of entanglement, and that a firewall at the horizon might restore unitarity. With no independent interior degrees of freedom, IAM's reading needs no firewall. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:bhinformation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Analogy (status label)} & ANALOGY: a correspondence between two domains that guides a method but is not a derivation. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:entanglement}, \ref{ch:meta}, \ref{ch:translation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Apparent horizon} & The surface of radius $\tilde r_A=c/H$ in a flat Friedmann universe on which Cai and Kim apply the first law $-dE=T\,dS$; its area is $A_H=4\pi c^2/H^2$ and its temperature the Gibbons--Hawking value. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Architecture position $P_{\rm cell}$} & The healthy cell type's position above the IAM-A floor, $P_{\rm cell}=H(\varepsilon_{\rm healthy})/H(\varepsilon_0)$, measured once on healthy cells with one read-level pipeline and frozen. Neutrophils: $1.099$ (three donors, 1.084--1.108) on \texttt{loyfer\_pat\_v1}; any other pipeline needs its own $P$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:saturation}, \ref{ch:iama}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Array (methylation array)} & A bisulfite microarray (Illumina 450K, EPIC v1, EPIC v2) that reports two fluorescence intensities per CpG probe, from which $\beta$ is computed. The chain reads EPIC v1 only; 450K and EPIC v2 are refused until each has its own healthy reference. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:landauer}, \ref{ch:meta}, \ref{ch:instrument})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Arrow of time} & The direction in which irreversible records accumulate. In IAM's reading it has a physical carrier, $E(a)$, which only increases because each record is irreversible. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:theoryinterp}, \ref{ch:time}, \ref{ch:virial_decoherence})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Assumptions 1--3} & The premises of the cosmological result: (1) gravity is a thermodynamic equation of state; (2) records written in the bulk contribute to the entropy of the nearest horizon (the one identification); (3) the virial half holds for the whole ensemble of bound structure, $\beta_m=\Omega_m/2$. Chapter~\ref{ch:theory} adds (4) $E(a)$ exact and (5) the term acts on perturbations only. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:bekenstein}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Astro-Genetics} & The name used here for reading the methylome as cosmology reads the sky: calibration, component separation, residual maps and null tests carried from CMB analysis to methylation data. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:bridge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Asymptotic expansion rates} & The rates the two sectors settle at as $a\to\infty$: for the photon-sector $H_0=67.16$, $H_0\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}=55.57$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ for light and $H_0\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda+\beta_me}=70.86$ for the matter flow (55.78 and 71.12 with the Planck 2018 base values $H_0=67.4$, $\Omega_m=0.315$, with $\beta_m=0.15765$ fixed). \calc {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:statusall})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Atlas (cell atlas)} & The set of healthy cell methylation profiles into which a specimen is separated and on which each cell's identity loci are chosen. Atlas v2 is one hierarchical Bayesian fit of 74 cells at about 814,000 CpGs, on the chain's own Stage~1 scale, with a posterior at every value and nothing filled where a cell was not measured. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:giants}, \ref{ch:translation}, \ref{ch:atlas})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{ATP (adenosine triphosphate)} & The cell's energy currency. Its cytosolic free energy of hydrolysis, $\dGATP=54$~kJ\,mol$^{-1}$, sets the cell's Mahaffey number $\mathcal M=20.94$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:landauer}, \ref{ch:floorbreach})}\\[1pt]
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\normalsize\textbf{B}}\\[2pt]
\textbf{Background (cosmological)} & The homogeneous expansion history $H(a)$ from the Friedmann equation. In IAM it does not change and the term lives in the entropy functional; placed in the background instead (Level~2b), the same term moves $H_0$ to $61.5$, $10.9\sigma$ from Planck in units of Planck's error, about $9\sigma$ ($8.6\sigma$ with both errors). {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:dual}, \ref{ch:level2})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{BAO (baryon acoustic oscillations)} & The sound-horizon scale imprinted in the clustering of matter. Its angular and radial positions are measured with light and so belong to the photon ruler; they are those of $\Lambda$CDM when $\Sigma=1$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:latetime}, \ref{ch:s8trend}, \ref{ch:sectortension})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Barrow entropy} & A deformation of the horizon area law, $S=(A/A_0)^{1+\Delta/2}$. It changes the background and adds a parameter; contrasted with IAM, which keeps the area law and adds a source. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:theory}, \ref{ch:entropicgravity})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Baryon asymmetry $\eta$} & The surplus of baryons over antibaryons per photon, $\eta=(n_b-n_{\bar b})/n_\gamma\simeq6.1\times10^{-10}$, measured from light-element abundances and from the CMB peaks. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:baryon}, \ref{ch:higgsrecord})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bayesian evidence} & The marginal likelihood of a model; proposed for asking which tissue of origin explains one specimen, as model comparison rather than a classifier. Not built. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:skytools})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{BCS theory} & The Bardeen--Cooper--Schrieffer theory of superconductivity: electrons bind into Cooper pairs across an energy gap $\Delta$; it supplies the density of states used for quasiparticles. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:scprimer})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bead count} & The number of beads behind a probe's reading; probes with fewer than three beads do not count toward the call rate. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:instrument})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bekenstein bound} & The maximum entropy a region of given size and energy can hold. A black hole saturates it. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:floorbreach}, \ref{ch:synthesis})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bekenstein--Hawking coefficient $1/4$} & The $1/4$ in $S=\kB A/4\lP^2$: the $2\pi$ period of the Euclidean horizon over the $8\pi$ of the field equations; given $G$, nothing else enters. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:bekenstein}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bekenstein--Hawking entropy} & The entropy of a horizon, $S=\kB c^3A/4G\hbar=\kB A/4\lP^2$: one unit of $\kB$ per $4\lP^2$ of area, one bit per $4\ln2\,\lP^2$. The $1/4$ is the ratio $2\pi/8\pi$ of the Euclidean period to the factor in the field equations. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:theory}, \ref{ch:dsnote})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bell inequality, CHSH} & A bound $|S|\le2$ on correlations obeyed by any local hidden-variable theory (CHSH form). Quantum mechanics reaches the Tsirelson bound $2\sqrt2$. Loophole-free violations were reported in 2015. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:quantumrecords}, \ref{ch:entanglement}, \ref{ch:nonlocal})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bennett's result} & Acquiring a record has no minimum dissipation; the Landauer cost $\kB T\ln2$ per bit is paid when a record is erased or an apparatus reset. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:measurement}, \ref{ch:open})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{$\beta$ (methylated fraction)} & The fraction of the DNA copies at one CpG that carry a methyl group, a probability in $[0,1]$. Near 1 or 0 at a site a cell holds; $\beta=\tfrac12$ is a coin flip. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:dsvalidation}, \ref{ch:bridge}, \ref{ch:surface})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{$\beta_\gamma$} & A separate coupling of the record term to photons. The CMB acoustic scale bounds it at $\beta_\gamma<0.0052$ (95\,\%), under 3.3\,\% of $\beta_m$; the dual-sector model predicts zero. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:theory}, \ref{ch:dual}, \ref{ch:dsvalidation})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{$\beta_m$ (matter-sector coupling)} & The coupling of the record term, $\beta_m=\Omega_m/2=0.15765$ with Planck~2018 $\Omega_m=0.3153$: the virial half of bound matter. Fixed in every chain and never sampled. Written 0.1575 where $\Omega_m=0.315$ is used. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:virial_law}, \ref{ch:virial})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{BGS, LRG, ELG, QSO (DESI tracers)} & The galaxy and quasar samples DESI measures: the Bright Galaxy Survey, luminous red galaxies, emission-line galaxies and quasars, each covering its own redshift range (the BGS at $z=0.295$). {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:latetime}, \ref{ch:sectortension}, \ref{ch:quantumrecords})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bianchi identity} & $\nabla^aG_{ab}=0$; with conserved matter it fixes the trace term in Jacobson's derivation and is respected by the record term. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:bekenstein}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Big Rip} & A future singularity in which a phantom density grows without bound. The record term avoids it: $dw_{\rm info}/da=1/(3a^2)>0$ and $\rho_{\rm info}\le e\,\rho_{\rm info}(1)$, so $w\to-1$ from below. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:darkenergy}, \ref{ch:wzfuture}, \ref{ch:statusall})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Binary entropy $H(x)$} & $H(x)=-x\log_2x-(1-x)\log_2(1-x)$ in bits: 0 when every copy agrees, 1 bit at $x=\tfrac12$. Symmetric, $H(x)=H(1-x)$, and concave. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:giants}, \ref{ch:surface}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bisognano--Wichmann theorem} & The result of algebraic quantum field theory that the vacuum restricted to a Rindler wedge is thermal with respect to boosts; equivalently, regularity at the Euclidean Rindler cone tip requires angular period $2\pi$. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:bekenstein})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bispectrum} & The three-point statistic of a field. With the IAM friction its shape kernel $F_2$ is that of general relativity and its amplitude follows $\sigma_8(z)$. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bisulfite conversion} & The chemical step that turns unmethylated cytosine into uracil so that methylation can be read. Incomplete conversion reads as methylation; it dominates isolated calls on the unmethylated channel, which IAM-A therefore does not use. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:gauge}, \ref{ch:iama}, \ref{ch:chain})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bit, nat} & The units of information: one bit is the choice between two equally likely states, one nat is $1/\ln2$ bits. One irreversible bit costs at least $\kB T\ln2$ (Landauer's principle); a horizon holds one nat per $4\lP^2$ of area. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:bekenstein}, \ref{ch:koide}, \ref{ch:surface})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Black hole} & A region bounded by an event horizon. A Schwarzschild black hole of mass $M$ has $T_{\rm BH}=\hbar c^3/8\pi Gk_BM$ and $S_{\rm BH}=4\pi Gk_BM^2/\hbar c$; its horizon is the place in IAM where a concentrated collapse writes its records. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:blackholes})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Black-body approximation} & Treating a black hole as a thermal emitter of area $A$ at $T_{\rm BH}$, $P=\sigma_{\rm SB}AT_{\rm BH}^4$; graybody factors and particle species change the coefficient, not the scaling. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:bhinformation})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Black-hole information paradox} & The problem that thermal Hawking radiation would destroy the information that fell in, violating unitarity. It changes character when information is placed on the horizon surface rather than in the interior. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:bhinformation})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Blast (leukemic blast)} & An immature leukemic cell. It lies outside Stage~A's eight blood groups, has no template, and so is not detected directly: its DNA is spread over the eight and only the fit's residual signals it. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:separation}, \ref{ch:identity}, \ref{part4:ch:reach})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Blinding} & Fixing an analysis before the labels or the answer are seen. Reserved in the chain for commissioning and evidence runs. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:skytools})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Block mean} & The average of the residual over 50 consecutive identity sites; if departures are scattered at random block means vary as $1/\sqrt{50}$ of the site values, if they cluster they vary more. The basis of the C-score. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:cscore})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bohmian mechanics} & A deterministic interpretation of quantum mechanics with particle positions guided by the wave function; one of the answers to the measurement problem. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:measurement})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Boltzmann factor} & The relative probability $e^{-E/\kB T}$ of a state of energy $E$. Two-state form: the floor $\varepsilon_0=1/(1+e^{E_{\rm hold}/\kB T})$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:giants}, \ref{ch:xqp}, \ref{ch:iama})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Boltzmann solver} & A code that evolves the cosmological perturbation equations to give CMB and matter spectra (CAMB, MGCAMB); the Level~2 term is written into the solver's equations rather than imposed as a function. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:latetime}, \ref{ch:level2})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Boost Killing vector} & The approximate symmetry generator $\chi^a=-\kappa\lambda k^a$ of the local horizon near a point in Jacobson's construction; the heat flux across the horizon is measured along it. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:bekenstein})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bootstrap} & Resampling with replacement to estimate an interval; used, over cells, for the cross-platform Deming slopes of atlas v2. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:lensdyn}, \ref{ch:atlas})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bottom-up and top-down exponents} & Two routes to the structure exponent $n$: bottom-up, from IAM's Law, the virial partition, the measured $\sigma(M)$ and halo mass functions, which runs from about $5.5$ at $z=9$ to about $2$ at $z=1$ and averages close to $4$; top-down, the matter-era requirement $n=7/2$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:virial}, \ref{ch:quantumrecords})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Boundary capacity} & The assumed number of bits, $\kB T/E_G$, that the boundary of a superposition can hold; it enters $\tau_{\rm IAM}$ and is listed as an open assumption still needing derivation. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:entanglement}, \ref{ch:measurement}, \ref{ch:gravdec})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Breach} & The condition in which records are produced or lost faster than the surface can maintain them. On a cell gauge breach and the cancer region lie to the right of Normal and are still to be measured from per-person data. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:saturation}, \ref{ch:gauge})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bulk flow} & The coherent motion of galaxies in a large volume, scaling with $f\sigma_8$; measured flows are far larger than the predicted four percent change, so they do not test the term at present precision. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:surveys})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bullet Cluster} & A merging cluster whose lensing mass is offset from its gas; one of the successes of particle dark matter any alternative must face. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:time})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Bundle (run bundle)} & The JSON file each chain run writes beside its report: every stage's output and the versions of the floor and reference files read, with the integrity hash of the IDAT pair in the intake record. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:chain}, \ref{ch:report})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Burn-in} & The initial part of a Markov chain discarded before computing posterior quantities; 30\,\% in the book's extractions. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:dual}, \ref{ch:latetime}, \ref{ch:baryon_chain})}\\[1pt]
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\normalsize\textbf{C}}\\[2pt]
\textbf{Cai--Kim derivation} & Cai and Kim (2005): applying $-dE=T_H\,dS$ at the apparent horizon of a Friedmann universe with the Gibbons--Hawking temperature and area entropy returns the Friedmann equations. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Calculated (status label)} & CALCULATED: a number computed from a derived formula and published constants or measurements, recomputed for this edition. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:s8trend}, \ref{ch:measurement}, \ref{ch:conclusion})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Calibrated (status label)} & CALIBRATED: a value set once from healthy or reference data for one system and then held fixed; the data are named. Examples: the Met-A healthy reference, the C-score healthy baseline. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:meta}, \ref{ch:cscore}, \ref{ch:measurement})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{CAMB} & The Boltzmann code that computes CMB and matter power spectra. Level~2 modified its matter perturbations (\texttt{adotoa\_matter}) to carry the matter-sector rate. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:level2}, \ref{ch:dsvalidation}, \ref{ch:threeway})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Cancer region} & The part of a cell gauge beyond breach, on the high side of Normal; to be placed from per-person data (tumor/normal pairs, the DNMT1-inhibitor series, blood cancers). {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:saturation}, \ref{ch:gauge}, \ref{ch:reach})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Catelani rate} & The qubit relaxation rate from quasiparticle tunneling, $\Gamma_1^{\rm qp}=\xqp(\omega_q/\pi)\sqrt{2\Delta/\hbar\omega_q}$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:scprimer}, \ref{ch:xqp})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{$C(d)$ (genome-distance correlation)} & The correlation of methylation between two CpGs $d$ bases apart within one specimen, the methylome's counterpart of the angular power spectrum: 0.97 at 10--18~bp, halving just below 1~kb, 0.32 at 1--1.8~kb and 0.05 at 3--6~kb on six healthy neutrophil arrays. \measured {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:skytools})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Cell-count floor} & The binomial spread $\sqrt{\beta(1-\beta)/2N}$ of a measured fraction from $N$ diploid genome copies, the methylome's counterpart of cosmic variance; negligible in whole blood, part of every interval in plasma. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:translation}, \ref{ch:sky}, \ref{part4:ch:reach})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Cell-free DNA (cfDNA)} & DNA fragments circulating in plasma, shed by cells across the body; about $10^4$ genome equivalents in a milliliter-scale draw. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:skytools}, \ref{ch:sky}, \ref{part4:ch:reach})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Cellular margin} & The ratio of the chemical cost of one methyl mark to the Landauer floor of one bit, $\gtrsim\mathcal M/\ln2\approx30$. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:landauer})}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Cellular Performance Gauge} & The chain that reads cells' methylation (MethylPhys CPG): from a specimen's raw array files to a reading on the one gauge. Chain v3 is a development build reading neutrophils on EPIC v1; it is not commissioned and not a diagnostic test. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:ledgers}, \ref{ch:chain})}\\[1pt]
