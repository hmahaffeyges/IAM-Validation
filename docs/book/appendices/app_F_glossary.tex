\chapter{Glossary}\label{app:glossary}
This glossary lists the technical terms, named quantities, data sets and acronyms used in Parts~\ref{part:1}--\ref{part:5}, in cosmology, quantum
physics, quantum computing, semiconductors, methylation and statistics, with one or two plain sentences each and the chapters where the term is
used or defined. Where the canon file (\texttt{CANON/\allowbreak{}iam\_canon.json}, reproduced in Appendix~\ref{app:canon}) defines a term, the entry
follows the canon. Symbols, units and constants are in Appendix~\ref{app:notation}; every displayed equation is in Appendix~\ref{app:formulas}.
Status labels are defined in the front matter and listed here under their names. Entries are alphabetical by their first word; a symbol is
filed under the name of its letter.

\iamrowrule
\textbf{6dFGS, SDSS MGS} & Low-redshift galaxy surveys providing $f\sigma_8$ points at $z=0.067$ and 0.15. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:dual}, \ref{ch:latetime}, \ref{ch:level2})}\\[1pt]
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\normalsize\textbf{A}}\\[2pt]
\textbf{$A$ (informational fidelity ratio)} & A reading divided by the same system's healthy reading (the healthy reference), measured on the same instrument. Dimensionless; $A=1$ is the system working as itself, in the middle of the gauge; above 1 is more error than healthy; the floor $\Hmin$ lies below 1 and nothing reads below it. Cells: Met-A and IAM-A; qubits: $\varepsilon$ over the device as built; chips: $E_{\rm sw}$ over the chip as built. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:gauge}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{$A_{\rm rel}$ (tared reading)} & A Met-A reading divided by the median reading of at least three healthy references run the same way (same slide, else same batch). The gauge state is printed on $A_{\rm rel}$ only; without references a reading is reported as untared. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:instrument}, \ref{ch:separation}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Acoustic scale ($\theta_s$)} & The angle subtended by the sound horizon at recombination, read from the CMB peak spacing. A photon-ruler quantity; with $\Sigma=1$ it is that of $\Lambda$CDM, and it bounds any photon coupling to $\beta_\gamma<0.0039$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:theory}, \ref{ch:dual}, \ref{ch:dsvalidation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Across-array SD} & The standard deviation of $\beta$ at a site across the physical reference arrays; the identity-site rule requires at most 0.05. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:identity})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{ACT, WMAP} & CMB experiments (the Atacama Cosmology Telescope and the Wilkinson Microwave Anisotropy Probe); combined with SDSS and supernovae they give $\mu_0=0.02\pm0.19$ independently of Planck. \observed {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:entropicgravity}, \ref{ch:latetime}, \ref{ch:surveys})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Activated process} & A rate proportional to $e^{-E_a/\kB T}$. Its local logarithmic slope $E_a/\kB T$ changes with temperature, so a power law in $T$ describes it only over a window. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:thermaln})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Activation energy $E_a$} & The energy barrier of a thermally activated process; its local logarithmic slope is $E_a/\kB T$. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:thermaln})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Activation function $E(a)$} & $E(a)=\exp(1-1/a)=e^{-z}$, the accumulated record term of the cosmic horizon normalised to $E(1)=1$. It vanishes before structure forms, grows monotonically because no payment is returned, has its inflection at $a=1/2$, and tends to $e$. Derived from the record-density constraint $\dot\rho_{\rm info}=\rho_{\rm info}H/a$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:virial}, \ref{ch:level2}, \ref{ch:darkenergy})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{AdS/CFT} & The correspondence between gravity in anti-de Sitter space and a conformal field theory on its boundary. Cited as the setting in which a fixed-charge sector reduces to an orbit; in de Sitter space the reduction is open. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:bhinformation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Affine parameter} & A parameter $\lambda$ along a null geodesic in which the geodesic equation takes its simplest form; in Jacobson's construction it measures distance along the generators of the local horizon, zero at the surface element. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:bekenstein}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Affleck--Dine mechanism} & A baryogenesis model in which a scalar condensate carrying baryon number decays; like other mechanisms it leaves the size of the asymmetry set by its own parameters. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:baryon})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Ageing clock} & A population model that places a person on an age curve built from many people; set aside for the instrument, whose measure of ageing is one person's own earlier readings. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:reach})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Alcubierre metric} & A solution of general relativity in which a flat region is carried by reshaping the geometry around it. It requires negative energy density in the wall, violating the weak energy condition. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:exploratory})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{$A_{\max}$ (full surface)} & The far end of a cell gauge: the reading when every identity site sits at a coin flip, one bit over the healthy reference. For neutrophils 3.03 on Met-A and 4.45 on IAM-A, both computed from the healthy reference alone. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:surfaces}, \ref{ch:floorbreach}, \ref{ch:gauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{AMD Ryzen 9 9950X} & A desktop processor; the worked example of the chip gauge and the baseline of prediction S2. Its power and clock are the maker's specification; no maker-published transistor count exists, so its reading is the range 576--593 times $\kB T_j\ln2$ set by the two die-level counts, $(20.0$--$20.6)\times10^9$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:cmos}, \ref{ch:landauer}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{AML (acute myeloid leukaemia)} & A cancer of myeloid progenitors. On the neutrophil identity sites, diagnosis blood stayed in Normal in 6 of 10 patients because the blasts share those sites (PROC-AML-SERIAL-01); progenitor floors on EPIC are not yet built. The leukaemic cell is usually the majority cell, so the read line does not limit it. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:identity}, \ref{ch:firstreadings}, \ref{part4:ch:reach})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{AMPS argument, firewall} & The argument of Almheiri, Marolf, Polchinski and Sully that late Hawking radiation carrying information would violate the monogamy of entanglement, and that a firewall at the horizon might restore unitarity. With no independent interior degrees of freedom, IAM's reading needs no firewall. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:bhinformation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Analogy (status label)} & ANALOGY: a correspondence between two domains that guides a method but is not a derivation. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:entanglement}, \ref{ch:meta}, \ref{ch:translation})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Apparent horizon} & The surface of radius $\tilde r_A=c/H$ in a flat Friedmann universe on which Cai and Kim apply the first law $-dE=T\,dS$; its area is $A_H=4\pi c^2/H^2$ and its temperature the Gibbons--Hawking value. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:blackholes}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Architecture position $P_{\rm cell}$} & The healthy cell type's position above the IAM-A floor, $P_{\rm cell}=H(\varepsilon_{\rm healthy})/H(\varepsilon_0)$, measured once on healthy cells with one read-level pipeline and frozen. Neutrophils: $1.099$ (three donors, 1.084--1.108) on \texttt{loyfer\_pat\_v1}; any other pipeline needs its own $P$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:saturation}, \ref{ch:iama}, \ref{ch:onegauge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Array (methylation array)} & A bisulfite microarray (Illumina 450K, EPIC v1, EPIC v2) that reports two fluorescence intensities per CpG probe, from which $\beta$ is computed. The chain reads EPIC v1 only; 450K and EPIC v2 are refused until each has its own healthy reference. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:landauer}, \ref{ch:meta}, \ref{ch:instrument})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Arrow of time} & The direction in which irreversible records accumulate. In IAM's reading it has a physical carrier, $E(a)$, which only increases because each record is irreversible. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:theoryinterp}, \ref{ch:time}, \ref{ch:virial_decoherence})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Assumptions 1--3} & The premises of the cosmological result: (1) gravity is a thermodynamic equation of state; (2) records written in the bulk contribute to the entropy of the nearest horizon (the one identification); (3) the virial half holds at the population level, $\beta_m=\Omega_m/2$. Chapter~\ref{ch:theory} adds (4) $E(a)$ exact and (5) the term acts on perturbations only. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:bekenstein}, \ref{ch:theory})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Astro-Genetics} & The name used here for reading the methylome as cosmology reads the sky: calibration, component separation, residual maps and null tests carried from CMB analysis to methylation data. {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:bridge})}\\[1pt]
\iamrowrule
\textbf{Asymptotic expansion rates} & The rates the two sectors settle at as $a\to\infty$: for the photon-sector $H_0=67.16$, $H_0\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}=55.57$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ for light and $H_0\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda+\beta_me}=70.86$ for the matter flow (55.78 and 71.12 with the Planck 2018 base values, $H_0=67.4$). \calc {\footnotesize(Ch.~\ref{ch:statusall})}\\[1pt]

\iamrowrule
\textbf{ATP (adenosine triphosphate)} & The cell's energy currency. Its cytosolic free energy of hydrolysis, $\dGATP=54$~kJ\,mol$^{-1}$, sets the cell's Mahaffey number $\mathcal M=20.94$. {\footnotesize(Chs.~\ref{ch:iams_law}, \ref{ch:landauer}, \ref{ch:floorbreach})}\\[1pt]
