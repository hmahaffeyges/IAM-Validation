\chapter{Glossary}\label{app:glossary}

\begin{description}\small
\item[$A$] a cell's reading: $H$ of its mean $\beta$ on its identity loci over its class floor.
\item[architecture class] the set of cell types sharing one governing inversion; names the floor a cell is divided by.
\item[atlas] the reference set of purified-cell methylomes, a posterior with an interval at every locus.
\item[Cellular Performance Gauge (CPG)] the instrument: the chain from raw arrays to per-cell readings.
\item[floor, $\Hmin$] the minimum entropy a cell of a class holds on its identity loci; calibrated.
\item[floor point, $\bstar$] the $\beta$ at which $H=\Hmin$, upper branch.
\item[identity loci] loci where a cell's own methylation sits at its floor point; chosen per cell.
\item[inversion] a class's dominant way of losing identity.
\item[Landauer Metrology] measurement of a cell's methylation state against its physical floor with the inference methods of CMB analysis.
\item[Mahaffey number, $M$] $\Edrive/\kB T$; for the cell $\dGATP/R\Tb=20.94$.
\item[out-of-span map] the part of a specimen's residual no mixture of atlas cells can produce.
\item[physics of methylation] the study of the methylome as an encoding surface with a Landauer cost.
\item[serial mode] readings of one person across draws: per-cell differences, the difference sky, trajectories.
\item[sky] a specimen's whole-array residual after composition.
\item[tier] the word given to a reading by the tier scheme in force.
\end{description}
