# Law and Order — Part 2 chapter drafts (development)

`mgcamb_validation/CHAIN_EXTRACTION_FINAL.csv` (one extraction of the final chain files, 30 % burn-in). Status labels follow the book's macros.
- `p2_dualsector_chains.tex` — the dual-sector model and the 18-chain record.
- `p2_baryon.tex` — the present-epoch baryon relation and the baryon chain.
- `p2_lambda.tex` — the cosmological constant: the 123 orders cancel; the present-epoch relation sqrt(Omega_Lambda) = (16/3) Omega_b/Omega_m (0.8 %), derivation open.
- `p2_virial.tex` — 2.1: virial partition, the Landauer identity, beta_m = Omega_m/2, E(a), mu(a), the two consequences n = 7/2 (Theory paper Eqs. 36-41); N-body comparison held until traced.
- `p2_theory.tex` — the IAM Theory paper carried section by section (§1–12, 14–15); author-hold boxes for Eq. 41, w_a, the N-body tables, §11.5, Fig. 2.
- `p2_late_time_growth.tex` — the Level 1 paper (Late-Time Growth Suppression) carried in its order with the final chain numbers.
