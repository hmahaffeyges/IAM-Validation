\chapter{The baryon fraction}\label{ch:baryon}

\section{The size of the asymmetry}

The universe holds baryons and almost no antibaryons. The CMB acoustic peaks measure $\Omega_bh^2=0.02237\pm0.00015$, so the baryon-to-photon ratio is
$\eta=273.9\times10^{-10}\,\Omega_bh^2=(6.13\pm0.04)\times10^{-10}$ \observed. Big-bang nucleosynthesis gives a consistent, broader range from the light elements.
Making any asymmetry needs Sakharov's three conditions: baryon-number violation, C and CP violation, and departure from equilibrium.
The Standard Model's CP violation is far too small to give $\eta\sim10^{-9}$.

\section{The present-epoch relation}

Chapter~\ref{ch:lambda} gives the relation between the dark-energy and matter budgets. Solved for the baryons it reads
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\Omega_b}{\Omega_m}=\frac{3}{16}\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}.
 \label{eq:baryonrel}
\end{equation}
\calc\ With Planck 2018 ($\Omega_m=0.3153$, $h=0.6736$, $\Omega_bh^2=0.02237$): the measured ratio is $0.15636$; the right-hand side is $0.15515$.
They agree to $0.8\,\%$. In baryon-to-photon units, Eq.~\eqref{eq:baryonrel} gives $\eta=6.08\times10^{-10}$ against the measured $6.13\pm0.04$.

\observed\ Eq.~\eqref{eq:baryonrel} is a relation among today's measured densities. It holds to $0.8\,\%$. \openprob\ It is not yet derived:
no mechanism in the framework produces it at the epoch when the asymmetry was made, and the $\eta$ value is the same relation in other units, not a second test.
$\Omega_m$ also contains $\Omega_b$, so the two sides are correlated through the data.

\section{The baryon chain}

The eighteenth chain runs Planck 2018 with the IAM growth modification fixed at $\mu_0=-0.13495$ and the baryon density sampled under a flat prior
$0.01<\Omega_bh^2<0.04$, six times the width of the range used in the other chains (Run A: $0.020$--$0.025$). There is no nucleosynthesis prior.
\observed\ Result (final file, 30\,\% burn-in, last $R-1=0.0099$): $\Omega_bh^2=0.022319\pm0.000136$, $\eta=(6.113\pm0.037)\times10^{-10}$,
$\sigma_8=0.8016$, $H_0=67.04$.

\begin{checkbox}
This is the CMB's own measurement of $\Omega_bh^2$ under IAM growth. It agrees with the $\Lambda$CDM value ($0.022319$ against $0.02237$, $0.3\sigma$).
It shows that the matter-sector modification leaves the baryon inference untouched, as it must: the acoustic peaks fix $\Omega_bh^2$ at early times,
where $E(a)\to0$ and IAM and $\Lambda$CDM are the same equations. The chain is a consistency check of the growth modification. It does not test
Eq.~\eqref{eq:baryonrel}.
\end{checkbox}

\section{What would make it a derivation}

\openprob\ A derivation must produce $\Omega_b/\Omega_m$ or $\eta$ from the record-writing budget at the electroweak or QCD transition, without taking
$\Omega_\Lambda$ from today's data. Until then Eq.~\eqref{eq:baryonrel} is printed as an observed relation of the present epoch, $0.8\,\%$ from exact.
