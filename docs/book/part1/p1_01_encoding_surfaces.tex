\chapter{The places in IAM}\label{ch:surfaces}

\section*{The statement}
Every irreversible transition from quantum superposition to classical record pays $k_BT\ln2$ per bit to the nearest encoding surface, at that
surface's temperature, and the record is written on that two-dimensional surface. This is IAM's Law (Chapter~\ref{ch:iams_law}). IAM is not found in a cell, a device, a black hole or the cosmos: these are places found in
IAM, each a surface where records are written at a temperature. The law is one; the places differ only in the surface, its temperature and the
number of bits it holds. Every chapter of this book is one such place. This chapter assembles the law from five established results and one
identification, then lists the places.

\section{Six steps}
\paragraph{1. A surface has a capacity (Bekenstein 1973).} A horizon of area $A$ carries entropy $S=k_BA/4\ell_P^2$, $\ell_P=\sqrt{\hbar G/c^3}$:
one unit of $k_B$ per four Planck areas, $1.5\times10^{77}$ bits for a solar-mass black hole. Information is bounded by area, not volume.
\paragraph{2. The surface has a temperature (Hawking 1975; Gibbons and Hawking 1977).} A black hole radiates at $T_H=\hbar c^3/8\pi GMk_B$
($6.2\times10^{-8}$\,K for one solar mass); a cosmological horizon at $T_{GH}=\hbar H/2\pi k_B$ ($2.65\times10^{-30}$\,K today).
\paragraph{3. A bit has a price (Landauer 1961; B\'erut et al.\ 2012).} Irreversibly fixing one bit at temperature $T$ costs at least
$k_BT\ln2$: $2.87\times10^{-21}$\,J (17.9\,meV) at 300\,K, the bound approached in measured erasures. The price depends only on the temperature.
\paragraph{4. Gravity is the thermodynamics of surfaces (Jacobson 1995; Cai and Kim 2005).} Applying $\delta Q=T\,dS$ to every local causal
horizon gives the Einstein equations as an equation of state. The entropy functional determines the dynamics; Jacobson used the geometric
entropy alone and obtained general relativity.
\paragraph{5. The records were always in the entropy (the identification).} Decoherence of matter on timelike worldlines produces classical records,
and the records are written on the nearest surface. The entropy functional becomes $S=S_{\rm geo}+S_{\rm info}$. The Einstein equations are
untouched; the entropy is completed. Light, on null worldlines with no proper time, writes nothing. The missing information was always information.
\paragraph{6. The same price in the cell.} A cell maintains its identity as a methylation record across its CpG sites. Each maintained
site is one binary record at body temperature: $k_B\,(310.15\,{\rm K})\ln2=2.97\times10^{-21}$\,J. Over the $2.82\times10^7$ CpG sites of the human genome the floor is
$8.37\times10^{-14}$\,J per copy, about $1.0\times10^6$ ATP at 50\,kJ\,mol$^{-1}$.

\section{The floor and its breach}
Each surface has a floor: the minimum record it must maintain to remain what it is. Measured against that floor, a system can hold its record
(the reading sits at the floor) or lose it faster than it is maintained. The breach is the same condition at every place: the record is produced
or lost faster than the surface can maintain it. Two endpoints are named in this book. When a collapsing mass concentrates records faster than any
surface outside it can hold, a horizon forms (Chapter~\ref{ch:blackholes}). When a cell loses its methylation record faster than maintenance restores
it, it departs from its own floor; the gauge reads that departure (Parts~3--4).

\paragraph{The reading.} For the cell, the floor is measured, not fitted: the mean per-site entropy $H(\beta)$ over the cell's identity sites in
purified healthy cells of the same type and platform. The reading is Met-A $=$ observed over floor; Normal is $0.95$--$1.05$ on one gauge for every
platform and cell. For sequencing reads the floor is $\varepsilon_0=1/(1+e^{\varphi M})=0.032$, derived, at the cell's frozen position on it, and the reading is
IAM-A. The same reading is taken at every
place: measured over its floor.

\section{What is established and what is open}
Steps 1--4 are established physics; step 5 reveals what they share and is the one identification, tested in cosmology by the chains (Part~2), and step 6 applies the same
price to the cell. The law fixes the price per bit at each surface. What it does not yet give is each surface's floor from first principles: the
cell's floor is measured, and deriving it through the full chain is the open problem that would make the ladder a numerical unification.
