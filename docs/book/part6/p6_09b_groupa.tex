\chapter{Group-A: has a whole group moved?}\label{ch:groupa}

\section{One person, and a group}

In whole blood, healthy people differ from one another by about 2\,\% after the tare (Chapter~\ref{ch:meta}). A 1\,\% loss of the
neutrophils' pattern moves one person's reading by about 2\,\%, which sits inside that spread: one person cannot be called on it. A group can.
The standard error of a group's mean falls as the square root of its size, so a hundred people read a shift ten times smaller than one person
can. Group-A reads that: whether a trial arm, an exposure or a cohort has moved as a whole.

\section{How it reads}

Each person is read by the one command, unchanged: Stage~0, Stage~1, the composition, Met-A against the person's own expected reading.
Each reading is then tared against healthy arrays run the same way, on the same slide when the slide holds at least three, else in the same
series:
\begin{equation}
  A_{\rm rel}=\frac{A}{\mathrm{median}(A_{\rm ref})}.
  \label{eq:groupa_tare}
\end{equation}
The two groups' tared means are compared,
\begin{equation}
  t=\frac{\bar A_{\rm rel,1}-\bar A_{\rm rel,2}}{\sqrt{s_1^2/n_1+s_2^2/n_2}},
  \label{eq:groupa_t}
\end{equation}
and a group that moved is one with $|t|\ge 2$. Nothing is fitted: no model is trained on either group, and the reading of each person is
the same reading the report prints. The groups must be run in the same series, because across laboratories the untared readings differ by
several percent while the tare only removes what is shared by one run. \measured{}

\section{The commissioning test}

The rule was sealed before any array was opened: two healthy groups (women and men) read the same after the tare, under 2\,\% and under
2 standard errors apart, and a 1\,\% loss planted by the chain's own rule into half of the healthy arrays is found at more than 2 standard
errors. The data were the 278 healthy controls of a study run in one laboratory no IAM instrument had read before (GSE318173), read on
GitHub's machines and scored once.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\hline
bar & result & \\
\hline
arrays read & 263 of 278 (15 withheld for coverage) & met \\
women against men & $-0.36$\,\%, $-1.206$ standard errors & met \\
planted 1\,\% loss & $+2.00$\,\%, $7.253$ standard errors & met \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Group-A is commissioned on groups of whole bloods run in one series} (EPIC v1). The healthy spread after the tare in
this new laboratory was 2.2\,\%, the same as before. The planted loss is scattered, and the clustering meter (Chapter~\ref{ch:cscore}) did
not call it: on each planted array Met-A moved by up to 0.036 and the C-score by at most 0.006. The cell mixes of women and men differ, and
Met-A still read the two groups the same, because each person's expected reading follows that person's own mix. Record:
\texttt{doors/COMMISSIONING\_NOTE\_GROUPA.md}.
