\chapter*{Preface: nothing is new, just revealed}\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

\begin{quote}``If I have seen further than others, it is by standing on the shoulders of giants.'' --- Isaac Newton, 1675\\[2pt]
``I just noticed what was already there and pulled on a thread that never stopped giving --- so I had to learn the language of the giants and
learn to use their tools.''\end{quote}

\paragraph{What this book is.} It is a model of reality as it already exists. It is not a new model set against general relativity, quantum
mechanics or the standard cosmological model. Those are correct descriptions of their own places, and every result they have earned stands. The
Einstein equations are untouched. Nothing is replaced. One quantity is counted that was always there, and counting it shows that results from
separate fields describe the same event.

\paragraph{The event.} Every irreversible transition from a quantum superposition to a classical record pays $k_BT\ln2$ per bit to the nearest
encoding surface, at that surface's temperature. That is IAM's Law. The pieces are not ours. Bekenstein put entropy on the horizon, proportional
to its area. Hawking gave the horizon a temperature. Shannon gave information its mathematics and Landauer gave it a price. Zurek showed how
records form. Jacobson showed that the Einstein equations follow from the thermodynamics of horizons, with the entropy as the engine. Every piece
was on the table. What was missing from the entropy was the record itself: the classical information that matter writes as it decoheres. The
missing information was always information.

\paragraph{What kind of unification.} Not a unification of forces. No new particle, no extra dimension, no new field. It is a unification at the
boundary. The quantum and the classical are two sides of one transition, and IAM's Law prices the crossing. Neither comes out of that transition;
both depend on it. The quantum description says what can become a record. The classical description is made of records already written. The
crossing is the same in a cell holding its methylation record at body temperature, in a qubit being measured, at the horizon of a black hole and at
the horizon of the universe. Those are not places where IAM turns up. They are places found in IAM, and each chapter of this book is one of them.

\paragraph{What the reader will find.} At the cosmic horizon the record is written by matter collapsing into structure. Before structure there is
nothing to write, so the early universe is exactly the standard model, and the cosmic microwave background, the acoustic peaks and the light
elements are unchanged. As structure forms, matter writes, and only matter: light has no proper time and writes nothing. Matter feels a weaker
effective coupling ($\mu<1$), light feels standard gravity ($\Sigma=1$), with the strength fixed by the virial half of the bound matter,
$\beta_m=\Omega_m/2$, and nothing adjusted. Eighteen Planck chains are consistent with it. The matter-sector expansion rate is $72.26$ against the
photon-sector $67.16$\,km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$, from one posterior, and $\sigma_8$ is lower in every data combination. The rate at which records
are written, per e-fold, peaks today. The prediction $\mu_0=-0.135$ is on the record; Euclid's full first data release, expected in 2027, measures
$\mu_0$ to about $\pm0.04$. In the cell, the same price over the genome's $2.8\times10^7$ CpG sites sets a floor, and the instrument in the
second half of this book reads a cell against its own floor.

\paragraph{Why an outsider.} In power-grid operations the Reliability Coordinator does not run any one generator. The coordinator watches the
whole interconnect at once: the storm in the distance, the imbalance between two regions that no local operator can see. Science needs its
specialists; everything here stands on them. But a wide-area view can see that a tributary nobody mapped feeds several channels at once. The
Hubble tension, the low growth of structure, and the coincidence of dark energy with structure formation look like separate puzzles from inside
their fields. Seen from above, they are one source.

\paragraph{How to read it.} Each chapter opens by naming its place: the surface, its temperature, and what is written there. Every number is
recomputed by a script that ships with the book. Where a result is open, the book says so. If it is right, it is right everywhere at once;
the predictions are dated, and the data is coming.
