\begin{abstract}
Time is two-faced. The first face, coordinate time as a redundant label on
the spacetime manifold, was identified by the Machian relational framework
\citep{BarbourBertotti1982}. The second face has remained elusive.
We ask whether \textit{duration} is equally redundant, and propose
a distinction that bears on this question.
The relational framework not only accommodates the findings that follow,
it points directly toward them.

We propose a distinction among three quantities that are often treated
interchangeably: (i)~coordinate time, a label on the manifold with no
preferred direction and no accumulation, redundant as a fundamental concept;
(ii)~proper time $\tau$, geometric path length along a worldline, reversible
in principle; and (iii)~duration, measured by $E(a) = \exp(1 - 1/a)$,
thermodynamic, irreversible, beginning at the electroweak transition,
accumulating on massive particles only.

The distinction is not terminological. Photons travel null geodesics:
$d\tau = 0$. They plot on the manifold but accumulate neither proper time
nor duration. Massive particles travel timelike worldlines, accumulate both
$\tau$ and $E(a)$, and constitute the matter sector. This sector separation, photon sector and matter sector, follows directly from the geometry
of the manifold combined with the thermodynamic cost of irreversible
decoherence events on timelike worldlines.

The arrow of time is not a statistical artifact of initial conditions. It is
a thermodynamic necessity: each decoherence event writes information
permanently on the cosmic horizon, with each bit erased carrying a minimum
thermodynamic cost of $k_BT\ln 2$~\citep{Landauer1961}. The accumulated ledger
is $E(a)$, monotonically increasing by construction. Duration has direction
because actualization has direction. Potential moves toward act, never the
reverse.

\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} time; duration; Machian relationism; arrow of
time; electroweak symmetry breaking; gravitational decoherence; sector
separation; Informational Actualization Model

\section{Two Faces}
\label{sec:twofaces}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Time is two-faced.

Barbour identified the first face in 1982 with the Machian relational
framework: coordinate time is redundant, a label on a dimension, not a
river carrying us forward~\citep{BarbourBertotti1982}. That result
has shaped how foundational questions about time are posed.

The second face has remained elusive. Is duration equally redundant? We
followed that question and found something unexpected. Duration is real,
irreversible, and has a specific physical origin. And the relational
framework Barbour built not only accommodates this finding, it points
directly toward it.

\section{Three Quantities Worth Distinguishing}
\label{sec:threequantities}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Time appears in physical theory in at least three distinct roles that
are worth distinguishing carefully. The differences between them are
not merely terminological.

\subsection{Coordinate time: the map}

Coordinate time, $t$ or the scale factor $a$, is a label on the
spacetime manifold. It assigns a number to each event. It does not require
anything to happen. It does not require worldlines to exist, mass to be
present, or irreversible processes to occur. The manifold exists with its
coordinate structure whether or not any event within it is irreversible.

Barbour's central insight~\citep{BarbourBertotti1982,Barbour1994} is that
this coordinate has no privileged status. It is redundant as a fundamental
concept. The relational framework constructs dynamics from configurations
alone, without reference to an external temporal parameter.
Coordinate time is the map. It is not the territory.

\subsection{Proper time: path length on the map}

Proper time $\tau$ is the geometric path length along a worldline:
\begin{equation}
\tau = \int \sqrt{-g_{\mu\nu}\,dx^\mu dx^\nu}.
\end{equation}
It is still a geometric quantity, a property of the spacetime manifold
measured along a particular path. A clock measures proper time. It tells
you how far you have traveled on the map.

Critically, proper time is reversible in principle. In a universe with
time-reversal symmetric laws, a worldline running forward and backward in
coordinate time accumulates $\tau$ in both directions. Proper time
distinguishes clocks from one another but does not, by itself, distinguish
past from future.

For null geodesics, photons, $d\tau = 0$ identically. Photons plot on
the manifold but accumulate zero proper time. This is not an approximation.
It is the definition of a null geodesic.

\subsection{Duration: the journey itself}

Duration is categorically different from both coordinate time and proper
time. It is not a geometric quantity. It is thermodynamic.

Duration measures the accumulated irreversible acts on a worldline, the
decoherence events that write classical information permanently on the cosmic
horizon at Landauer cost $k_BT\ln 2$ per bit~\citep{Landauer1961}. It
requires:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Massive particles on timelike worldlines ($d\tau > 0$),
\item Irreversible quantum-to-classical transitions (gravitational
decoherence),
\item A preferred direction established by CP violation at the electroweak
transition~\citep{Sakharov1967}.
\end{enumerate}

Before the electroweak symmetry breaking at $t \approx 10^{-12}$~s: no
massive particles, no timelike worldlines, every process time-reversible.
Duration cannot accumulate. $E(a) \equiv 0$.

After the electroweak transition: the matter sector exists, CP violation
locks in the arrow of time at the particle physics level, and duration
begins. $E(a)$ starts its 13.8-billion-year journey.

The map and the ledger are shown in Figure~\ref{fig:twofaces}. The same
worldline admits two categorically different descriptions.

\section{The Second Face: Duration}
\label{sec:duration}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\subsection{The activation function}

Within the Informational Actualization Model
(IAM,~\citealt{mahaffey2026}), the accumulated duration since the
electroweak transition is measured by the activation function:
\begin{equation}
E(a) = \exp\!\left(1 - \frac{1}{a}\right),
\label{eq:Ea}
\end{equation}
where $a$ is the cosmological scale factor normalized to unity today.

The properties of $E(a)$ follow directly from its physical interpretation:
\begin{itemize}
\item $E(a) \to 0$ as $a \to 0$: no duration before matter exists.
\item $E(1) = 1$: normalized to today.
\item $E(a) \to e$ as $a \to \infty$: duration approaches but never reaches
Pure Act; the asymptote is approached, never attained.
\item $dE/da > 0$ always: monotonically increasing, one direction only.
\end{itemize}

The monotonic increase is not imposed. It follows from the irreversibility
of each decoherence event. Each bit written on the horizon cannot be
unwritten. The ledger only accumulates. This is the arrow of time, not
statistical, not initial-condition-dependent, but thermodynamically
necessary.

\subsection{Photons do not accumulate duration}

Photons travel null geodesics. $d\tau = 0$ by construction. No proper time
accumulates. No gravitational decoherence occurs; decoherence requires
nonzero proper time on a timelike worldline. Therefore:
\begin{equation}
E(a)\big|_{\text{photon}} \equiv 0.
\end{equation}

This is the physical basis for the sector split. Photons interact with the
spacetime geometry; they are bent by gravity, redshifted by expansion,
but they do not write in the ledger. The modification to gravitational
dynamics that $E(a)$ produces applies exclusively to the matter sector.

\subsection{The Machian framework already knew this}

The relational framework insists that physics be built from observable
relations between things. Coordinate time fails this test, it is not
directly observable. Proper time passes, clocks measure it. Duration
passes with particular force: it is the only temporal quantity that
distinguishes past from future by means of a physical mechanism rather than
a statistical assumption.

Mach's criterion, ``time is an abstraction at which we arrive through
the changes of things'', selects duration over coordinate time
immediately. But not all changes are equal. Reversible changes do not
advance the ledger. Only irreversible changes, only decoherence events,
only Landauer costs paid, constitute duration in the sense that gives
time its direction.

The relational framework points naturally toward this distinction. The physical mechanism, the electroweak transition establishing irreversibility and the Landauer cost quantifying it, connects the relational picture to thermodynamics.

\section{What the Sector Split Implies}
\label{sec:sectorsplit}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The distinction between the map and the ledger has an immediate
observational consequence that may help clarify a persistent tension
in cosmological observations.

The CMB measurement of $H_0$ uses photons emitted at last scattering,
traveling null geodesics for 13.8 billion years. The entire measurement is
geometric. No proper time accumulated. No duration written. It reads the
map. $H_0^{\text{photon}} = 67.16$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ is what the
manifold says about its own expansion rate~\citep{PlanckCollaboration2020}.

The local distance ladder uses Cepheids, Type~Ia supernovae, and TRGB
stars, massive objects on timelike worldlines, that have been
accumulating duration for billions of years. The calibration is built
from clocks that tick. It reads the ledger.
$H_0^{\text{matter}} = 72.26$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ is what the
matter sector says about its own expansion
rate~\citep{Riess2022,mahaffey2026obs}.

Within this framework, these are not necessarily two measurements of the
same quantity. They may be measurements of two distinct quantities,
one geometric and one thermodynamic, that a purely geometric treatment
does not distinguish. The Hubble tension is consistent with the sector
split, and the predicted values are derived from first principles with
zero free parameters beyond $\Lambda$CDM.

The predicted split follows from a single coupling constant derived from
the virial theorem:
\begin{equation}
\beta_m = \frac{\Omega_m}{2} = 0.1575,
\label{eq:betam}
\end{equation}
with zero free parameters beyond $\Lambda$CDM~\citep{mahaffey2026}.

\section{The Relational Framework and Shape Dynamics}
\label{sec:shapedynamics}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Shape dynamics~\citep{Barbour2011,BarbourKoslowskiMercati2014} replaces
four-dimensional spacetime refoliation invariance with three-dimensional
conformal invariance. The universe is described by the evolution of shape,  the relational configuration of matter, without reference to
absolute scale or absolute time.

IAM is compatible with this framework at every level. IAM does not modify
the background geometry. It does not alter the Friedmann equations. It
operates at the perturbation level, adding a single Hubble friction term
to the matter perturbation equations:
\begin{equation}
\ddot{\delta}_m + 2H\dot{\delta}_m - \frac{3}{2}\Omega_m H^2 \mu(a)\,\delta_m = 0,
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\mu(a) = \frac{H^2_{\Lambda\text{CDM}}}{H^2_{\Lambda\text{CDM}} + \beta_m E(a)} < 1.
\end{equation}

The geometric arena, Platonia, shape space, the relational configuration,
is untouched. Duration is what happens on the worldlines \textit{within}
that arena. It does not modify the arena itself.

Shape dynamics asks: what is the universe's shape? IAM asks: what has the
universe written in the process of becoming its current shape? The two
questions are complementary, not competing.

Platonia provides the map of all possible configurations. IAM reveals
what happens at the boundary when one is chosen. Each event written into
history and onto the map, where potential becomes actual, one
decoherence event at a time.

\section{An Open Question}
\label{sec:openquestion}

If duration begins at the electroweak transition at the moment the Higgs
field, undergoing spontaneous symmetry breaking through a quantum
fluctuation, selected a direction in field space irreversibly: was that
selection itself the first decoherence event? The first information written
on the horizon, the first Landauer cost paid, the first moment $E(a)$ could
step away from zero? This remains an open question~\citep{mahaffey2026higgs}.
