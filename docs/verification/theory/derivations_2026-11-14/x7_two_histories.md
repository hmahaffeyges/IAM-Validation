# X7: the two writing histories (desk, sympy)

- (R) matter era: dS/d ln a ~ a**(n - 9/2); with n = 7/2: 1/a
- (F) matter era: dS/d ln a ~ a**5*exp((a - 1)/a)
- (F)/(R) = a**6*exp((a - 1)/a): not a constant, so the two premises give DIFFERENT histories of the same S_info
- n that (R) would need to reproduce (F): n_eff(a) = 19/2 + 1/a; at a = 0.25, 0.5, 1: 13.50, 11.50, 10.50
- full LCDM, a: (R) with n = 3.5 vs (F), each normalized at a = 1:
-    a = 0.3: (R)    7.8579   (F)    0.0021
-    a = 0.5: (R)    4.1423   (F)    0.0715
-    a = 0.7: (R)    2.3349   (F)    0.3617
-    a = 1.0: (R)    1.0000   (F)    1.0000
- Reading: (R) with 7/2 makes the per-e-fold writing FALL with a in the matter era (1/a); (F) makes it RISE steeply (E a^5). Since the record term is rho_info ~ E(a) in both, and (F) is the first law applied to that term, the 7/2 obtained from (R) is not the writing history the first law requires. The book's two statements (eq:law_n from (R); eq:law_Sneed from (F)) are therefore not the same S_info.
- (R)+(F): written between a and today, matter era: integral of a^-7 d ln a = (1 - a**7)/(7*a**7)
- so the record term built from the 7/2 history is dominated by the EARLIEST writing and is nearly constant afterwards: Lambda-like, not E(a).
- Alternative reading (rate of a per-area density sigma, S = sigma A_H): S ~ a^3 (1 - 1/a + c) in the matter era, also not E a^5. Neither reading closes.
