## Corrections and holds
1. **The prediction depends on the form of the term (hold).** M_dyn ∝ µM_true needs µ in the Poisson equation (the Level 1 G_eff form). The abstract
   and §7 state the Level 2 form: the term is a friction term in the matter perturbations and "the linearized Einstein equations for the metric
   potentials remain unmodified". Then Ψ obeys the standard Poisson equation, matter in a virialised cluster moves in the true potential, and
   M_lens/M_dyn = 1. The paper asserts both. Same hold as the cluster three-way test (VIRIAL_CHECK): carried only with the form stated, and only
   once whether a linear µ applies inside virialised, non-linear clusters is derived.
2. **§3.3 and Table 2 "f(R): Σ > 1; IAM unique in µ < 1, Σ = 1"**: in the quasi-static limit f(R) has Σ = 1 and µ ≤ 4/3 (lensing mass = true mass,
   dynamical mass larger); self-accelerating DGP has µ < 1, Σ = 1 (LG12, SX8).
3. **§4.1 Planck SZ "resolved naturally"**: σ8 0.811 → 0.800 (−1.4 %) is a small fraction of the S8 ≈ 0.78 that the SZ counts require; the tension
   is reduced, not resolved.
4. **§4.2–4.3 CCCP 1.20 ± 0.12, WtG 1.31 ± 0.11**: the published quantities are M_HSE/M_WL (or M_Planck/M_WL) ratios with their own conventions;
   trace each to its table before printing. X-ray hydrostatic masses also follow Ψ, so lensing-vs-X-ray and lensing-vs-velocity are the same test
   only under item 1's form.
5. **"15 converged chains"** → 18; σ8 0.800 is the Level 2 chain value.
