### Building a bigger map of healthy cells: IAM-Atlas v2.1

**Running now.** Every IAM reading compares a sample with healthy cells. The atlas is that comparison: a map of what 74 kinds of healthy human cells look like. Version 2.1 more than doubles the samples behind it and adds second and third laboratories for key cells, so the map stops depending on any one lab's habits.

- **What we hope:** The new laboratories agree with the old ones, the map gets steadier, and tests that were too close to call, like lung, get a fair chance.
- **What could go wrong:** Cells grown in a dish may have drifted too far to use; a new laboratory may disagree with the old ones; the fit may need more computing time than planned.

### Brain cells: does healthy order hold in cells that never divide?

**Getting ready.** Neurons stop dividing for life, so they can never refresh their switch pattern by making a new copy. If they still sit where physics says healthy cells should, that tells us how cells hold their information. We built a healthy brain-cell reference from one laboratory's neurons, using a rule written before we looked.

- **What we hope:** Healthy neurons from two other laboratories, which we have never opened, read inside the healthy range we set in advance.
- **What could go wrong:** How the cells were prepared moved our first readings more than age or brain region did, so the reference may first need a rule about preparation before the test is fair.

### Lung cancer: waiting for a better map of the lung

**On hold.** We compare each patient's tumor with the same patient's normal lung. Before any tumor is opened, made-up tests on normal lungs must show that ordinary differences between people's lungs cannot fake a cancer signal.

- **What we hope:** With dish-grown lung support cells checked and added to the atlas, the made-up tests pass and the real tumors can finally be read.
- **What could go wrong:** The dish may have changed those cells too much to use, in which case lung waits for fresh, undish-grown samples that nobody has made public yet.

## IAM's Law

Every irreversible transition pays a thermodynamic cost at the nearest encoding surface: `k_B T ln 2` per bit, at that surface's own
temperature. The cost cannot be recovered or engineered away. Different substrates expose it differently; the law is the same.
The cost is Landauer's and has been measured; the surface and its temperature are Bekenstein's and Hawking's; Jacobson showed that
gravity follows from the thermodynamics of such surfaces.

## What IAM is

The Informational Actualization Model is a testable set of equations inside general relativity, the piece Jacobson's thermodynamic
derivation of Einstein's equations left open. At the cosmic horizon it fixes a late-time coupling with no free parameter: IAM fits the
Planck CMB as well as ΛCDM and predicts slower growth of structure with lensing unchanged, a test Euclid and DESI will make. In the
cell, the same price per bit sets a floor under how faithfully a methylation pattern can be held, and two instruments read each
cell's informational fidelity against that floor and against its own healthy state. The substrate changes. The noise changes. The measured inputs
change. The thermodynamic accounting does not.

## Why pick it up

- **For a cosmologist:** one parameter-free line fixes `μ_0`, the `fσ_8` ramp, `Σ = 1`, `E_G`, `S_8` and the two Hubble rates together, and
  the full Planck likelihood with the coupling fixed fits as well as ΛCDM. The surveys now running measure its size.
- **For a quantum physicist:** a gate error and a quasiparticle density have a thermal floor at the device's own temperature, from
  `k_B T ln 2` alone. The book gives the physics, with makers' published device values as illustrations.
- **For a geneticist:** the methylation pattern is a record a cell pays to keep. Each cell type is read against its own healthy state,
  so one person can be measured, measured again, and compared with themselves.

Every claim carries one status label (DERIVED, CALCULATED, CALIBRATED, MEASURED, OBSERVED, FITTED, CONJECTURE, PREDICTION or OPEN), and
every number in the book is recomputed by a script in this repository. The invitation is to try to break it.

## The atlas

**IAM-Atlas v2** is the reference every instrument reads against: 74 purified healthy human cell types, every CpG with its value and a
measured uncertainty, from arrays and sequencing of several laboratories fitted as one model and fixed before any sample is read.
It was built for IAM's measurement and is independent of IAM's physics: the atlas supplies the healthy end of the gauge and IAM's Law the floor,
so a reading that lands where the law predicts is evidence, not a reflection of the reference.
What it is, how it was built, how to get it and what it has passed: [`Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/atlas/`](Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/atlas/); the fitted atlas: DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23325865](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23325865).
