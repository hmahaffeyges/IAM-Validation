# Reality in bits: IAM's Law across every scale (outline, 2026-10-15)

**IAM is a model of how reality begins with a bit and the cost that comes with it.** The book stays the source; this paper translates it into
computational language and shows where each prior piece fits. Manifest OTH-061 (bit convention), OTH-062 (literature positioning).

Author, 2026-10-15: "we are taking all of the 'bits' and pieces from all of these ... and we are showing how they all connect ... credit each
of these people ... and reveal the ones that were missing IAM's law such as the entropy functional, and reveal how the virial theorem is one
example of IAM's law."

## Sections
1. **The law.** One bit, one price: k_B T ln 2 at the temperature of the surface where the record is written (book, Ch. IAM's Law).
2. **The computational dictionary.** For each domain: bits stored (capacity), bits per operation (snapshot, limit), bit rate (in motion),
   gate error (the firm end), leakage (the loose end), operating point (healthy), utilization and throttling (right of healthy),
   saturation and overflow (the ceiling). Source: FOUR_DOMAINS.md, every entry from its book section.
3. **The pieces and whose they are.** For each prior result: the piece, where it sits on IAM's diagram, what IAM connects it to.
   Every credit checked against the original paper before it is written (OTH-062).
4. **What was missing.** The entropy functional (horizon entropy completed with information, book abstract); the virial theorem as IAM's
   Law at a bound state, its mechanical special case (book, What the kinetic half is); one price at every surface; the record rate (7/2),
   E(a) = exp(1 - 1/a), information density = rho_m/2 today.
5. **Scale by scale, in the same units.** Hydrogen, qubit, chip, cell, star, black hole, horizon.
6. **What can fail.** The predictions, each with the book's tag.
