IAM Disease Signature Wall v1_13 — discriminating fingerprints

Reference library of disease fingerprints for matching a patient's whole-blood IDAT. Trajectory rows trimmed to the cells that discriminate — immune compartment (lymphoid/myeloid) + cell-of-origin — so a patient profile can be matched, not drowned in backdrop.

What the numbers mean.
Each cell's A-score = mean of H(β)/Hmin over its marker CpGs (whole blood through the IAMAtlas, no deconvolver). The number is Cohen's d: (meancase−meancontrol)/pooled SD. +0.33 = cases sit 0.33 SD above controls. Rule of thumb: 0.15 ≈ noise, 0.2 small, 0.5 medium, 0.8 large, 1.2+ very large.
