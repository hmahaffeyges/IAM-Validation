# chain/TOOLKIT.md - the toolkit: built, kept, not yet wired into chain v3

Every module below is in the repository and importable, and **none of it is part of a reading**. Each enters chain v3 at
commissioning, one stage at a time, after its own pre-registered check has passed on v3 and the outcome is recorded in `doors/`
(order and checks: [`../sop/MethylPhys_CPG_SOP_v3.md`](../sop/MethylPhys_CPG_SOP_v3.md) section 2b). Every module carries the line
*Toolkit: not yet wired into chain v3; enters the chain at commissioning with its own pre-registered check.* Stage numbers and names
are those of SOP v3 section 2b. Paths are relative to `Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/`. The class-era engine these modules were
first used with (chain v2) was retired on 2026-10-03 and is archived privately; nothing here imports it.

Notes

- `CPG_Null_Runner/cpg_null_runner.py` null N7 loads the synthetic patient generator, which was retired with chain v2; N7 reports DEFERRED until a v3 generator exists. The other nulls run without it.
- `cmb_tools.py` checks fields of the retired v2 bundle (`patient_sky`, `foreign_detection`, `second_opinion`); on a v3 bundle every entry reads NOT_RUN until the stage it checks is wired in.
- The class-era wrappers that called these modules inside the retired conductor (the stage 4.6 sky wrapper, the class-level NILC second opinion) were not carried over: they read retired files (class identity loci, beta scale maps). Each stage gets a v3 wrapper when it is commissioned.
