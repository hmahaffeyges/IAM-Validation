# chain/TOOLKIT.md - the toolkit: built, kept, entering chain v3 one stage at a time (2026-10-03: 12b wired; 2026-10-04, development round 2: stages likely to work behind development flags)

Every module below is in the repository and importable. **None of it is part of a reading**; stage 12b (difference map) is wired as an optional second-draw comparison and changes no reading. Since 2026-10-04 (author decision N, `doors/DEV_FLAGS_01.md`) the stages likely to work run behind development flags of `run_sample.py` (`chain/dev_stages.py`): each writes `bundle["development"][<stage>]`, labelled DEVELOPMENT - not commissioned, and none changes the reading. Development round 2 records: `doors/DEV_ROUND2_REPORT.md`. Each enters chain v3 at
commissioning, one stage at a time, after its own pre-registered check has passed on v3 and the outcome is recorded in `doors/`
(order and checks: [`../sop/MethylPhys_CPG_SOP_v3.md`](../sop/MethylPhys_CPG_SOP_v3.md) section 2b). Every module carries the line
*Toolkit: not yet wired into chain v3; enters the chain at commissioning with its own pre-registered check.* Stage numbers and names
are those of SOP v3 section 2b. Paths are relative to `Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/`. The class-era engine these modules were
first used with (chain v2) was retired on 2026-10-03 and is archived privately; nothing here imports it.
