# Operations Manual: chain v4 (retired into the SOP)

This chapter was retired into the one operating procedure on 2026-11-12: everything an operator needs (installing, checking the chain, reading
one sample or a series, development readings, what the chain declines, before a run, when the chain stops, outputs, the stages and the frozen
inputs) is in [the SOP](../sop/MethylPhys_CPG_SOP_v4.md), section 5b for before a run and the exit codes. How to read the report is in the
report itself and in [the Decoder](OM_v4_decoder.md).

Two statements this chapter made were out of date when it was retired, and are corrected in the SOP: a 450K array is read by the atlas readers
(it was described as refused), and healthy comes only from the atlases (it described same-run healthy references). The chapter's earlier text
is in the repository's history.
