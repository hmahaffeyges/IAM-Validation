## Why the chain works: IAM's physics

Every reading this chain makes rests on one physical fact. A methyl mark on DNA is a stored record, and keeping a record against heat
costs energy. Landauer's principle sets the least energy that can hold one bit against thermal noise. IAM's Law is that this price
governs every physical record: a qubit, a transistor, the large-scale structure of the universe, and a methyl mark. Two consequences
make the instruments possible.

1. **The scale is derived, not learned.** The energy a cell spends holding a mark sets the least error it can reach: the floor. A site
   whose mark is lost entirely reads as a coin flip, one bit of disorder: the ceiling. Counting single DNA molecules in 56 healthy human
   cell types, the energy that holds a methyl mark is 3.08 k_B T, about 0.147 of the ATP the cell pays to keep each site (M = 20.94 k_B T). The restore odds behind that share follow the same rule as a slowly ramped write; restored suddenly, a mark would be a coin flip.
   A healthy cell sits at a measured place between the floor and the ceiling, so a reading in bits means the same thing in any
   laboratory, for any cell type, before anything has been trained. A candidate derives that place from M and the price of the methyl
   donor (one SAM costs about 2.0 ATP): restoring paid against thermal kicks gives a held error of
   0.0445 and 3.07 k_B T, against the measured 0.0453 (candidate, not yet tested).
2. **Disorder is counted, and counting has exact statistics.** A sample's disorder at a site is the binary entropy of its measured level,
   and the disorder that heat and counting alone would give at that site's number of reads follows exactly. So every instrument sets a
   measured disorder beside an expectation derived from the physics and from the sample's own reads. Nothing is fitted to the sample.

**How each part of the chain uses it.**
- **Met-A** reads a sample's disorder at its cells' identity sites against the disorder healthy cells of the same kind carry there, on
  the floor-to-ceiling scale; 1 is healthy.
- **IAM-A** reads the held error on single molecules against what the cell's own loosely held share predicts, on a line whose form comes
  from the holding physics. The sample is its own reference.
- **The C-Score** asks whether departures from the expected disorder are scattered along the genome, as ordinary wear is, or clustered,
  as when a whole region loses its holding.
- **The territories** make the same comparison at the islands every healthy cell holds unmethylated and the domains some cell types hold
  loosely.
- **Coupling-A** asks whether neighboring marks on one molecule were lost together, against its counted floor and ceiling.
- **The tumor gauge** reads the distance from the measured disorder to the entropy ceiling.
- **Group-A** asks whether a whole group has moved against the healthy samples of its own series.
- **The atlases and the IAM-Deconvolver are the rulers.** They say which healthy cells a sample is made of and where healthy disorder
  sits for each, so the physics can be applied to each person's own mix of cells.
- **The library floor.** Every laboratory's chemistry adds a small rate of false "methylated" calls. The chain measures it at the sites
  no healthy cell marks, and reads it as a property of the library, not of the cell.

**The chain's job:** one sample goes in; every commissioned instrument reads it inside its commissioned scope, reads outside it with a plain note saying it has not yet been tested there, or declines with its reason. Research use only; not a diagnostic test.

## 4b. What the chain works out for itself, and what you tell it

**The chain reads from the files:** the array type, from the IDAT header (450K, EPIC v1, EPIC v2); the platform of a values file, from its probe names and count; the array's sex, from its X and Y signals; and a sequencing file's genome build, from its name and its CpG index ranges (Stage Q0). Nothing has to be switched by hand for the chain to pick its stages.

**You tell it:** the specimen (`--specimen`); the file (`--grn` and `--red`, `--betas`, `--pat` or `--bam`); for sequencing, the cell type (`--seq-cell`). Nothing else: no healthy samples from the same run or laboratory are needed or used.

**Healthy references (author 2026-11-07: 'healthy for comparisons MUST always come from the atlas's ... never tare our instruments with anything other than what we can be certain is healthy').** There is no list of approved laboratories, and no reading is tared with same-run or same-laboratory healthy samples: a laboratory rarely has three healthy samples of the same cell, and samples labeled healthy cannot be trusted to be. Every reading is referenced to the sample itself, the atlas, or the law, and every laboratory or library difference is measured from the sample's own reads. On sequencing (the chain's own table, `stage_metaa_atlas.SEQ_REFERENCE_RULE`, also in every bundle as `reference_rule`):

| Reading | Referenced to |
|---|---|
| IAM-A | self: its own line (held error against its own loose share); no atlas, no tare |
| CP1 run lengths, islands meter, clock sites, copy timing, TERR/GLB line locality | self: the sample against itself (independent losses, the islands' own zero, late against early copied DNA, tile against tile) |
| IAM-A C-Score, whole-genome sequencing globe | self: each region against the sample's own held error |
| Deconvolver | atlas: which cells and their shares (identity only) |
| Coupling-A | atlas: the type's healthy coupling |
| Met-A sequencing (sorted cell, whole sample, each cell in the mix), Met-A-side C-Score, territories | atlas, in this sample's own Deconvolver mix; laboratory part from its own reads: the islands meter (its own false-mark rate), the library floor at the sites read, the expectation at its own read count; for an atlas library, the other sealed donor half (--dev-seq-ref-half, --dev-seq-half-cols) |
| IAM's Law Sphere | the law's expected point, with the sample's own line (hold against self) and the atlas angle beside it |
| legacy | the word 'untared' and the optional same-run tare (A / median of >= 3 same-run healthy samples) are left from the array method that this rule retired; on sequencing neither applies |

No sequencing reading is tared with same-run or same-laboratory healthy samples. The atlases are the only healthy reference; laboratory and library differences are measured from the sample's own reads.

### The three Met-A readings on a sequenced sample, and why they can differ on one library

Met-A asks whether a cell's methylation record has become more disordered than a healthy cell of the same type holds it (normalized methylation entropy against a cell-type-matched healthy reference). On a sequenced sample the chain reads it three ways. Each uses a different set of sites and a different healthy expectation, so on one library the three need not agree.

| Reading | Sites read | Healthy expectation | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sorted-cell Met-A | The identity sites of the library's own cell type (Met-A's site rule on IAM-Atlas v2.1) | That type's healthy people in the IAM Sequencing Atlas: each person's expected disorder at the sample's own read depth, averaged over the people, so the differences between healthy people enter as they are | Has this cell type's identity record moved? |
| Met-A for each cell in the mix | Each found cell's identity sites | The atlas mixed in the sample's own cell shares (IAM-Deconvolver) at those sites | In a mixed sample, which cell has moved? |
| Whole-sample Met-A | Every IAM-Atlas v2.1 site, each weighted by how firmly a healthy sample of this mix holds it, \|0.5 - e\| | The atlas mixed in the sample's own cell shares, each type's level pooled over its healthy people | Has the sample as a whole departed? |

**Why "whole sample" on a sorted library.** The name comes from blood, where a sample is a mix of cells. On a sorted library the same route still runs, with the IAM-Deconvolver's shares: most of the share goes to the library's own type and a few percent to close relatives (on a sorted heart-muscle library, for example, a few percent to lung epithelium and heart fibroblasts). Sorted-cell Met-A uses the library's own type only.

**Status.** Whole-sample Met-A is development. On healthy atlas libraries never used to build the reference it reads below sorted-cell Met-A in most types; two causes are being tested (the pooled healthy expectation, and the mix weights on a sorted library), and the measurements are in the development log.

### The Deconvolver and Met-A's tare on sorted sequenced samples

#### How the sequencing Deconvolver works (Stage D21 on the IAM Sequencing Atlas)

- **The rule:** Splits a sample into the 57 healthy cell types of the IAM Sequencing Atlas. For every marker CpG it solves the sample's level as a non-negative mix of the types' healthy levels (non-negative least squares), each site weighted by the atlas's person-to-person spread at that site times the current shares, plus a laboratory term; three passes, each re-weighting from the last shares. Shares are normalized to 1. 95 % intervals from 200 resamplings of the marker sites, each also drawing that person's cells from the atlas's spread (seed 2026).
- **Why it is needed:** Every reading that compares a sample with its healthy expectation needs to know which healthy cells it is made of. In whole blood, plasma and tissue the Deconvolver decides what is there: no cell is assumed absent (the rule of 2026-11-07).
- **Physics:** Not a step of IAM's Law: linear unmixing (a sample's level at a site is the share-weighted sum of its cells' levels). The cosmology analogue is component separation of foregrounds (credited: Tegmark, de Oliveira-Costa & Hamilton 2003, Phys. Rev. D 68:123523).
- **Where the data comes from:** deconv_reference_v1.parquet (chain Runtime Matrices/Seq_Atlas): for each of 5,699 marker CpGs (named by array probe; mapped to the sequencing CpG index by atlas/v2.1/sites_sequencing_key.parquet) each type's healthy level (|mu) and person-to-person spread (|sd), from the IAM Sequencing Atlas's own donors. Markers: for each type, the 50 sites where it sits most above and the 50 most below its NEAREST other type, in units of their combined spread (stage_deconv_v21.markers_compute, PER_CELL = 50). The sample's level and read count at each marker.
- **The numbers:** 57 types, 5,699 markers (2026-10-30 build: 56 types, 5,599 markers; megakaryocytes added later as an identification-only column). On the build day 8 of 9 planted mixes read their shares; the ninth gave 8 % to close relatives; a pure cell gives others 1-2 % from counting alone (development log 2026-10-30). On 25 pure never-used healthy libraries (2026-11-21): median non-main share 0.006, 95th percentile 0.053, largest 0.305 (spill25.csv).
- **Method and standard:** Weighted non-negative least squares (scipy.optimize.nnls) with iterative re-weighting; intervals by resampling (bootstrap). Error bars are labeled 'information, not calibrated'. A share outside its own 95 % range is flagged 'not resolved from its close relatives'.
- **Known-answer test:** Planted mixtures through the one button (2026-11-21, plantmix_14.csv): planted share recovered 0/0.043/0.134/0.159/0.216/0.320/0.522 (heart + liver) and 0/0.046/0.082/0.126/0.177/0.263/0.459 (oligodendrocytes + acinar) at 0/0.05/0.10/0.15/0.20/0.30/0.50.
- **Limits:** Near-identical types trade share (kidney tubular and glomerular epithelium: podocyte libraries read tubular 0.48-0.69 and glomerular 0.21-0.26); types missing from the atlas appear as spread 'ghost' shares; same laboratory as the atlas.
- **Where the result goes:** bundle cell_shares (each cell's share, 95 % interval, resampled median, status); used by Met-A, IAM-A in the mix, the mixture check, each cell's Met-A, the globe's weights, and the report.
- **Code:** stage_v4.stage_d21; stage_deconv_v21.deconvolve, _solve, markers, markers_compute (LAB_SD 0.02, PER_CELL 50, BOOT 200, SEED 2026); stage_seq_atlas.deconv_reference_of, DECONV_REF, DECONV_DROP
- **How to rebuild it:** From the IAM Sequencing Atlas's per-type levels and spreads: run markers_compute (50 above and 50 below the nearest other type per type), keep those sites' |mu and |sd as deconv_reference_v1.parquet; deconvolve each sample with stage_deconv_v21.deconvolve (seed 2026).

#### Met-A's tare on sequencing: the Deconvolver's spill inside a tissue family

- **The rule:** SORTED (isolated, purified) sequenced samples only; in whole blood, plasma and tissue Met-A keeps the Deconvolver's shares and no cell is assumed absent. Inside Met-A's healthy expectation: the Deconvolver's largest share is the main cell; a share in the main cell's tissue family (stage_0_intake.lineage_group) counts as the main cell; a cell in another family keeps its share only at or above SPILL_FLOOR = 0.05; megakaryocytes are left out (identification only); the rest is renormalized. The sorted label is recorded but never used: when it names a different type, the Deconvolver's call is used and the disagreement is flagged (tested: reading against the label's type moved Met-A away from 1). The Deconvolver, its reported shares, IAM-A in the mix and the mixture check are unchanged.
- **Why it is needed:** On healthy pure libraries the Deconvolver names the main cell but gives a few per cent to related references in the same tissue family (colon epithelium -> colon enteroendocrine 0.190; heart fibroblasts -> smooth muscle 0.238; tongue base -> tonsil palatine 0.305). Taken as real cells, those shares make Met-A's expected disorder too high at the sites where those references differ, so a healthy sample read low: median whole-sample Met-A 0.938 on 31 never-used healthy libraries (sorted-cell Met-A on the same libraries 1.004). In grid terms: the meter and relay are right; the setpoint was built with phantom load added in.
- **Physics:** Not a step of IAM's Law: a calibration of the reference that Met-A (the disorder of the record against its healthy expectation) is read against. Met-A's expectation is the entropy of the atlas mixed in the sample's shares; entropy is steep near 0 and 1, so a few per cent of a related type at a firmly held site raises the expected disorder much more than it changes the level (the reason small shares bias Met-A, recorded 2026-11-21).
- **Where the data comes from:** (1) The Deconvolver's shares for the sample (Stage D21, deconv_reference_v1, 5,699 marker CpGs, 57 types). (2) The chain's tissue families (stage_0_intake.lineage_group, the same map the mixture check uses). (3) SPILL_FLOOR from spill25.csv: every non-main share on 25 pure healthy libraries of atlas types (GSE186458, never used to build the atlas; real types from each GEO file name, lib49_real_types.csv).
- **The numbers:** Spill on the 25 pure libraries: 326 non-main shares, median 0.006, 95th percentile 0.053, largest 0.305; 15 of 19 shares of 0.05 or more stay in the main cell's family. Whole-sample Met-A on the 31 never-used atlas-type libraries: median 0.938 with the Deconvolver's shares, 1.016 with the rule; correlation with sorted-cell Met-A 0.18 -> 0.51; 27 of 31 within the GUM bar. Outside: GSM5652341 Prostate-Epithelial 0.865 (z -6.27, k 4.3); GSM5652237 Liver-Hepatocytes 1.200 (z 2.33, k 2.0); GSM5652330 Tongue_base-Epithelial 1.166 (z 4.84, k 4.3); GSM5652261 Kidney-Glomerular-Podocytes 1.208 (z 5.30, k 4.3).

## 4d. On GitHub's machines

**Public data.** Fork the repository, turn on Actions, and run `series` (a list of GEO arrays) or `reproduce_all` (every reproduction). Each job runs up to six hours; up to twenty jobs run at once.

**Your own samples.** A fork of a public repository stays public, so for samples that are not public make a private copy (GitHub's Import repository, or a copy pushed to a new private repository). Put each array's two IDAT files in one folder, commit, and run `my_samples` with that folder and the specimen (no healthy references are needed; the legacy array same-run CSV is retired). Every array is read by `run_sample.py`, unchanged; the reports, bundles and ledgers come back as the run's artifacts. A private repository uses the account's included Actions minutes. Tested on a public array (GSM7508429): one array took about 18 minutes on its machine, most of it installing the chain's packages, and read the same Met-A and C-Score as the committed example report.

## 5. What the chain declines, and why

- An EPIC v2 array: refused at intake with one sentence (`PLATFORM_REFUSED`; Stage 1, methylprep 1.7.1, does not calibrate EPIC v2).
- A 450K array is read: the atlas readers (the IAM-Deconvolver, and Met-A and the C-Score against IAM-Atlas v2.1) read it by site name, flagged with their own coverage; only the earlier same-run readers, built on EPIC v1 references, do not read it.
- A sequencing file on another genome build than hg19: stopped at intake, by its name before any line is read, then by its CpG index ranges.
- A failed array (Stage 0 quarantine): exit 2, no report.
- A sequencing sample whose shape is outside the healthy shape of its declared cell type, or with too few qualifying molecules: IAM-A declines and says why.
- A noisy array (the noise gate): the Met-A state is withheld and no gauge is drawn.

## 5b. Operating: before a run, and when the chain stops

Folded in from the operations manual (manual/OM_v4_chain.md, retired into this SOP on 2026-11-12).

**Before a run.**

1. Run the release check once per installation and after every update: `python3 release_check_v4.py`. Every check must pass.
2. Arrays: the Illumina manifest must be in `$HOME/.methylprep_manifest_files/` (methylprep fetches it on first use). Read the first array of a batch alone, so the download is not raced.
3. Sequencing: give the cell type with `--seq-cell`, and for a BAM the exact reference FASTA it was aligned to (`--ref-fasta`, indexed).
4. Declare the specimen (`--specimen`): whole blood, buffy coat, isolated, sorted or purified neutrophils, isolated, sorted or purified cells of any IAM-Atlas v2.1 type, or a constructed DNA mixture of blood cells (test material). Anything else is refused at intake with its reason.
5. Healthy comes only from the atlases: no same-run or same-laboratory healthy references are needed.

**When the chain stops.**

| exit | printed | meaning | what to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | the report | read, or declined with a reason printed in the report (specimen, platform, sample shape) | read the report |
| 1 | an argument message | a command-line input is missing or malformed | correct the command |
| 2 | `QUARANTINE` and its code | Stage 0 failed the file (corrupt or truncated IDAT, missing channel, array type mismatch, low detection) | nothing is scored; check the file, rerun from the scanner output |
| 3 | `ENVIRONMENT_MISSING_MANIFEST` or `GENOME_COVERAGE_REFUSED` | the Illumina manifest could not be fetched, or a sequencing file covers too little of the genome | put the manifest in place, or supply a fuller file; the sample was not judged |
| 7 | `REFERENCE_REFUSED` | an atlas file does not match its published checksum | restore the atlas files from their Zenodo record; nothing was read |

The commissioning tests and the headline results rebuild from public data with one command each: `Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/reproduce/README.md`.

## 7c. How the references are split and loaded

Generated from the reference files when this document is built. `chain/Runtime Matrices/` holds 843.0 MB; `atlas/` holds 512.8 MB. A copy of the repository carries every reference. ## 9. Rules

- Synthetic first: every new test runs on planted data, read by the chain's exact rule, before any real data is opened.
- Sealed before read: a test's samples, rule and bars are committed before its data is downloaded; it is scored once.
- Clean clone: every chain change passes `release_check_v4.py` on a fresh clone, on a clean machine, before it is called tested.
- One reading per instrument; no lines beyond Normal are printed until they are measured on the gauge.

The earlier procedure, for chain v3, is kept as the record of how the chain was built: [MethylPhys_CPG_SOP_v3.md](MethylPhys_CPG_SOP_v3.md) (with its full version); neither is updated after 2026-11-12. This SOP is the one operating procedure.


## 10. Every instrument's record

Generated from the instruments database (manual/instrument_records/), never typed here: for each sequencing instrument, what it is in the field's terms, how it measures, where IAM's Law enters, the tool it borrows, its credit, each rule's full record, the questions a researcher will ask with their answers and evidence, and its files in the repository. Every input and output, with all their fields, is in the Decoder.

### 0. Intake

- **In the field's terms:** Sample quality control: genome build, bisulfite conversion, CpG coverage, specimen
- **Reads from:** both: reads either input and says which it received
- **What it measures and how:** Checks that reads are aligned to hg19, that bisulfite conversion is at least 98 %, and CpG coverage chromosome by chromosome; records the specimen; refuses a damaged or incomplete file with a stated reason. Nothing is read until the file passes.
- **Where IAM's Law enters:** Not the law: instrument or meter.
- **Tool borrowed from cosmology:** Data-quality masks: a sky survey flags bad fields before anything is measured.
- **Credit:** IAM's own intake rules. Bisulfite conversion is measured the standard way from non-CpG cytosines; the alignment reference is the UCSC hg19 build (Genome Reference Consortium GRCh37). The quality-mask idea is borrowed from survey astronomy, where it is common practice rather than one person's method.

#### Intake: accept or refuse the file before anything is read

*Status:* runs; development for sequencing

- **How it works:** Before any reading: the file must be readable and complete, on the hg19 build, with bisulfite conversion of at least 0.98, an accepted specimen word, at least 100,000 opportunities, and genome coverage on every chromosome. A failing file is refused with a plain reason (exit code 3, INTAKE_REFUSED), and no reading is shown.
- **Why:** A reading from a damaged, partial or wrong-build file would look like biology; refusing it plainly protects every instrument downstream.
- **Physics:** Not IAM's Law: quality control.
- **Where the data comes from:** The sample file; the specimen word; intake_thresholds_v2.json (Runtime Matrices/Intake).
- **The numbers:** Conversion >= 0.98; opportunities >= 100,000. Relay test: a truncated file stopped with a raw error, fixed by a full-file check in a later chain update.
- **Method and standard:** Fixed limits from measured QC.
- **Known-answer test:** Relay test (2026-11-20): a truncated molecule file is refused with a plain reason after a chain update (partly met before).
- **Limits:** (1) Limits are operational, not derived. (2) Specimen words decide the route (sorted, whole blood, plasma, tissue), not which cells are present.
- **Where the result goes:** bundle intake checks and limits; refusal code and reason.
- **Code:** stage_0_intake (specimen rules, thresholds, refusals); run_sample.py intake block (a chain update)
- **How to rebuild it:** Apply the listed checks in order; refuse on the first failure with its reason.
- **History:** Dated entries in the development log (METHYLPHYS_DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md) and the instrument's development record.

### 3. Deconvolver and the mixture check

- **In the field's terms:** Reference-based cell-type deconvolution: composition, purity and cell of origin

- **Tool borrowed from cosmology:** Component separation: the cosmic microwave background is separated from galactic foregrounds by their known spectra; here each cell type is a component with a known methylation spectrum.

#### How the sequencing Deconvolver works (Stage D21 on the IAM Sequencing Atlas)

*Status:* development on sequencing (the six-cell blood panel is commissioned on arrays)

- **How it works:** Splits a sample into the 57 healthy cell types of the IAM Sequencing Atlas. For every marker CpG it solves the sample's level as a non-negative mix of the types' healthy levels (non-negative least squares), each site weighted by the atlas's person-to-person spread at that site times the current shares, plus a laboratory term; three passes, each re-weighting from the last shares. Shares are normalized to 1. 95 % intervals from 200 resamplings of the marker sites, each also drawing that person's cells from the atlas's spread (seed 2026).
- **Why:** Every reading that compares a sample with its healthy expectation needs to know which healthy cells it is made of. In whole blood, plasma and tissue the Deconvolver decides what is there: no cell is assumed absent (the rule of 2026-11-07).
- **Physics:** Not a step of IAM's Law: linear unmixing (a sample's level at a site is the share-weighted sum of its cells' levels). The cosmology analogue is component separation of foregrounds (credited: Tegmark, de Oliveira-Costa & Hamilton 2003, Phys. Rev. D 68:123523).
- **Where the data comes from:** deconv_reference_v1.parquet (chain Runtime Matrices/Seq_Atlas): for each of 5,699 marker CpGs (named by array probe; mapped to the sequencing CpG index by atlas/v2.1/sites_sequencing_key.parquet) each type's healthy level (/mu) and person-to-person spread (/sd), from the IAM Sequencing Atlas's own donors. Markers: for each type, the 50 sites where it sits most above and the 50 most below its NEAREST other type, in units of their combined spread (stage_deconv_v21.markers_compute, PER_CELL = 50). The sample's level and read count at each marker.
- **The numbers:** 57 types, 5,699 markers (2026-10-30 build: 56 types, 5,599 markers; megakaryocytes added later as an identification-only column). On the build day 8 of 9 planted mixes read their shares; the ninth gave 8 % to close relatives; a pure cell gives others 1-2 % from counting alone (development log 2026-10-30). On 25 pure never-used healthy libraries (2026-11-21): median non-main share 0.006, 0.95 quantile 0.053, largest 0.305 (spill25.csv).
- **Method and standard:** Weighted non-negative least squares (scipy.optimize.nnls) with iterative re-weighting; intervals by resampling (bootstrap). Error bars are labeled 'information, not calibrated'. A share outside its own 95 % range is flagged 'not resolved from its close relatives'.
- **Known-answer test:** Planted mixtures through the one button (2026-11-21, plantmix_14.csv): planted share recovered 0/0.043/0.134/0.159/0.216/0.320/0.522 (heart + liver) and 0/0.046/0.082/0.126/0.177/0.263/0.459 (oligodendrocytes + acinar) at 0/0.05/0.10/0.15/0.20/0.30/0.50.

### 4. IAM-A

- **In the field's terms:** Maintenance-methylation fidelity: read-level discordance on fully methylated molecules, against the sample's own expectation
- **Reads from:** molecules (each read followed CpG by CpG; needs a molecule file)
- **What it measures and how:** On every read at least 80 % methylated, counts the isolated unmethylated CpGs (an unmethylated CpG between two methylated neighbors): the held error. The sample's own share of loosely held (intermediate) CpGs predicts the held error a healthy cell of that looseness makes, from a line measured on healthy cells. IAM-A is the observed held error over that prediction: 1 is healthy; above 1 means marks are being lost on molecules the cell holds firmly, the footprint of failed maintenance after DNA replication (DNMT1 with UHRF1).
- **Where IAM's Law enters:** IAM's Law, step 1 (Actualization): a sudden write of an einselected pointer into a blank Gaussian surface splits the energy exactly: held = spent = U = k_BT times the two relative entropies. Status: DERIVED GIVEN the four write conditions (sudden, no opposing record, Gaussian, einselected). IAM's Law, step 1d (The maintained hold (the slow write)): held/lost = (x/2)/(1 - (1 - e^-x)/x); sudden x -> 0 kept = lost; the cell at x = 2M: odds 21.45, E_hold 3.066 kT, phi = 0.1464 ~ ln M/M. Status: DERIVED (identity). IAM's Law, step 2 (The price): each nat costs at least k_BT held and k_BT spent; k_BT ln 2 per bit is the floor, paid at the receiving surface's own T. Status: DERIVED (Landauer) for the floor. IAM's Law, step 2b (The funding): the payment is free energy degraded to heat, traced to the low-entropy start (ATP <- food <- sunlight; stars <- fusion and contraction). Status: DERIVED given the Past Hypothesis, which is a postulate of established physics too (gap 16, not closable).
- **Tool borrowed from cosmology:** Self-calibration: each sample is its own standard candle, read against the line its own loose share predicts.
- **Credit:** IAM (this work): the held error read from the sample's own molecules and its line. Uncertainty and coverage: Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (2008) JCGM 100:2008, Evaluation of measurement data: Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement (GUM 1995 with minor corrections), BIPM.

#### IAM-A: how it tares (against the sample's own line) and how it is defended

*Status:* commissioned on healthy blood CD8 T cells (WGBS, second laboratory, 2026-10-17); development on other types

- **How it works:** On every read at least 80 % methylated (a held molecule), count each unmethylated CpG with methylated neighbors on both sides (an isolated loss) and the chances for one: the held error. Count the sample's share of loosely held molecules: the loose share. The healthy line predicts the held error of a healthy cell of that looseness: held = 0.0403 + 0.0346 x loose share (commissioned line). IAM-A = observed held error / predicted. A_derived uses the derived line (intercept 0.04454 from M alone). Refused for mixtures (then IAM-A in the mix, development).
- **Why:** A held molecule is one the cell actively maintains, so a loss on it is a maintenance failure (DNMT1 with UHRF1 after replication), not a cell-type difference. Reading against the sample's own loose share removes the cell type's and the laboratory's general level, so no healthy samples from the same run are needed (the 2026-11-07 reference rule) and every sample is its own standard.
- **Physics:** IAM's Law in the cell (links 1, 1d, 2, 2b): the floor of held error is where thermal kicks beat one full coin, 1/(1 + e^M), M = dG_ATP / RT = 20.94 (derived); one restore costs one SAM = 2 ATP (derived, adenylate kinase at equilibrium); the best hold two coins buy is a constant push (derived ceiling); hence the held error from M alone, 0.04454 per site (derived intercept). The slope is partly derived (its within-sample part from the pump; replicated in a second laboratory). Each cell type's own level is not derived: it is measured from the atlas's healthy libraries and used as a substitute, labeled as such.
- **Where the data comes from:** The sample's molecules (pat file: one row per read and its CpG pattern) - nothing else. The line constants 0.0403 / 0.0346 and the derived intercept 0.04454 from the chain (stage_q2_selfref). For A_derived's state: each type's measured healthy position (type_positions_v1.json, 56 types, from the IAM Sequencing Atlas's healthy libraries, GSE186458).
- **The numbers:** Band v1 (commissioned): Normal 0.7787-1.0551. Commissioning test, a laboratory IAM-A had never seen (GSE144691, sorted human CD8 T-cell subsets): 7 of 8 read samples Normal against a sealed bar of 0.75 with at least 8 read; the shape check declined 5 of 13 (four naive, one central memory). Band v2 (pooled, in commissioning): 0.8744-1.1783 from 150 atlas readings; pooled within-type spread s = 0.035 (94 df). Per-type band (the substitute): 113 of 123 healthy libraries inside with each donor left out (91.9 %, built for 95 %); out of sample, 40 of 41 healthy libraries that built neither band within (hold alarm, jackknife floor).
- **Method and standard:** Per-type band = the type's healthy center +/- t(0.975, df) x s x sqrt(1 + 1/n) (a Student's t prediction interval for one new library; GUM type A). The out-of-sample floor is a block jackknife over the Law Sphere's units (it measured spread about four times plain counting noise). Bars come from measured spread, never a chosen number.
- **Known-answer test:** Relay test (2026-11-20, test library GSM5652214, through the one button): planted single losses on held molecules moved IAM-A and no other instrument's main reading (partly met: the counter's pooled run ratio was blind to planted runs, fixed by a chain update run excess by length). - **Limits:** (1) Each type's own healthy level is measured, not derived: IAM-A_derived's state rests on that substitute. (3) Commissioned only on blood CD8 T cells; other types are development. (4) Needs reads long enough to hold three CpGs; shallow libraries carry more counting error (reported). ### 11. IAM-A C-Score (molecules: clumping of held errors)

#### Met-A on sequencing: its three readings, its healthy reference and how it is defended

*Status:* development; never commissioned on sequencing (bars from held-out pass 9 owed)

- **How it works:** Met-A = the sample's methylation disorder (Shannon entropy of the CpG's methylated fraction) over the disorder a healthy cell of the same composition shows at the same read depth. Three readings: sorted-cell Met-A (the library's own type's identity sites, against each healthy person of that type separately, averaged); Met-A for each cell in the mix (each found cell's identity sites against the atlas in the sample's shares); whole-sample Met-A (every IAM-Atlas v2.1 site, 790,761 in the test library, weighted /0.5 - e/, against the atlas in the sample's shares). 1 is healthy; above 1 the record is more disordered than healthy holds it, below 1 more ordered.
- **Why:** A cell that loses control of its record shows it first as disorder at sites a healthy cell holds firmly; comparing with the same type's healthy people at the same read depth removes the counting noise that depth alone would add, so a shallow library does not look disordered.
- **Physics:** IAM's Law in the cell (link 1d, the maintained hold): the healthy level at held sites is set by the same hold as IAM-A's floor, 1/(1 + e^M) with M = 20.94; the gauge's floor and ceiling are derived (M1). Met-A reads the record's disorder; IAM-A reads its losses on held molecules: two views of the same hold.
- **Where the data comes from:** The sample's level and read count at each site (from its molecules). The IAM Sequencing Atlas (GSE186458, Zenodo record): each type's healthy level per person, the sealed donor halves and two noise maps (map 1 donor halves, map 2 read halves); the identity-site rule (healthy spread <= 0.05; mean 0.75-0.95 or 0.05-0.25; at most 3,000 per side). The Deconvolver's shares for the mix readings, through Met-A's tare on sorted samples.
- **The numbers:** Held-out pass 8 (each of 137 atlas libraries read against its other donor half): whole sample 0.990 +/- 0.177; sorted cell and own cell in the mix measured (bucket bands8/held_out_pass8.csv). - **Method and standard:** Expected disorder computed at the sample's own read count at each site (counting noise modeled: exact draws, the counted expectation of 2026-11-20, common depth at most 20). Healthy bars by the 2026-11-07 rule: 1 is the middle; each type's width from its held-out person-to-person spread, Student's t coverage for few libraries (GUM). #### Information sphere: the sample's distance from its own expected healthy self, by groups of sites

*Status:* development

- **How it works:** Sites are placed on a sphere by how they behave across the atlas's healthy cell types (each site's square-root level vector, its departure from a flat profile at its own mean level, the three leading principal directions; frozen layout v5). #### IAM's ratings methodology: per-unit readings and one observed normal operating range per instrument (adopted from power-system practice)

*Status:* designed 2026-11-21; built after held-out pass 9

- **How it works:** Every IAM score is reported per unit of its own healthy base: the instrument is first CENTERED (its healthy expectation corrected so healthy lands on its healthy point; an offset is never absorbed into a wider band), then TARED (divided by its measured healthy center; one center per instrument, or one per cell type where the types' centers differ by more than their noise, as for IAM-A), so healthy reads 1.00 pu. Each score carries ONE band: the observed normal operating range, the range 95 % of healthy controls are expected to fall in, from the pooled within-type spread of the healthy controls (Student's t coverage), widened for the sample's own read depth. A reading outside it is flagged: 'Outside our observed normal operating range (based on a limited set of healthy controls)'. No further tiers: where danger begins is not ours to set. The band's basis (number of healthy libraries and cell types) is printed beside it, and the raw measurement in field terms beside the score.
- **Why:** A per-unit base lets readings on different scales be compared at a glance; one observed normal operating range says only what healthy controls show, like a laboratory reference range or a historical (PI) operating range, without claiming where harm begins. Per-type ranges from 2-3 libraries would be wider, not narrower (Student's t with 1-2 degrees of freedom), so the spread is pooled within types and each type keeps its own center only where its offset is resolved.
- **Physics:** Not a step of IAM's Law: a measurement and reporting convention. Where an instrument's healthy point is derived (IAM-A's floor and line, the C-Score's counting limit), the raw reading against the derived point is kept beside the per-unit reading, because that distance is the physics test (for IAM-A, the open question of each type's own level).
- **Where the data comes from:** Each instrument's raw reading and its counting uncertainty; each cell type's healthy center and person-to-person spread from held-out pass 9 (Student's t standard error of the center for few libraries); the sample's read depth.
- **The numbers:** Pending pass 9. Pass 8 (before Met-A's mix rule) for orientation: whole-sample Met-A 0.990 +/- 0.177, domains 0.993 +/- 0.234, islands 1.011 +/- 0.327.
- **Method and standard:** Per-unit system of power-system analysis (quantities as fractions of a defined base). Tier boundaries from the measured spread by the GUM (JCGM 100:2008): combined standard uncertainty = person-to-person spread, the tare point's standard error and the sample's counting uncertainty in quadrature; coverage k = 2 and k = 3 (Student's t for few libraries). Structured as a documented ratings methodology in the manner NERC requires of facility ratings (FAC-008: a documented methodology, ambient conditions, at least Normal and Emergency ratings) and operating limits (FAC-011): our counterpart of ambient conditions is read depth.
- **Known-answer test:** Owed: on the 31 never-used atlas-type libraries, healthy libraries fall Within healthy at about the stated coverage; planted departures cross the tiers in order.
- **Limits:** The flag means outside the observed healthy range and nothing more; the report's claim scan applies. Readings of other kinds of samples are separate observations and never set a range. The per-unit and ratings structure is a method adopted from power-system practice, not a claim that NERC reviews IAM. Instruments not yet centered (run lengths, the Law Sphere's level alarm) get no range until they are. Ranges are provisional while the healthy controls are few (pass 9: 137 libraries, 48 types, 2-3 per type; never-used check 26 of 31 inside).
- **Where the result goes:** Every score's per-unit reading, tier, healthy range for the type at this depth, and raw measurement in field terms: the report's results table, the SOP and the Decoder.
- **Where on the DNA:** Applies to each place's reading as well as the whole sample, once the where stage exists.
- **Code:** (to be built: the final tare and ratings after pass 9)
- **How to rebuild it:** From pass 9's per-type centers and spreads; the formula above.
- **History:** Dated entries in the development log (METHYLPHYS_DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md) and the instrument's development record.

*Questions a researcher will ask:*

- **What exactly is this test comparing?** It compares a later sample from a person against an earlier sample from that same person, looking at every CpG site that both samples happened to measure. *(Evidence: instrument record: field_how)*
- **Why use the person's own earlier sample instead of comparing against a panel of healthy people?** Because the person acts as their own reference point, so a separate healthy reference group isn't needed for this comparison. *(Evidence: instrument record: rule 'why' explanation)*
- **Is this comparison based on some established statistical law?** No. It is a comparison rule of the pipeline: the person's earlier sample serves as the reference; IAM's Law does not enter it. *(Evidence: instrument record: law field)*
- **What are the limitations of this method that I should know about before relying on it?** Not yet recorded: the limits of serial comparison (how far apart the samples may be, and how depth differences are handled) have not been written for this instrument. *(Evidence: instrument record: rule 'limits' field)*

### 22. The Locator (where it changed: places and genes)

- **In the field's terms:** Differential methylation at regions and genes (DMRs and DMGs), called per sample against a healthy reference, with gene annotation
- **Reads from:** both: places for the molecule instruments come from molecules, places for the counts instruments from counts
- **What it measures and how:** Per-place observed versus expected counts against an empirical healthy null with Benjamini-Hochberg control, annotated with CpG island, gene and promoter/gene-body/nearby relation from UCSC hg19 tracks

#### The Locator: naming the places that changed, and the genes there

*Status:* development

- **How it works:** For each instrument, every place it reads (an island or block for the Law Sphere and the counts; a region for the globe) is tested: the sample's count at the place against the count the healthy atlas expects there at the sample's own read depth and cell mix, judged against the spread measured among healthy libraries (not counting alone), with Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery control across that instrument's places. Each place named is given its chromosome and position, its CpG island context (island, shore, shelf, open sea), its genes (promoter: within 2 kb of a transcription start; gene body; or the nearest gene and its distance), and whether any array probe covers it.
- **Why:** A score says how much a sample changed; a researcher also needs to know where, and which genes, to compare with expression, with other studies and across patients.
- **The numbers:** 592,067 places (CpG islands, then 50-CpG blocks) and 28,218 regions; places by RefSeq relation: promoter 104,607, gene body 254,895, intergenic 232,565; by context: island 95,089, shore 35,892, shelf 21,335, open sea 439,751; 18,755 promoter places without an array probe. Planted tests on GSM5652214: 9 of 10 gain islands found, loss blocks named at 6.3 times their healthy level and above, none below 3.3; no place named in any healthy or unplanted read (every loss and gain unit tested).
- **Method and standard:** Positions reference built from public UCSC hg19 files and checked against all 813,951 array probe positions; healthy null by leave-one-out over the 205 atlas libraries; planted-place tests for detection.
- **Limits:** Detection depends on depth: at about 30-fold, a loss block expecting about 5 held errors is named once it shows about 4 to 6 times that (finding ); each report prints its own sample's limit, so 'nothing found' is read against it. Promoter calls depend on the gene table (both kept). Positions are hg19.
- **How to rebuild it:** python3 'chain/Runtime Matrices/Where/build_where_ref.py' hg19.fa.gz cpgIslandExt.txt.gz refGene.txt.gz sites_sequencing_key.parquet units.npz OUT; then build_where_genes_v2.py OUT refGene.txt.gz knownGene.txt.gz kgXref.txt.gz cpgIslandExt.txt.gz OUT (public UCSC hg19 files from hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu; both builds check themselves and stop on any disagreement)
- **Files in the repository:** [build_where_ref.py](https://github.com/hmahaffeyges/IAM-Validation/blob/main/Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/chain/Runtime%20Matrices/Where/build_where_ref.py), [build_where_genes_v2.py](https://github.com/hmahaffeyges/IAM-Validation/blob/main/Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/chain/Runtime%20Matrices/Where/build_where_genes_v2.py), [README.md](https://github.com/hmahaffeyges/IAM-Validation/blob/main/Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/chain/Runtime%20Matrices/Where/README.md), [where_ref_v2.json](https://github.com/hmahaffeyges/IAM-Validation/blob/main/Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/chain/Runtime%20Matrices/Where/where_ref_v2.json)

*Questions a researcher will ask:*

- **How do you know a named place is not just noise?** Each place is judged against how far healthy libraries actually stray there (measured on the 205 atlas libraries), not against counting alone, with false-discovery control. In every healthy and unplanted read so far, no place was named; planted places were found (9 of 10 planted gains; loss blocks at 6.3 times their healthy level and above). *(Evidence: findings)*
- **Why do the gene calls follow one study's definitions?** So named genes compare directly with that study's published gene lists: promoter within 2 kb of a transcription start, shores within 2 kb of an island, shelves 2 to 4 kb. Both RefSeq and the UCSC known-genes table are given, since the second calls more promoters. *(Evidence: a chain update; finding)*
- **What does 'array-blind' mean for a gene?** No array probe covers that place, so no array study could have measured it. 18,755 of the Locator's promoter places (RefSeq) have no probe. *(Evidence: finding)*
