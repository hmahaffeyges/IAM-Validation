# Met-A on sorted neutrophils (450K) and sorted monocytes (EPIC v1), against IAM-Atlas v2.1 — commissioning note

**Status: COMMISSIONED 2026-11-09** on the sealed test COMMISSION-METAA-SORTED-01 (all three bars met; `COMMISSION_METAA_SORTED_01_OUTCOME.md`).

**Reference:** IAM-Atlas v2.1 alone: each cell's healthy profile, and Normal as its measured width on the atlas's own donors.

**Reference sets:** IAM-Atlas v2.1's own neutrophil and monocyte donors, from GSE103541, GSE118144, GSE122244, GSE124565, GSE224807, GSE226298, GSE252045, GSE49618, GSE88824 (their widths; `atlas_donor_readings.csv`, `atlas_donors_sources.csv`); no reference set is taken from the samples read.

**Synthetic test:** chain/stage_metaa_atlas.py, chain/MethylPhys_Interface/run_sample.py, DEV_METAA_HEALTHY_01/width_new3_summary.txt

**Prior reading:** GSE110554 (the rehearsal) and the atlas's donor series GSE103541, GSE118144, GSE122244, GSE124565, GSE224807, GSE226298, GSE252045, GSE49618, GSE88824 (read to measure the widths and build the atlas) were read before (`CANON/data_spent.csv`); the sealed test's 79 arrays were never read before it.

**Milestone:** the first instrument commissioned against the atlas alone.

**In plain words.** Met-A reads healthy sorted neutrophils and monocytes as Normal, against the atlas alone, in laboratories the atlas never
held. To finish the rest of Met-A's commissioning: lymphocytes, neutrophils on EPIC and cells inside whole blood must each pass a sealed test
of the same kind, after the cross-laboratory work.

**What it would take** to finish: those three sealed tests met; nothing more is needed for the scope commissioned here.

## Scope
Met-A for the cell the IAM-Deconvolver finds most of, in a sorted neutrophil sample on Infinium 450K or a sorted monocyte sample on Infinium
EPIC v1, read by the one command's default chain against IAM-Atlas v2.1 alone. Normal is that cell's measured width on the atlas's own
donors (`Runtime Matrices/Met_A_Widths/donor_widths_v1.json`, `stage_metaa_atlas.width_state`), centered on 1. Nothing is taken from other
samples, runs or laboratories. Everything else stays development: other cells, other platforms, cells inside mixed samples, sequencing.

## What the reading says when it cannot read
Arrays of poor quality are declined with the reason (cell shares withheld below the marker-site floor; Met-A not read when fewer than half
of the cell's identity sites are measured), and a cell found below the share at which its width was measured gets no state, with the
share named.

**Prior first commissioning.** Met-A on isolated neutrophils (EPIC v1) was first commissioned 2026-10-09 against a same-run healthy tare
(`COMMISSIONING_NOTE_METAA_NEUTROPHILS.md`); that reference is retired, and EPIC neutrophils are not in this note's scope.
