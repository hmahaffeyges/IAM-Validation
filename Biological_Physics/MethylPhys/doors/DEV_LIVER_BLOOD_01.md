# DEV-LIVER-BLOOD-01 — liver cancer in blood DNA, and recurrence after transplant (candidates; metadata only, 2026-10-12; NOT designed)

**Why (author, 2026-10-12).** The goal is a blood test that sees liver cancer early, and the question of whether the disorder behind it is
systemic. Two routes: (1) the cancer's fingerprint in plasma cell-free DNA; (2) whether blood cells of cancer patients read lower fidelity than
healthy people's (commissioned Met-A on isolated neutrophils), worded as an open question, not a claim.

**Search** (metadata only; full table, counts and the search's own limits: `data/DEV_LIVER_BLOOD_01/candidates_search_2026-10-12.json`).
- **Recurrence after liver transplant:** no public raw data found. The one cell-free DNA methylation set that follows liver-cancer patients after
  transplant (Toronto, cfMeDIP; EGAS50000000450) is controlled access; counts are from the paper, not a repository query. A laboratory holding it
  could test an IAM prediction on it: a natural entry for the open-prediction list.
- **Liver cancer against healthy plasma, raw data public:** GSE149438 (bisulfite sequencing, HCC and normal plasma among other GI cancers) is the
  closest to what both instruments read; GSE63775 and GSE124775 read only selected sites; GSE93203 is a targeted panel of a few hundred sites;
  GSE129374 (450K plasma) has no healthy donors. GSE298812 is already in storage.
- **Before diagnosis:** no public plasma set sampled before liver cancer was diagnosed was found.

**Order.** After lung and liver tissue, one cancer at a time. Plasma needs a healthy plasma reference and a detection-limit test first (to-do B6, B8).
**Not a milestone:** candidates (development).
