# PROC-EPIC-01 — three rendered runs

One execution of the commissioned chain per specimen, rendered through the report interface rather than
scored through the stage functions, so a reader can see what the procedure's arrays actually look like.

**Each is the MEDIAN member of its group, not the most extreme.** A report chosen for having the highest
reading would misrepresent the breast group in particular, whose whole result is that it sits *below*
controls. The first render of these three did pick the maximum, and was replaced.

EPIC-Italy has no commissioned laboratory zero, so every one of these reports withholds absolute
placement and the tier word, exactly as it should. A' below is the comparison quantity the procedure
uses — A mapped minus the commissioned age term — and is not printed in the report itself.

| report | array | what it is | A' |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`MethylPhys_GSM1235738_methylation_RUN-20260925-02.html`](MethylPhys_GSM1235738_methylation_RUN-20260925-02.html) | GSM1235738 | a healthy control | 0.9099 |
| [`MethylPhys_GSM1235762_methylation_RUN-20260925-01.html`](MethylPhys_GSM1235762_methylation_RUN-20260925-01.html) | GSM1235762 | a colorectal case, blood drawn 6.8 years before diagnosis | 0.9261 |
| [`MethylPhys_GSM1236129_methylation_RUN-20260925-03.html`](MethylPhys_GSM1236129_methylation_RUN-20260925-03.html) | GSM1236129 | a breast case, blood drawn 10.4 years before diagnosis | 0.8890 |

The control's A' of 0.9099 is the group median; the colorectal case sits above it and the breast case
below, which is the whole finding in three specimens.
