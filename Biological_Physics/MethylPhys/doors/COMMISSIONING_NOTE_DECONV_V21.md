# The deconvolution tool (atlas v2.1): commissioned for blood-cell shares, 2026-10-17

**Status: COMMISSIONED 2026-10-17** for the shares of neutrophils, monocytes, CD4 T cells and CD8 T cells in blood-cell mixtures read on
arrays against IAM-Atlas v2.1 (chain `Biological_Physics/MethylPhys/chain/stage_deconv_v21.py`, version 2). Every other cell and use is development.

**The tool's job:** given a mixed sample, report how much of each cell is in it.

**The evidence (both sealed before scoring, each scored once):**

| test | mixtures | share error (median absolute) | bar | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEV-DECONV-V21-02 (version 2, the tool commissioned) | 200, from E-MTAB-2145, a laboratory the atlas never saw: neutrophils, monocytes, CD4 T, CD8 T | **0.016** | at most 0.03 | met |
| DEV-DECONV-V21-01 (version 1) | 200, six blood cells from independent laboratories | **0.029** | at most 0.03 | met |

**The sample check is part of the tool.** Before mixing, each pure array must read as its own cell; in the version 2 test it refused all
six of that laboratory's B-cell arrays (they read 0.29-0.68 B cells; DEV-DECONV-V21-01 data, deconv_v2_scored.json) and one monocyte array, rather than report a wrong share.

**The error bars ship as information, labeled not calibrated.** Both sealed tests also carried a second bar, the true share inside the
tool's 95 % bar at least 80 % of the time (0.42, then 0.78). On 2026-10-17 the author's limits study held each tool to its job
(`development/LIMITS_STUDY_2026-10-17.md`): the deconvolution tool's job is the share, so the error-bar bar no longer gates it. Disclosed
here because it was decided after both scores: the tests and their scores stand unchanged (DEV_DECONV_V21_01.md, DEV_DECONV_V21_02.md).

**Development:** B cells and NK cells (read in version 1 only: errors 0.013 and 0.035), tissue cells, cell-free DNA, the error bars.

**Synthetic test:** chain/stage_deconv_v21.py, DEV_DECONV_V21_01/planted_deconv_v2.txt

**Reference sets:** IAM-Atlas v2.1, built from GSE103541, GSE110554, GSE116754, GSE118144, GSE122126, GSE122244, GSE124565, GSE137716, GSE167998, GSE186458, GSE215353, GSE224807, GSE226298, GSE252045, GSE306226, GSE49618, GSE63409, GSE75405, GSE88824, E-MTAB-4957 and ENCODE (DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md).

**Prior reading:** GSE103541, GSE110554, GSE118144, GSE122126, GSE122244, GSE167998 and GSE226298 are build sources of the atlas, read to build it (CANON/data_spent.csv); none is a mixture source. The mixtures were built from arrays of laboratories outside the build (DEV-ATLAS-USE-01 lists; E-MTAB-2145), read once in U1 and never mixed before these tests.

**Sources:** public: the atlas v2.1 compact copy in this repository; held-out GEO and ArrayExpress arrays of sorted healthy blood cells
(DEV-ATLAS-USE-01 lists; E-MTAB-2145).

**What it would take to succeed:** the share bar met on a laboratory the atlas never saw; met (0.016 against 0.03).

**In plain words:** the tool takes a blood sample's methylation reading and says how much of it came from each kind of blood cell. On
mixtures made from a laboratory it had never seen, it was off by 1.6 percentage points at the median, and it refuses samples it cannot read.
