AstroGenetics: the physics behind each reading

One law runs under every reading here: a mark held against thermal noise costs energy, and the loss of that hold shows as entropy, in bits. Each reading is a ratio to its healthy reference, so healthy reads 1. Its gauge runs from 0, the floor (below it a cell can no longer hold its identity), to saturation, the most the scale can register (every identity site fully scrambled). Both ends are derived from physics, not fitted to data.

How to read this gauge

  • The gauge reads the writing on the cell's 2D surface, in bits: less writing to the left, more to the right.
  • Left end, the floor (H_min): the least writing an X cell can run on. Below it the bit rate is too low to maintain the X cell. Derived: one full ATP per site against thermal kicks.
  • Left of healthy: less writing than healthy cells. Senescence reads here.
  • Healthy: the balance, the bit rate this cell type is built for. Measured on purified healthy cells, not chosen.
  • Right of healthy: more and more writing; the surface fills up toward the full surface, the black-hole side. Cancer sits here. It has not lost its identity; it is writing more than the cell can manage.
  • Right end, the ceiling (full surface): every identity site fully scrambled, 1 bit per site, the surface full. Derived.

A misreading to avoid. It was made more than three times while this gauge was being built, so it is printed on every report: the gauge reads writing on the surface, not holding energy; left is less writing, right is more. The floor is NOT the most a cell can hold, and cancer does NOT lose its identity. Below the floor the bit rate is too low to maintain the cell; cancer moves right, toward the full surface.
