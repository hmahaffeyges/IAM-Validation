**In plain words** (Met-A on patients' breast tumours against their own normal tissue): Real patients, not lab cells. Each woman's breast tumour was compared with her own healthy breast tissue, processed on the same plate, reading only places on the DNA that a change in the mix of tissue types cannot move, so a different mix cannot fake a result. The bar was strict: healthy tissue crosses it only about one time in twenty. Far more tumours crossed it than chance could produce, and most tumours read above the patient's own healthy tissue. This shows the measurement sees breast cancer in patients as a group; flagging each individual patient reliably is the next step.
