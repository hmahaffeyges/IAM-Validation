# DEV-ATLAS-LAVAGE-01 — atlas v2 lung templates against lavage differential counts (written 2026-10-10, before any array is downloaded)
Data, method and bars are in `data/DEV_ATLAS_LAVAGE_01/score_lavage_01.py` (committed with this note): GSE133062, 70 EPIC lavage arrays
(Karolinska) with cytospin percentages; the simulation's method unchanged (60 atlas blocks, 11-cell lung panel, 8,000 most informative loci,
non-negative least squares on posterior means); leukocyte fractions compared with the counts. **Bars:** neutrophil mean |error| ≤ 0.02 and every
sample within 0.05. A pass is independent-laboratory evidence for atlas v2's templates in the lung. It does not commission the whole-blood step.
