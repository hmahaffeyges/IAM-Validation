- **The IAM Sequencing Atlas: every CpG, counted** — _development_  
  A second atlas for sequenced samples, beside IAM-Atlas v2.1: each healthy cell type's level, spread, each donor's disorder and two noise maps at every hg19 CpG, from the 153 healthy sequencing inputs.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **Google spot machines join GitHub's** — _development_  
  Eight discounted spot machines beside GitHub's twenty, each launch price-checked, capped to delete itself, and logged with its cost.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **A megakaryocyte column for the sequencing atlas** — _development_  
  The plasma cell type the atlases lack, from two healthy donors, moved from GRCh38 to hg19 by a new chain step tested on a known answer first; a third donor is held back to test it.
