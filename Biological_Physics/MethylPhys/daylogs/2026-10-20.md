## What we learned that we did not know yesterday

- **The chain's whole-blood spread is 2.01 %, not 6.36 %** — _development_  
  An earlier development script skipped the chain's self-tare step; read by the chain itself, healthy whole bloods differ by 2.01 %, so one person needs about a 1.57 % loss to clear the healthy range. Per-person reading in whole blood is open again.  
  _Why it matters:_ The earlier figure had closed a question the chain itself had not answered.  
  [the record](../doors/data/DEV_BLOOD_SENSITIVITY_01/blood_spread_02_chain_summary.txt)
- **Two Stage 0 checks were misreading healthy arrays (GRP-004, PROC-STAGE0-04)** — _diagnosis_  
  The bisulfite check read all five control pairs in the green channel; three are red probes. Read in their own channels, healthy arrays read 0.927 to 0.978 in one laboratory instead of about 0.74. On two whole slides of another laboratory the high and low hybridization controls trade places exactly while the middle one stays put: a label swap, not a failed hybridization. Both are fixed in the chain, and no healthy rehearsal array is now stopped.  
  _Why it matters:_ When every healthy sample fails a check, the measurement is the first suspect.  
  [the record](../doors/PROC_STAGE0_04_PREREG.md)
