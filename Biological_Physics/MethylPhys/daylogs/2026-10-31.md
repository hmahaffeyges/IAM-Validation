**Learned:**
- The median of 1 to 3 donors cannot be a healthy level on sequencing: at thinly read sites it lands on exactly 0 or 1, so the expected disorder is zero and healthy samples read high.
- Pooling several donors overstates one person's disorder (donors at different levels look disordered together); the median of a few understates it. The sample has to be compared with each healthy donor, at the same number of reads.
- Each donor's stored disorder is measured at that donor's own depth and runs low, so it cannot be used directly.
- Sorted-cell Met-A still falls with depth on real libraries (rho −0.55), which the synthetic does not show.
- GEO served success codes and then dropped transfers, for Google machines, GitHub machines and the laptop alike.
