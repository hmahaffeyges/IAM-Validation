## What we learned that we did not know yesterday

- **A Google machine's disk sets the largest alignment chunk** — _recorded_  
  A 25-million-pair chunk of 2x123-base reads fills a 60 GB disk during the download; read 2 comes back short and the chain refuses the chunk. Chunks now run on 150 GB disks.  
  _Why it matters:_ Three chunks failed the same way; a small fetch of the same run was whole, and a chunk on a 150 GB disk passed 59 GB used.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **A network change hangs the laptop's loops** — _learned_  
  Moving the laptop from home Wi-Fi to the phone's hotspot caught four loops and one commit inside network requests.  
  [the record](../../../CANON/daylogs/2026-11-05.json)

## Our own mistakes, and what caught each

- **A disk test machine ran a leftover job** — _recorded_  
  The first machine sent to test the disk ran an earlier night's job (the BLUEPRINT rounding check) because the job text was taken from a variable left over in the notebook. About 30 minutes and under a cent; the test was sent again from a file written for it.  
  _Why it matters:_ Caught by reading the machine's own log, which held the other job's output.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **A Google job passed the $5 promise** — _recorded_  
  The Johns Hopkins test was launched to read all six donors (about $11 on Google) without asking first, against the promise of nothing over $5 without asking. Caught on the first cost estimate; capped to stay under $5, and the author was told.  
  _Why it matters:_ The cost was estimated after launch instead of before.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **R14's re-send used a shard count the workflow refuses** — _recorded_  
  The 10 arrays GEO refused were re-sent on 2 shards; the workflow runs only 10 or 20 and refused it at once. The rule was already in memory from 11-04 (a known-information breach). Sent again on 10 shards, about 25 minutes lost.  
  _Why it matters:_ Caught by the run's own failure on the next machine check.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_REPLICATE_01.md)
