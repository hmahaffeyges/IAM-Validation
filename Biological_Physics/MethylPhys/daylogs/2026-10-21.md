## What we set out to do

Set Stage 0's control-probe limits from healthy EPIC v1 arrays of three laboratories with the corrected measurements, then score every blood cell in whole blood and finish the documentation pass.

## What ran, and how each came out

- **Stage 0's control-probe limits set from 337 healthy arrays (GRP-004, PROC-STAGE0-04)** — _audit_  
  The bisulfite, hybridization and extension controls of 337 healthy whole-blood arrays from three laboratories, read with the corrected Stage 0 on GitHub. The limits are now set from them: bisulfite conversion at least 0.80 (the author's choice, the 2.5th percentile with each laboratory counted equally), hybridization spread at least 2.0, extension balance 0.2 to 5.0. One healthy array is stopped, a real low conversion; the rest pass.  
  _Why it matters:_ A check that refuses a sample has to be set from what healthy samples actually look like, in more than one laboratory.  
  [the record](../doors/PROC_STAGE0_04_OUTCOME.md)
- **The self-tare on real arrays (GRP-014, DEV-SELFTARE-03)** — _diagnosis_  
  On 55 real healthy bloods the self-tare removes a 1 % loss of the neutrophils' pattern that reaches every site, and keeps the same loss at the identity sites. A version that keeps it fails the replicate and other-laboratory bars, so the commissioned self-tare stays. The number it corrects each array by carries that loss and becomes the next reading to build.  
  _Why it matters:_ A real loss of held marks may reach every site; the reading must not erase it.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_SELFTARE_03_OUTCOME.md)
- **The two healthy references compared (ATL-070)** — _diagnosis_  
  IAM-Atlas v2.1 and Met-A's purified-array reference agree closely value by value, but the atlas sits slightly closer to 0.5 at nearly every site, so Met-A read on the atlas neutrophil gives 1.062. Met-A keeps its own reference.  
  _Why it matters:_ Two references made different ways are not interchangeable on an entropy reading.  
  [the record](../doors/data/DEV_REFERENCES_01/atlas_vs_purified_01_summary.txt)
- **Hard-to-map regions against our instruments (OTH-079)** — _diagnosis_  
  Of the array's probes, 3.14 % cannot be placed uniquely in the genome; every instrument's sites fall on them less often (0.86 to 1.90 %). A re-read without them runs on GitHub. Result: Met-A and the cell shares did not move (at most 0.002); the C-Score moved only by its own repeat noise. The mask is not needed.  
  _Why it matters:_ A probe that reads two near-identical places mixes their signals.  
  [the record](../doors/data/DEV_MAPPABILITY_01/masked_reread_01_summary.txt)
- **An every-site loss seen on real bloods (GRP-014)** — _diagnosis_  
  A new preliminary reading, the anchor range, read a 2 % loss planted at every site of 72 real healthy bloods as 1.84 % and a 5 % loss as 4.61 %; healthy bloods read 0.0 %.  
  _Why it matters:_ Met-A's self-tare cancels a loss spread evenly over every site; this reading keeps it.  
  [the record](../doors/data/DEV_SELFTARE_03/anchor_range_real_01_summary.txt)

- **The globe shows a fixed change (ATL-063)** — _structure_
