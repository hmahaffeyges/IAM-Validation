## What ran, and how each came out

Each rule below was committed before it ran.

- **The two donor halves for the half-atlases (readiness L19)** — _structure_  
  818 samples joined into 328 donor units by genotype, metadata donor ids, and, for Loyfer's samples, lab, age and sex together (which can merge two people but never split one). Half A holds 171 units, half B 157. 79 of 97 cells are in both halves; 18 single-unit cells are not measured in one half.  
  _Why it matters:_ Two independent halves give map statistics with no noise bias, and half A minus half B is a pure noise map.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **The array-to-sequencing transfer, made bounded (readiness L21)** — _development_  
  A 3-parameter logistic missed 3 of 42 bins with a smooth wave and was not used. The adopted transfer is a monotone calibration curve that cannot leave [0, 1]. Built on 9 of the 17 shared cells and checked on the other 8, and the reverse, the worst bin was 0.0179 against a 0.02 bar; the synthetic test was met.  
  _Why it matters:_ The straight line v2 used predicts negative readings near 0 and overshoots near 1; the new curve stays inside what sequencing can read.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
