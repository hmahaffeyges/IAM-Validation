## What we learned that we did not know yesterday

- **Each cell's healthy prediction centers the per-cell readings** — _development_  
  Dividing each cell's reading by the array's own line and noise applied to the atlas at that cell's identity sites moved the readings' center from 0.89 to within 0.008 of the truth.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_HEALTHY_01.md)

## Our own mistakes, and what caught each

- **The per-cell prediction refused every cell, silently** — _mistake_  
  The agent reused the whole-sample prediction, which refuses fewer than 10,000 sites, for cells that hold at most 6,000 identity sites; the third run came back identical to the second.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_HEALTHY_01.md)
- **A page address given as a bare path** — _mistake_  
  The agent told the author it had given him the showpiece page's link when it had written only a path, and the page was linked only from the sequencing cell gallery.  
  [the record](../METHYLPHYS_DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md)
