- **The C-Score's synthetic test chose its cells by assumption**  
  The first version planted losses in four blood cells picked in advance. The author caught it: which cells are there is the IAM-Deconvolver's answer. It was canceled before it ran; the test now plants in every cell the Deconvolver finds, and every cell found gets a C-Score (flagged below 1 %).  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_EACH_CELL_01.md)

  The second Met-A seal's synthetic arrays held only the 6,000 identity sites; the chain's own self-tare refused all 36 because a real array has both probe designs. Rebuilt at every atlas site.  
  [the record](../doors/data/METAA_ATLAS_SEAL_02/synthetic_02.py)

  The explorations it needed had finished unread; the author asked where the test was. Both were read and the second sealed test written the same hour.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_ATLAS_01.md)
