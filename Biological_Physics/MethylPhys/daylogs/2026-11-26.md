**Ran, and how each came out:**
- The Locator in the one button (edits 259 and 266): unplanted known-answer library, no places named; planted library, exactly the planted places the tested engine names on the same counts (9 loss blocks and 8 gain islands). Edit 266 fixed the sample's own rate, which had been taken over more units than the reference uses (finding F298).
- Sex and age on sequencing (edit 265): the sample's sex is now read from its own chrX and chrY molecules; on 49 never-used libraries it agreed with the declared sex in every case it could judge. The partial-read options were removed. Edit 271 runs intake and the sex reading on mixtures too.
- Presence on IAM-Atlas v2.1 (edits 267, 270, 271), six in-silico mixtures through the full one button: the Sequencing Atlas read granulocytes 0.59-0.64 (planted 0.60), T cells 0.22-0.25 (0.25), monocytes 0.05-0.11 (0.08), NK 0.05-0.07 (0.07), erythrocyte progenitors 0.078 (0.10), with no false presence at 5 %, also at plasma depth. On sequenced blood v2.1 read monocytes and NK at 0 and put 26-36 % in lung macrophages; on the liver mixture, whose sources are in neither atlas, the two atlases agreed (hepatocytes 0.59 of 0.70) (finding F307).
- v2.1 as a healthy reference for sequenced samples (edits 268, 269, the agreement test, 51 reads on 17 never-used healthy libraries): neither conversion met its bar; the offset depends on the cell type, about 2-3 % for cardiomyocytes and up to 23 % for cortical neurons (finding F301).
- IAM-A is now read on every sample (edit 272); a sample outside its type's healthy shape is read and flagged, never stopped.
- Every place (edits 273, 274, 275): the 156 healthy Sequencing Atlas input libraries were recounted on all 592,067 places (half on Google machines, half on GitHub runs, identical to the earlier counts on shared places), and the Locator's healthy reference and measured null were rebuilt over every place.
- Laboratory correction (edit 276): the per-site clip was the main cause of the residual (finding F302); with level fits taking unclipped levels and the Met-A family reading against expectations put on the sample's own conversion, whole-sample Met-A planted over unplanted went from 1.039 to 0.995, the second cell share from 1.129 to 0.966 (finding F305).
- Variant guard (edit 277): on every place, the unplanted library named one island; 147 of its 160 errors sat at one CpG changed on 97 % of molecules, a genetic variant read as a lost mark (finding F304). The Locator now reads each named place CpG by CpG and marks such a place as a probable genetic variant; tested on the same library, it marks that island and counts 0 changes.

**Successes:**
- The Locator names planted places and no others on both sides, and every place it names carries its genes, its island context and whether any array probe sees it.
- The Sequencing Atlas identifies the cells of sequenced blood and plasma-depth mixtures with no false presence.
- The every-place reference reads 401,229 places on the loss side and 92,381 on the gain side of the known-answer library (165,285 and 46,973 before); on the gain side it named all 10 new planted islands and 9 of 10 earlier ones, nothing else.
- The first every-place read found a genetic variant at a CpG in a healthy donor, and the chain now tells such a place apart from a change of methylation.

**Learned:**
- IAM-Atlas v2.1 is an array atlas: it identifies solid-tissue cells in sequenced samples but confuses blood monocytes with tissue macrophages, and it is not a healthy ruler for sequenced level readings. The Sequencing Atlas is the ruler for every cell it holds; v2.1 stays the atlas for arrays.
- The open sequenced marrow progenitors (GSE87196) are too thin to build columns; the leukemia study's own healthy comparison includes three cord-blood CD19+ B-cell donors with open raw reads, a candidate column; its pre-B2 controls are behind an access application and are not used.
- Testing every place tightens the loss side's false-discovery cut about 20 times; at this library's depth a single 50-CpG block needs roughly 7-9 times its expected lost marks to be named (finding F306).
- Region sets work: with their own measured null, a 30 % rise across five late-copied domains was found 23 of 25 times and a 60 % rise 25 of 25, with no false domains (finding F300).

**Errors (the agent's own), and what caught each:**
- We said the 16 open 'where' cells would close with the Locator; only IAM-A's does. Caught when the design was read again; the rest became their own item.
- Edit 268's v2.1 fallback answered every caller and changed where two development blocks stopped; caught by the agreement test's canary, fixed by edit 270.
- The presence reading first fed sequencing levels to an array atlas unconverted; caught by the presence canary, fixed by edit 271.
- The function check counted 'applied' and 'drawn' as failures; caught when 49 sex readings agreed while the check said the reading was missing.
- The plant record kept per-place counts in sorted order while its unit lists kept file order, which looked like a counting discrepancy; caught when the totals agreed exactly.
- Large comparisons were run on the author's laptop, and the app restarted; the presence watcher stopped with it and five samples waited about an hour. The rule now is that such work runs on Google.
- Overnight one step waited for an approval that expired after six hours, and nothing ran in that time.

**The author's instructions and corrections:**
- Commissioning never decides what runs; labels stay, explained in one commissioning supplement that each label links to.
- A tumor is a cell far to the right of healthy, read against that cell's healthy self; tumor-adjacent tissue is never a healthy control.
- Every wired route is explained in the SOP, the Decoder, the flowchart, the report and the instrument records, not only named.
- Spread tests over GitHub and Google machines.
- Depth over breadth: show reproducible results on the data we can get, and state plainly what open data cannot show and what data would close it.
- A Locator supplement in the report, linked from every 'where' result.
- Every test sample runs the whole chain with one button push, so each run also checks every instrument and the report; a run that tests one thing only is the exception.
- The 200-plus earlier validation tests are run again once the chain works on plasma cell-free DNA, buffy coat and whole-blood arrays, and compared with their first results; until then the work stays on making the chain work.
