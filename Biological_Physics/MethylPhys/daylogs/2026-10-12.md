## The second instrument's commissioning, check by check

Seventy-one libraries of IDENTICAL reference DNA, sequenced by several kits in two laboratories - so any disagreement is the instrument, not the donor. Scored once, under a seal written days earlier.

| check | what it asks | bar | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C1 conversion** | does incomplete bisulfite conversion distort the reading | keep the 0.98 intake limit, apply no correction, measure conversion the same way for every library | **MET** - and the rule itself was corrected first: synthetic test 5 showed the response is about half the derived factor, so dividing by it over-corrects |
| **C2 repeatability** | do two libraries of the same DNA read the same | in every kit, at least 6 of 7 cell lines' replicate pairs within 0.03 | **MET** |
| **C3 across kits** | do different library kits agree | each line within 0.03 of its median across the five kits, in at least 6 of 7 lines | **MET** |
| **C3 across laboratories** | does the one kit made in two laboratories agree between them | within 0.03, in at least 6 of 7 lines | **NOT MET** - 4 of 7. The tolerance was not loosened after seeing the numbers |
| **C4 healthy reads normal** | does a healthy specimen read as healthy | cannot be tested on this specimen type | **UNTESTED**, recorded as untested, to be met on neurons |
| **C5 planted change** | does a known planted loss move the reading | a planted 1 % loss must move the reading by more than 0.03 in every library | **MET** |

## What we learned that we did not know yesterday

- **Lung is the hardest tissue we have, and we can say why**  
  Across four attempts the failure is the same: a healthy lung whose cell mixture leans one way produces the same reading as a tumor, and there is no measured lung stroma in the atlas to separate them.  
  _Why it matters:_ It converts four separate disappointments into one stated cause with a fix (measure lung stroma) rather than four retries.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_TISSUE_LUNG_03.md)
- **A dish changes the pattern, and the change is partly our own instrument**  
  Measurement date tracked passage in most of the lines, so the first drift number mixed dish effect with batch. The batch-free core is about a quarter of it.  
  _Why it matters:_ Any atlas built from cultured cells inherits this, so the honest version is to report both numbers and mask on the larger set.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **A long rented run survives being interrupted if nothing is ever re-read**  
  Four reclaims in one run cost no completed work, because every finished library was already in storage and was skipped on resume.  
  _Why it matters:_ It is the difference between a day of compute lost and an hour.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_IAMA_COMMISSION_01.md)
