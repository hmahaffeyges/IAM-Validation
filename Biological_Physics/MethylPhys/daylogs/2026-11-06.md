**Learned:**
- An amplification library's calls are copies; counted as molecules they distort every reading that depends on counting noise.
- A synthetic only tests the conditions it holds: the copies synthetic used 6 molecules and 4 or 12 copies where the plasmas hold about 1 molecule and up to 270 copies.
- A shuffled null overstates noise when noisy pixels sit at the mask edges; a sign-flip null keeps each pixel's own noise.
- A "sorted" outside sample is not one cell: the Deconvolver finds 24 to 80 % of the labeled cell, and one CD4 sample was mostly monocytes.
- A tared median near 1 is almost automatic; only a per-person bar says whether each healthy person reads Normal.
- Outside laboratories differ in measurable library terms: the false-call rate at the floor sites spans 0.0016 to 0.0158 against the atlas laboratory's 0.0063, and depth 3 to 71 calls a site.
