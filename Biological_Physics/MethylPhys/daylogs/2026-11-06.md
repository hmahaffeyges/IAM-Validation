## Our own mistakes, and what caught each

- **GitHub machines left idle for about an hour** — _mistake_  
  From about 10:50 PM to 11:55 PM (11-05) GitHub's busy jobs fell from 17 to 6 of 20: the B and T dispatcher sent the next sample only when 3 or fewer jobs were busy, and the loop check looked only at whether each loop was logging. The agent's half-hour checks printed the falling count and reported the machines on track.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_ATLAS_CURVE_01.md)
- **A paper's per-mole unit copied into the prior-art record** — _mistake_  
  The record wrote the Landauer cost per mole in J where kJ is meant, copied from the paper instead of read from the canon.  
  [the record](../doors/PRIOR_ART_01.md)
- **The paper folder was not in the commit tool's sent folders** — _mistake_  
  The 11-04 data-we-need section of the paper outline, and the 11-06 acknowledgments, were written but never reached GitHub.  
  [the record](../paper/OUTLINE.md)
- **Prior-art search before reading the book's own credits** — _mistake_  
  The search reported the Landauer-on-methylation work as a finding; the book's giants chapter already credits it and states the difference (known-information rule).  
  [the record](../doors/PRIOR_ART_01.md)
- **The v2.1 chapter said both sequencing noise maps sit inside their null** — _mistake_  
  The atlas story (CANON/atlas_story.json), which writes the v2.1 book section and the website's atlas text, said the donor-half and read-half maps each sit inside their shuffled null; the record shows the read-half map fits its null in all 56 cells and the donor-half map stands above it in all 48 cells with donors to split.  
  [the record](../../../CANON/atlas_story.json)
- **A running merge held a second Google slot** — _mistake_  
  The B and T relay counted a sample as waiting for its merge while its merge machine was already running, so the slot it reserved sat empty (5:45 to about 6:10 AM, one of 32 machines).  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_ATLAS_CURVE_01.md)
- **A missing noise map explained with the wrong reason** — _mistake_  
  The new cell-page caption said a cell without a donor-halves map had a single donor; the eight such cells have their donors in one sealed half (adipocytes: 3 donors, all in half B). The first caption also still wrote 'healthy b cells'.  
  [the record](../../../website/build/seq_atlas_pages.py)
- **A Johns Hopkins chunk stopped after three tries, one of them a refused launch** — _mistake_  
  CD4 chunk GSM2103005 c4: its first launch was refused by the old 24-machine limit yet counted as a try, and two spot machines were reclaimed by Google partway; at three tries the dispatcher gave up, and while it showed CD4 work waiting the tissue dispatcher stood aside, so 1 to 2 Google slots sat empty from about 12:00 to 12:16 PM.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **A merge held its Google slot for one more relay pass** — _mistake_  
  The B and T relay counted a sample as waiting for its merge in the same pass that launched it, so its slot stayed reserved for about 15 minutes after each merge launch.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_ATLAS_CURVE_01.md)
