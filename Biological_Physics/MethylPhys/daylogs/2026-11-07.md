## What we learned that we did not know yesterday

- **A tare looks like it works** — _lesson_  
  A sample divided by its neighbors comes out near 1 by construction: the same-laboratory tare put the medians at 1 while only 38 to 52 % of people read inside Normal.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **Measure the real range before a synthetic** — _lesson_  
  Met-A's healthy-line synthetic missed on a weak array far more compressed than any of 331 real arrays the chain has measured; the formula itself is exact.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_HEALTHY_01.md)
- **A patient's 'normal' tissue is not healthy** — _lesson_  
  GEO's matched normal is a cancer patient's non-tumor tissue.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)

## Our own mistakes, and what caught each

- **A label chose the cell, inside the chain** — _mistake_  
  From 10-22 to 10-28 the agent added code that took the cell from the specimen's words (the sorted-cell Met-A, IAM-A's cell, a 'sorted X' override of the mix, a sample list's cell column), and the cross-laboratory scorers compared samples by label, against the author's 10-22 rule that the tools decide what is in a sample.  
- **GitHub filled to the last slot** — _mistake_  
  The R12 every-cell replay was sent on 20 machines with R10 and R6 queued behind it, so the commit gate waited over 30 minutes, a lesson already given on 11-04.  
- **Tares built on unchecked samples** — _mistake_  
  The same-laboratory tare, the Johns Hopkins CD4 tare and the tumor against own normal were designed against the agreement that the atlases are the ruler, and the array commissionings' same-run tare was never flagged.  
  [the record](../manual/decoder_text.md)
- **Merges dropped without a word** — _mistake_  
  Five tumor merges failed three times each and the dispatcher logged 'waiting 0'; the half-hourly checks read machines busy, not results.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **A UTC time read as Pacific** — _mistake_  
  The author was told a stall began '3 AM yesterday' from a UTC machine-log stamp.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **A range stated wider than the data** — _mistake_  
  The tissue record said every sample read 1.20 to 1.36; one tumor reads 1.0416.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01.md)
- **A flag that never reached arrays** — _mistake_  
  The censored-line flag was set only inside run_sample.py's sequencing branches, so synthetic test 4 on arrays ran the plain line.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_HEALTHY_01.md)
- **Record edits erased by a commit in flight (a repeat)** — _mistake_  
  Test 3's result and test 4's design were written into the record while a commit was in flight; it refreshed the working copy and erased them, so test 4 ran without its design on record. The rule against editing during a commit was already on file from 11-02.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_HEALTHY_01.md)
- **A Google machine idle for six hours** — _mistake_  
  The class-floor synthetic's job read the bucket name from a variable the machine never defines; under set -u it stopped at its first step, before the step that deletes the machine, and sat idle until its time limit. It was launched once, with no launcher watching it, and no check looked at whether a machine's log was moving.  
  [the record](../doors/data/DEV_ATLAS_V21_01/seq_atlas/outside_data/floor_class_synthetic.sh)
- **Five replays sent again that were already read on every cell** — _mistake_  
  R9 glioma blood, R5 liver cancer in blood, R7 prostate, R8 tissues and R9 endothelial were queued for every-cell reads although the replay record already holds each (r9_atlas_full, r5_atlas_full, and R7 and R8's full summaries, which already list every cell); the queue was chosen from the run registry, not the record.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_REPLICATE_01.md)
- **The 450K C-Score platform term came from labeled-healthy bloods** — _audit_  
  The C-Score's 450K limit term (1.00789) was calibrated on the spent healthy 450K bloods of the five-laboratory set, the same bloods the rehearsal read.  
  [the record](../doors/DEV_METAA_HEALTHY_01.md)
