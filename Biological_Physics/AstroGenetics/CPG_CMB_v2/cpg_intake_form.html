Before your sample is read

Your answers are recorded with your sample's anonymized ID. They give your clinician context for the report and form part of the chain-of-custody record. Answer honestly; leave anything blank you're unsure of.

What the engine reads from this form today: only date of birth, sex at birth, sample type, and the anonymized ID. Everything else is recorded for your clinician and the audit trail, and feeds future versions of the engine as those features are added. None of these answers change the cellular score itself — they label and contextualize the report.
