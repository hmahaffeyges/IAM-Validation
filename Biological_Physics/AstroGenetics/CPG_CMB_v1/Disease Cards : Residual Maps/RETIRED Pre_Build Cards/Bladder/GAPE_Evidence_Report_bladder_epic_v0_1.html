Three Phase C outcomes plus one Phase B calibration anchor sealed against pre-locked prereg.md files (with one CHK-3.1A tissue-class floor amendment 002 sealed AFTER β observed under honest CCL-041 second-best-path disclosure).

The card line under no-overclaim language: "Phase C scoring on TCGA-BLCA n=440 produced results consistent with the framework and with published bladder cancer biology, with three structural caveats sealed and propagated to v0.2 promotion path. Stage 1 immune red flag fires diagnostically at d_paired=+1.90 (p=3.1×10⁻⁸) but Xu-538 panel cohort-substrate coverage triggered O4 sealed outcome (DISC-BLADDER-004). DISC-BLADDER lessons (cookbook propagation)

DISC-BLADDER-001 — Gene-promoter atlas family fitness depends on cell-type DISTINCTNESS, not cell-type COUNT

Atlas family per-tissue test record (third entry):

AtlasTissuen cell typesOutcomeMax within-cohort range
HeartRef (VAL-111)cardiac5O3_TISSUE_FLOOR_DOMINATED0.0152 (collapsed)
ProstateRef (VAL-117)prostate6O1 sealed0.0597 (separated)
BladderRef (VAL-119)bladder4O1 sealed0.0694 (separated)

The hypothesis "more cell types = better gene-promoter atlas separation" is falsified by bladder. Future EpiSCORE per-tissue calibrations cannot be predicted from source matrix dimensions alone — each tissue must be smoke-tested independently.

DISC-BLADDER-002 — CHK-3.1A f_extreme floor is tissue-class-dependent

The VAL-106 kidney+prostate-derived 0.50 floor is empirically appropriate for solid parenchyma, NOT for mucosal tissue. Bladder cohort observed pass rate under that floor: 23.9%. Bladder cohort q1/q99 (f_extreme ≥ 0.387, f_middle ≤ 0.184) is the appropriate mucosal-tissue-class bracket. Pass rate under amended floor: 98.0%. Zero samples in cohort had genuine substrate corruption — was a gate-calibration mismatch, not a data integrity issue.

CHK-2.16 cookbook gate added: every card prereg specifies the tissue-class CHK-3.1A floor at prereg-write time, not inherited implicitly.

DISC-BLADDER-003 — Bulk-WGBS atlases on mucosal-cohort substrates produce inflated cross-tile A-scores

Loyfer Bladder POSITIVE +1.91 vs BladderRef Epi NEGATIVE −1.46 on same n=21 paired pairs. CHK-3.2 cross-tile sanity flagged ALL 14 Loyfer non-bladder solid-tissue tiles uniformly POSITIVE +2.34 to +2.92.

Bulk-tissue WGBS reference encodes mixed-cell-type beta profiles; |β_sample − β_bulk_ref| dominated by substrate-distribution mismatch on mucosal cohorts, not by cell-of-origin biology. Gene-promoter sub-cell-type references encode signature beta profiles for specific cell types and avoid this artifact.

Cookbook rule: Multi-atlas readings on mucosal cohorts MUST include a gene-promoter sub-cell-type atlas as the primary cell-of-origin reader. Single-atlas Stage 2 readings on mucosal cohorts using bulk-WGBS references can be substrate-substitution-fooled.

DISC-BLADDER-004 — Stage 1 panels require per-cohort substrate-coverage validation at prereg-write time

Xu-538 panel mean per-sample coverage on TCGA-BLCA: 78.0%; pass rate 51.1% at ≥80% threshold. The panel CpGs are all valid HM450 designs (substrate-applicable) but per-sample coverage drops on this specific cohort. Stage 1 panel transferability is cohort-specific, not platform-specific.

CHK-2.17 cookbook gate added: Stage 1 panels must be validated against the target Phase C cohort's substrate-coverage envelope at prereg-write time. Validation procedure: sample 5-10 random Phase C cohort β files, compute per-sample panel coverage, FLAG if mean < 90% or q5 < 80%.
