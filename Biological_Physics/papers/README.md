## Mahaffey (2026) — Thermodynamic Operating Constraints of Mammalian Somatic Cell Classes

**Full title:** Thermodynamic Operating Constraints of Mammalian Somatic Cell Classes:
A First-Principles Derivation from DNA Methylation Maintenance Information Costs

**Status:** Preprint, April 2026

**Abstract summary:** Derives thermodynamic lower bounds on DNA methylation entropy
for eight mammalian somatic cell architecture classes from the Landauer cost of
irreversible information maintenance at physiological temperature. Validates H_min
for all 8 classes by MCMC (5 chains, R-hat < 1.001, 8×10^5 samples) against 37
published reference cell measurements. Confirms zero-free-parameter prediction
in 27/28 TCGA cancer types (n = 4,304 matched pairs). Extends validation to
Alzheimer's disease and Type 2 diabetes. Fits E(a_bio) activation function to
DunedinPACE aging data.
