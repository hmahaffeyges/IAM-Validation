# The four-domain schematic

Generated by `CANON/four_domains.py` from `CANON/four_domains.json`; do not edit by hand.
**The virial part of IAM's Law.** The book's thermodynamic identity, <K> = Q = T dS = E_Landauer, "is IAM's Law at the boundary of a bound state; the virial theorem is its mechanical special case" (Ch. What the kinetic half is, p1_04, tagged \interp). The one half it fixes holds for every 1/r binding: gravity and the Coulomb binding of every atom, molecule and bond. It therefore applies to the electrons and molecules in qubits, chips and cells, not only to the cosmos (Ch. One half at every scale, p1_03, \derived). The evidence, none of it produced by us: Hartree-Fock tables for 20 atoms and 10 molecules (Clementi & Roetti 1974; Bunge et al. 1993), the Sun and the Chandrasekhar limit, Zwicky's Coma cluster, six independent N-body simulation groups (Bryan & Norman 1998; Bett 2007; Neto 2007; Ludlow 2010; Power 2012; Klypin 2016), and the Smarr relation for black holes (Smarr 1973; Bardeen, Carter & Hawking 1973). What is still open in each domain is not whether the half holds, but which process carries which half (the 'two halves' row).

**Three bit measures (author: 'a bit story, literally the processor of reality down to the bit').** Bits stored is the surface and its capacity (data at rest). Bits per operation is a snapshot or a limit. Bit rate is anything in motion. Every gauge reads writing in bits: less to the left, more to the right.

## The cell's three numbers, told with a computer and a qubit (author: the way to explain them)

Each number below is a row of the schematic (its slot id in brackets); the values are the cell's, from those rows.

| IAM | A computer's memory | Value for the cell |
|---|---|---|
| **M**, the M number [mahaffey] | The energy spent to write one bit, in units of thermal noise. Every bit faces the same noise, so this is the write budget. | 20.94 (a present chip spends 399 to 411) |
| **φ**, the drive fraction [held_share] | The share of that energy that ends up holding the bit; the rest is lost as heat along the way (a transistor stores 1/2 of what it draws per write). | 0.147 |
| **ε**, the held error [reference] | The bit error rate: how many stored bits are wrong at any moment, ε₀ = 1/(1 + e^(φM)). | 4.4 % |
| The floor [floor] | The error rate if all of the energy held the bit, 1/(1 + e^M). | about 1 in a billion (8 × 10⁻¹⁰) |

The cell's methylation is the same contest. The 4.4 % is its error rate at a held site; IAM-A measures that rate against what the cell's own state predicts, the way a qubit is benchmarked against its specification. This puts the cell, the qubit and the chip on one scale: energy per write in units of kT, against the error rate it buys.
